## What's new in 1.166.0

- **Mold & die: opening direction, material and air traps**
  The Mold & die tool now suggests the best opening direction: each direction shows how much of the surface would stick, and undercuts are red on the model. There is also a free direction you turn by a knob in the view, and a casting material (silicone, casting resin, plaster, wax, soap, concrete) with its shrinkage and density.

– Before making: outside size, pour volume with the sprue, weight and air traps, with a red dot where air would stay.
– Several masters get a sprue each.
– On the outside a block or a contour shell that saves filament.
– Stamp & die can copy the relief of a coin or a logo.
- **Hollow: Gridfinity floor, stacking and new latches**
  A box hollowed with its top open can now get a Gridfinity floor, with a socket for each whole cell, magnet holes on request, and dividers like a Gridfinity bin's. Stacking now goes by a foot that fits into the box below, hollow and with grip recesses if you like; a stacking top takes the next box's foot.

The lid has two new kinds of latch next to the clips: sliders as on a tool case, and barrel bolts that sit in a tube on the wall and are printed in place.
- **Print analysis: every layer checked, with a layer view**
  Next to Print check there is now Print analysis. It cuts the selected parts (or every visible one) into the layers your printer lays down and compares each with the one below. You get a list, problems first and then hints: floating parts, areas without support, overhangs, bridges, walls that are too thin, small islands and too little bed adhesion, each with what helps, often with a button for it.

A click on a finding opens the layer view: the part cut open at that layer with the spot painted, a layer slider and the cross-section over the height. It is no slicer preview.
- **Screws and threaded rods, split lengthwise**
  A screw printed standing breaks along its layers under load; printed lying down its thread is many times stronger. In the properties of a screw or threaded rod, Split lengthwise and lay flat cuts it in two through its own axis and lays both halves on their cut faces, in one undo step.

With centring pins (on by default) the halves line up exactly, along the axis too, so the turns meet and the nut does not jam. Glue the halves afterwards.
- **Thread inserts: the hole, checked first**
  The new Thread insert tool makes the hole for a heat-set insert (M2 to M8, 1/4-20 or a size of your own) exactly where you click, on flat faces and on round walls. First it checks that there is enough wall beside the hole and enough depth behind it: a green ring means go, a red one blocks the hole. By @markopo1982.
- **A typed angle turns every selected part**
  With several parts selected, an angle typed into the angle box turned only the last of them: with two boxes and 30°, the first stayed at 0°. Now they all turn, and one Undo turns them all back.
- **Mounting holes for plates and lids**
  The Hole pattern has a second tab, Mounting holes. It puts screw holes in the corners of the smallest rectangle round the picked face, the same distance from its sides, even on a turned or notched plate where "Along the edge" spreads the holes unevenly.

– Distance from the sides: to the hole's edge (a screw head counts) or to the hole's middle.
– Number of holes: four in the corners, the rest in pairs, evenly spaced along the sides, for example for a watertight lid.
– On a round face, such as the lid of a round box, they go evenly round a circle, three or more.
– Plain, counterbore or countersink, through or to a depth.

They are made as ordinary hole shapes grouped with the body, so the group's hole diameter card works on them too.
- **New shape: Sector**
  The shape library has a new basic shape after the Torus, the Sector: a disc that you cut back with the angle (1 to 360°) like a clock hand, from twelve o'clock clockwise.

– Inner diameter: 0 is a solid piece, more gives a ring or a ring segment.
– End rounding: rounds the two ends, up to a half circle. That gives a curved slot, for example for monitor mounts and other parts that turn and get clamped.
– Diameter and height as on the Cylinder.

The frame stays the full circle, so the shape turns about the circle's middle.
- **Polygon with rounded corners**
  A polygon now has a "Corner fillet" slider under "Sides" and a drag dot on the model, like the Rounded box. One radius for every corner, for any number of sides.

The radius is in mm, so a stretched polygon keeps round corners. The largest value is where neighbouring roundings meet. 0 is the plain polygon, exactly as before.
- **Change the holes of a group at once**
  When a group, or a group inside it, holds round holes, its window shows a "Hole diameter" slider for all of them at once and a "One by one" switch that lists each hole with its own diameter.

Every hole keeps its middle and its depth. The groups are rebuilt from the inside out, the way Edit group → Done does it, as one undo step. Boards, tags, QR codes and bundles are left alone, and only round holes count, so square or oval ones are not touched.
- **Gridfinity bins that overhang the grid**
  When a padded baseplate fills a drawer that is not whole units, a bin can now reach out beyond the grid on any side, over the padding to the wall, so nothing falls down beside it. Its feet stay on the grid. A chamfer under the overhang lets it print without supports; a solid overhang or a lip kept on the grid are there to choose.
- **Lower dividers in Gridfinity bins**
  The dividers in a Gridfinity bin or tray no longer have to run their full height: set "Divider height" under "Compartments", measured from the floor, from 3 mm up. The walls stay as they are, and a label shelf on a divider comes down with it.
- **Text on a Gridfinity tray's label shelves**
  A Gridfinity tray's label shelves now take a text too, along a wall or in a compartment, with the same font, letter colour and letter size as a bin's. A shelf with a text sits 0.4 mm lower, so the letters end level with the tray's top. Type the text under "Text" below the shelf in the Properties panel.
- **Baseplates split to fit your printer**
  With your printer chosen in the workspace settings, a baseplate larger than the printer's bed has "Split to fit the printer" in its Properties panel. It cuts the plate on its cell lines into the fewest plates that fit the bed, each with "Swallowtail", and lays out the keys that hold them together. The plates stay where their cells were, so move or turn them onto the bed one by one.

## Kaj je novega v 1.166.0

- **Kalup: smer odpiranja, material in zračni žepi**
  Orodje Mold & die zdaj predlaga najboljšo smer odpiranja: vsaka smer pokaže, koliko površine bi se oprijelo, spodrezi pa so na modelu rdeči. Na voljo je tudi prosta smer, ki jo zasukaš z gumbom v pogledu, in material za ulivanje (silikon, ulivna smola, mavec, vosek, milo, beton) s skrčkom in gostoto.

– Pred izdelavo: zunanja velikost, prostornina vliva z vtokom, teža in zračni žepi, z rdečo piko tam, kjer bi ostal zrak.
– Več mojstrskih modelov dobi vsak svoj vtok.
– Zunaj blok ali konturna lupina, ki prihrani filament.
– Stamp & die lahko prekopira relief kovanca ali logotipa.
- **Votlenje: dno Gridfinity, zlaganje in nova zapirala**
  Škatla, izvotljena z odprtim vrhom, lahko zdaj dobi dno Gridfinity z ležiščem za vsako celo celico, na željo z luknjami za magnete in predelki kot Gridfinity zaboj. Zlaganje zdaj poteka po nogi, ki se prilega v spodnjo škatlo, po želji votli in z vdolbinami za prijem; vrh za zlaganje sprejme nogo naslednje škatle.

Pokrov ima poleg sponk dve novi vrsti zapirala: drsnike, kot so na kovčku za orodje, in zapahe v cevki na steni, ki se natisnejo na mestu.
- **Analiza tiska: vsaka plast preverjena, s pogledom plasti**
  Ob Print check je zdaj Print analysis. Izbrane dele (ali vse vidne) razreže na plasti, ki jih položi tvoj tiskalnik, in vsako primerja s tisto pod njo. Dobiš seznam, najprej težave, nato namigi: lebdeči deli, površine brez podpore, previsi, mostovi, pretanke stene, majhni otoki in premalo oprijema na podlago, vsako z napotkom, kaj pomaga, pogosto z gumbom za to.

Klik na najdbo odpre pogled plasti: del, prerezan na tisti plasti, z označenim mestom, drsnikom plasti in prerezom po višini. To ni predogled razrezovalnika.
- **Vijaki in navojne palice, razdeljeni po dolžini**
  Vijak, natisnjen stoječ, se pod obremenitvijo lomi po plasteh; natisnjen ležeč je njegov navoj večkrat močnejši. V lastnostih vijaka ali navojne palice »Split lengthwise and lay flat« prereže vijak na dva dela skozi njegovo os in obe polovici položi na prerezni ploskvi, v enem koraku razveljavitve.

S centrirnimi zatiči (privzeto vklopljeni) se polovici natančno poravnata, tudi vzdolž osi, zato se zavoji ujamejo in matica se ne zatakne. Polovici po tisku zalepiš.
- **Vložki za navoj: luknja, najprej preverjena**
  Novo orodje Thread insert naredi luknjo za vložek (M2 do M8, 1/4-20 ali tvoja velikost) natanko tam, kamor klikneš, na ravnih ploskvah in okroglih stenah. Najprej preveri, ali je ob luknji dovolj stene in za njo dovolj globine: zelen obroč pomeni naprej, rdeč luknjo blokira. Avtor: @markopo1982.
- **Vtipkan kot zasuka vse izbrane dele**
  Pri več izbranih delih je kot, vtipkan v polje za kot, zasukal samo zadnjega: pri dveh škatlah in 30° je prva ostala na 0°. Zdaj se zasukajo vsi, en Undo pa jih vrne nazaj.
- **Pritrdilne luknje za plošče in pokrove**
  Vzorec lukenj ima drugi zavihek, Mounting holes. Postavi luknje za vijake v vogale najmanjšega pravokotnika okoli izbrane ploskve, enako oddaljene od njenih stranic, tudi pri zasukani ali izrezani plošči, kjer »Along the edge« razporedi luknje neenakomerno.

– Razdalja od stranic: do roba luknje (glava vijaka se šteje) ali do sredine luknje.
– Število lukenj: štiri v vogalih, ostale v parih, enakomerno razporejene ob stranicah, na primer za vodotesen pokrov.
– Na okrogli ploskvi, kot je pokrov okrogle škatle, gredo enakomerno okoli kroga, tri ali več.
– Navadna, z izvrtino ali s posnetjem, skoznje ali do globine.

Naredijo se kot navadne oblike lukenj, združene s telesom, zato nanje deluje tudi kartica premera lukenj skupine.
- **Nova oblika: Sector**
  Knjižnica oblik ima novo osnovno obliko za Torusom, Sector: disk, ki ga z kotom (1 do 360°) odrežeš kot kazalec ure, od dvanajstih v smeri urnega kazalca.

– Notranji premer: 0 je poln kos, več da obroč ali odsek obroča.
– Zaobljenje koncev: zaobli oba konca, do pol kroga. Tako dobiš ukrivljeno režo, na primer za nosilce monitorjev in druge dele, ki se vrtijo in vpenjajo.
– Premer in višina kot pri valju.

Okvir ostane poln krog, zato se oblika vrti okoli sredine kroga.
- **Mnogokotnik z zaobljenimi vogali**
  Mnogokotnik ima zdaj drsnik »Corner fillet« pod »Sides« in vlečno točko na modelu, kot Rounded box. En polmer za vse vogale, pri poljubnem številu stranic.

Polmer je v mm, zato raztegnjen mnogokotnik ohrani okrogle vogale. Največja vrednost je tam, kjer se sosednji zaobljenji srečata. 0 je navaden mnogokotnik, popolnoma kot prej.
- **Spremeni luknje skupine naenkrat**
  Kadar skupina ali skupina v njej vsebuje okrogle luknje, njeno okno kaže drsnik »Hole diameter« za vse naenkrat in stikalo »One by one«, ki našteje vsako luknjo z lastnim premerom.

Vsaka luknja ohrani svojo sredino in globino. Skupine se zgradijo znova od znotraj navzven, tako kot Edit group → Done, v enem koraku razveljavitve. Plošče, oznake, kode QR in snopi ostanejo nedotaknjeni, štejejo samo okrogle luknje, zato kvadratne ali ovalne ostanejo, kot so.
- **Gridfinity zaboji, ki segajo čez mrežo**
  Kadar podložena osnovna plošča zapolni predal, ki ni cele enote, lahko zaboj zdaj sega čez mrežo na kateri koli strani, prek podloge do stene, tako da nič ne pade ob njem. Noge ostanejo na mreži. Posnetje pod previsom omogoča tisk brez podpor; na izbiro sta tudi poln previs ali ustnica, ki ostane na mreži.
- **Nižje pregrade v zabojih Gridfinity**
  Pregrade v zaboju ali pladnju Gridfinity ni treba več speljati po celi višini: nastavi »Divider height« pod »Compartments«, merjeno od dna, od 3 mm naprej. Stene ostanejo, polica za oznako na pregradi pa se spusti z njo.
- **Besedilo na policah za oznake pladnja Gridfinity**
  Police za oznake na pladnju Gridfinity zdaj sprejmejo tudi besedilo, ob steni ali v predelku, z isto pisavo, barvo in velikostjo črk kot pri zaboju. Polica z besedilom je 0,4 mm nižje, tako da se črke končajo v ravnini z vrhom pladnja. Besedilo vtipkaš pod »Text« pod polico v panelu Properties.
- **Osnovne plošče, razdeljene po tvojem tiskalniku**
  Če je v nastavitvah delovnega prostora izbran tvoj tiskalnik, ima osnovna plošča, večja od podlage tiskalnika, v panelu Properties »Split to fit the printer«. Razreže ploščo po črtah celic na najmanj plošč, ki gredo na podlago, vsako s »Swallowtail«, in postavi ključe, ki jih držijo skupaj. Plošče ostanejo tam, kjer so bile njihove celice, zato jih eno za drugo premakni ali zasukaj na podlago.
