## What's new in 1.117.1

- **Surface texture on the faces you pick**
  With "Pick faces" you click the top, the bottom or a side, and only there does the pattern go, knurling on a single side for example. On round tops grooves run in rings, and round angular bodies the pattern runs round the corners without a seam.
- **A flat torus keeps its hole**
  The torus now has its own "Tube thickness". The height only squashes the tube instead of making it thicker, so a flat ring stays open. Drag the handles and the tube grows along.
- **Keys for the snap grid**
  Alt+1 to Alt+0 pick that entry of the snap menu, and Alt+1 turns snapping off. Comma and period make the step one size finer or coarser, in the 3D view and in a sketch alike.
- **A filleted tube stays round when resized**
  A hollowed body you filleted or chamfered afterwards is hollowed again when you resize it, and the fillet goes back on the same edge. A tube no longer turns square.
- **Deleting a point changes only its neighbours**
  Deleting a point of a smooth outline in a sketch smooths only the two points beside it again. Handles you set by hand elsewhere stay as they are.

## Kaj je novega v 1.117.1

- **Površinska tekstura na izbranih ploskvah**
  Z »Izberi ploskve« klikneš zgornjo, spodnjo ali stransko ploskev in vzorec, na primer rezkanje (knurling) samo na eni strani, se položi le tam. Na okroglih vrhovih potekajo žlebovi v obročih, pri zaobljenih oglatih telesih pa vzorec teče okoli vogalov brez šiva.
- **Ploski torus obdrži luknjo**
  Torus ima zdaj svojo »Debelino cevi«. Višina cev samo stisne, ne da bi jo odebelila, zato ploski obroč ostane odprt. Ko povlečeš ročice, cev raste z njimi.
- **Tipke za mrežo pripenjanja**
  Alt+1 do Alt+0 izbere ta vnos v meniju pripenjanja, Alt+1 pripenjanje izklopi. Vejica in pika korak naredita eno stopnjo finejši ali grobši, v 3D pogledu in v skici enako.
- **Zaobljena cev ostane okrogla pri spremembi velikosti**
  Votlo telo, ki si ga pozneje zaobljil ali posnel, se ob spremembi velikosti znova izvotli, zaobljenje pa se vrne na isti rob. Cev ne postane več kvadratna.
- **Brisanje točke spremeni samo sosednji**
  Če v skici izbrišeš točko gladkega obrisa, se zgladita le dve sosednji točki. Ročice, ki si jih drugje nastavil ročno, ostanejo, kot so.
