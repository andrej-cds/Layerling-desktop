## What's new in 1.136.0

- **Object list: move shapes**
  Each row of the object list has a handle with six dots at its left. Drag it up or down, with the mouse or a finger, or focus it and press the arrow keys, and the shape moves to another place in the list. Undo takes it back. With the A–Z sort or a search the handles are hidden.
- **Holes along the edge, and shapes of your own**
  The hole pattern can now set its holes in a ring along the edge of a face: a hole on every corner and the sides shared out evenly, by spacing or by number, with a start you can move on round faces. Round holes can get a counterbore or countersink, with metric screw sizes. And with Own shape it repeats a shape from the scene over the face, raised or cut in: rivets, studs, a diamond plate.
- **The AI waits for your notes**
  An AI agent on the MCP bridge can now wait for your notes: ask it once, and it picks up every request as soon as you tick For the AI on a note, and answers on the model.
- **Protractor**
  Beside the framing square, the protractor now measures angles: click the corner and a point on each side, or two flat faces. It snaps to corners and edges and follows the bodies when they move.
- **Taper in proportion**
  The Taper card now has a Keep proportions switch: Length and Width of the same end then change together, so a square top stays square.
- **Intersection shows first what stays**
  The first click on Intersection shows the common part in green, and the shapes can still be moved; the second click applies it, Esc cancels. The separate "Show common part" button is gone, so the bar is one button narrower.
- **Bodies with chamfers and fillets follow at once**
  A body with chamfered or rounded edges reacted with a noticeable delay when moved, with the mouse as with the arrow keys, because its whole edge history was worked through again at every step. It now follows as quickly as a plain box, and saving afterwards is faster too.
- **Circles and rectangles snap with their middle**
  Drag a circle or a rectangle in the sketch and its middle snaps to the ends, corners and middles of the other lines, to circle centres and to crossings of construction lines, with a blue ring to show where. So a hole sits exactly on the middle of a line. Alt turns the snapping off.
- **STEP no longer writes the wrong shape**
  A body that was rounded or hollowed first and tapered or twisted after went into the STEP file without the twist, and nothing said so. Now the export leaves it out and tells you, so you can save it as STL. The object list now says Exact body or Mesh under such bodies, so you see beforehand what goes into STEP.

## Kaj je novega v 1.136.0

- **Seznam objektov: premikanje oblik**
  Vsaka vrstica v seznamu objektov ima levo ročaj s šestimi pikami. Povleci ga navzgor ali navzdol z miško ali s prstom, ali pa ga fokusiraj in pritiskaj puščice – oblika se premakne na drugo mesto v seznamu. Undo vrne nazaj. Pri razvrščanju A–Z ali iskanju so ročaji skriti.
- **Luknje ob robu in lastne oblike**
  Vzorec lukenj zdaj lahko postavi luknje v obroč ob robu ploskve: luknja v vsakem oglišču, stranice pa enakomerno razdeljene po razmiku ali številu, pri okroglih ploskvah z začetkom, ki ga lahko premakneš. Okrogle luknje lahko dobijo pogrez ali čelno poglobitev z metričnimi velikostmi vijakov. Z možnostjo Own shape pa vzorec ponavlja obliko iz scene čez ploskev, izbočeno ali vrezano: kovice, čepi, rebrasta pločevina.
- **Umetna inteligenca počaka na tvoje opombe**
  Agent umetne inteligence na mostu MCP zdaj lahko čaka na tvoje opombe: enkrat ga prosiš, nato sam prevzame vsako zahtevo, ko pri opombi obkljukaš For the AI, in odgovori na modelu.
- **Kotomer**
  Ob kotniku zdaj kote meri tudi kotomer: klikni oglišče in točko na vsaki strani ali dve ravni ploskvi. Prime se na oglišča in robove ter sledi telesom, ko se premikajo.
- **Zožitev v razmerju**
  Kartica Taper ima zdaj stikalo Keep proportions: Length in Width istega konca se spreminjata skupaj, zato kvadratni vrh ostane kvadraten.
- **Presek najprej pokaže, kaj ostane**
  Prvi klik na Intersection pokaže skupni del zeleno, oblike pa lahko še premikaš; drugi klik ga uveljavi, Esc prekliče. Ločenega gumba »Show common part« ni več, zato je vrstica za en gumb ožja.
- **Telesa s posnetimi in zaobljenimi robovi sledijo takoj**
  Telo s posnetimi ali zaobljenimi robovi se je pri premikanju (z miško in s puščicami) opazno zakasnilo, ker se je ob vsakem koraku znova predelala vsa zgodovina robov. Zdaj sledi tako hitro kot navadna škatla, tudi shranjevanje je nato hitrejše.
- **Krogi in pravokotniki se prilepijo s sredino**
  Če v skici povlečeš krog ali pravokotnik, se njegova sredina prilepi na konce, oglišča in sredine drugih črt, na središča krogov in na presečišča pomožnih črt, z modrim obročem, ki kaže mesto. Tako luknja sedi natanko na sredini črte. Alt izklopi lepljenje.
- **STEP ne zapiše več napačne oblike**
  Telo, ki je bilo najprej zaobljeno ali izdolbeno, nato pa zoženo ali zasukano, je šlo v datoteko STEP brez zasuka, ne da bi kdo to povedal. Zdaj izvoz takega telesa izpusti in te obvesti, da ga shraniš kot STL. Seznam objektov pod takimi telesi piše Exact body ali Mesh, da vnaprej vidiš, kaj gre v STEP.
