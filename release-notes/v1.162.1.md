## What's new in 1.162.1

- **Desktop app: no calls to outside addresses**
  The desktop app now reads the author's notices only from its built-in list and no longer asks the internet for a newer one.
- **Scale to a model scale**
  Scale by percent can now do a scale too: choose Model scale at the top and a gauge from Z (1:220) to G (1:22.5), or type a scale of your own.

If the model is built at full size, it becomes that much smaller. If you built it at any other size, type the original's real height or length in metres: a tree 16 m tall becomes exactly 184 mm in H0. The window shows the new size before you scale.

Find it by right-clicking the parts or with Ctrl+K, Scale by percent.
- **Align without the Alt key on a tablet**
  Align now has a switch, Size mode, beside the dots wherever a finger or pen replaces the mouse. It does what holding Alt does: the dots match sizes instead of moving the parts, and at the gap dot you type the gap. Switch it on, tap, switch it off again.
- **Tube and bent tube with matching fields**
  Both tubes now name their measures alike: Wall thickness instead of Thickness on the tube, Outer diameter on a round bent tube (Width across flats on an angular profile).

If the bent tube has the same profile inside, you can also type the inner diameter, and the wall follows. Quality sits at the top, where the plain tube has its sides.

Tip: a bent tube with a bend angle of 0° is a straight square, hexagonal or octagonal tube.
- **Wedge: the slope angle in its properties**
  The wedge's properties now show the slope angle beside length, width and height. Type an angle and the height changes to match at the same width. So you can set a workplane on its slope to exactly the angle you need, without a protractor.
- **Hide the build plate, keep the workplane on the face**
  With a workplane set on a face, the button with the eye over a grid in the view bar now hides only the build plate. The workplane on the face stays visible and still has its own eye.
- **The grid's centre cross in the axis colours**
  The grid's zero lines now take the colours of the axis arrows: the line along X red, the one along Y green, on the build plate and on a workplane on a face. So even on small parts you see at once which way each axis runs.
- **Smooth faces on swept sketches**
  A sketch swept along a curved path looked grainy, above all in the slicer. Its faces are now meshed in an even grid and stay smooth. A body built before gets this once it is built again, for example after a change to its sketch.
- **The message after each step can be switched off**
  After a step such as a union, a short message beside the view cube says what was done and offers Undo. If you don't need it, switch it off in the settings under "Appearance: the view" with "Show a message after each step". Messages that want something from you or report a problem still appear, and Undo in the ribbon names the step when you point at it.
- **Stretch: a group stays a group**
  Stretching a group now gives a group again, one you can ungroup. The parts the plane crosses get longer, the parts beyond it move along, and those before it stay as they were. Holes stay holes. An object selected with the others that lies wholly beyond the plane moves along too.
- **3D mouse: the view no longer stutters when turned freely**
  Twisting and tilting the cap of a SpaceMouse at the same time sometimes made the view jump a little. The cap's movements are now smoothed briefly, so the view follows calmly. Let go, and it stops at once.
- **An icon tag keeps its card after Edit Bundle**
  Opening an icon tag with Edit Bundle, or clicking one of its layers in the Objects list, left only the bundle's properties after Done: the card with the layers, border widths, heights and key ring hole was gone. Now it stays. The same goes for name tags, QR codes and SBC boards.
- **Backups without the save dialog: "Download instead"**
  On some PCs the save dialog of Chrome and Edge closes after Save but hands back no file, and Back up all then ended without a backup. The message now has a "Download instead" button: the backup then goes into the Downloads folder as a normal download. The same goes for backing up custom shapes.
- **Shapes opens when you point at its arrow too**
  The Shapes button carries the same arrow as the folded groups of the ribbon, but it opened only with a click. Now the shape library also opens when the mouse rests on the arrow for a moment, and closes again when the mouse leaves it. If you click the button, work in the library or pin it, it stays open.
- **Chamfer on a group: the mesh stays closed**
  After a chamfer on a group the mesh could have holes: Split stopped with "mesh not closed", the print check with an error, and the slicer left parts out. layerling now closes such gaps right when it chamfers or fillets.

A design already saved with such a mesh can be split again. To export it closed, do the chamfer once more.
- **Sketch line goes on when switching tools**
  Switching between Line, Bezier curve and Smooth line while drawing a sketch now goes on from the last point. Until now the next click started a new line.

## Kaj je novega v 1.162.1

- **Namizni program: brez klicev na zunanje naslove**
  Namizni program zdaj bere obvestila avtorja samo iz vgrajenega seznama in ne sprašuje več interneta za novejši seznam.
- **Spremeni merilo v merilo makete**
  »Scale by percent« zdaj zna tudi merilo: izberi zgoraj »Model scale« in merilo od Z (1:220) do G (1:22,5) ali vtipkaj svoje.

Če je model zgrajen v naravni velikosti, postane toliko manjši. Če si ga zgradil v kateri koli drugi velikosti, vtipkaj pravo višino ali dolžino originala v metrih: 16 m visoko drevo v H0 postane natanko 184 mm. Okno pokaže novo velikost, preden spremeniš velikost.

Najdeš ga z desnim klikom na dele ali s Ctrl+K, Scale by percent.
- **Poravnava brez tipke Alt na tablici**
  Poravnava ima zdaj stikalo »Size mode« ob pikah povsod, kjer prst ali pero nadomešča miško. Naredi to, kar drža Alt: pike uskladijo velikosti namesto da premikajo dele, pri piki za režo pa vtipkaš režo. Vklopi ga, tapni, spet izklopi.
- **Cev in ukrivljena cev z ujemajočimi polji**
  Obe cevi zdaj imenujeta mere enako: »Wall thickness« namesto Thickness pri cevi, »Outer diameter« pri okrogli ukrivljeni cevi (»Width across flats« pri oglatem profilu).

Če ima ukrivljena cev enak profil znotraj, lahko vtipkaš tudi notranji premer in stena sledi. Kakovost je zgoraj, kjer ima navadna cev svoje stranice.

Namig: ukrivljena cev s kotom 0° je ravna kvadratna, šestkotna ali osemkotna cev.
- **Klin: kot naklona v lastnostih**
  Lastnosti klina zdaj kažejo kot naklona ob dolžini, širini in višini. Vtipkaj kot in višina se ujema pri isti širini. Tako lahko delovno ravnino na njegovem naklonu nastaviš na natanko tisti kot, brez kotomera.
- **Skrij gradbeno ploščo, delovna ravnina ostane na ploskvi**
  Z delovno ravnino na ploskvi gumb z očesom nad mrežo v vrstici pogleda zdaj skrije samo gradbeno ploščo. Delovna ravnina na ploskvi ostane vidna in ima še vedno svoje oko.
- **Središčni križ mreže v barvah osi**
  Ničelni črti mreže zdaj prevzameta barve puščic osi: črta vzdolž X rdeča, vzdolž Y zelena, na gradbeni plošči in na delovni ravnini na ploskvi. Tako tudi pri majhnih delih takoj vidiš, kam teče vsaka os.
- **Gladke ploskve pri skicah, pometenih po poti**
  Skica, pometena po ukrivljeni poti, je bila zrnata, predvsem v razrezovalniku. Njene ploskve so zdaj razdeljene v enakomerno mrežo in ostanejo gladke. Prej zgrajeno telo to dobi, ko je zgrajeno znova, na primer po spremembi skice.
- **Sporočilo po vsakem koraku lahko izklopiš**
  Po koraku, kot je združitev, kratko sporočilo ob kocki pogleda pove, kaj je bilo narejeno, in ponudi Undo. Če ga ne potrebuješ, ga izklopi v nastavitvah pod »Appearance: the view« z »Show a message after each step«. Sporočila, ki nekaj zahtevajo od tebe ali javljajo težavo, se še vedno prikažejo, Undo v traku pa ob kazanju imenuje korak.
- **Stretch: skupina ostane skupina**
  Raztegovanje skupine zdaj spet da skupino, ki jo lahko razdružiš. Deli, ki jih ravnina seka, se podaljšajo, deli za njo se premaknejo, tisti pred njo ostanejo, kot so bili. Luknje ostanejo luknje. Tudi predmet, izbran skupaj z drugimi, ki leži povsem za ravnino, se premakne.
- **3D miška: pogled se ne trza več pri prosti vrtilni gibih**
  Hkratno vrtenje in nagibanje pokrovčka SpaceMouse je včasih povzročilo majhen skok pogleda. Gibi pokrovčka se zdaj za kratek čas zgladijo, zato pogled sledi mirno. Spustiš in se ustavi takoj.
- **Ikonska oznaka obdrži kartico po »Edit Bundle«**
  Odpiranje ikonske oznake z »Edit Bundle« ali klik na eno njenih plasti v seznamu objektov je po »Done« pustil samo lastnosti snopa: kartice s plastmi, širinami robov, višinami in luknjo za obesek ni bilo več. Zdaj ostane. Isto velja za imenske oznake, kode QR in plošče SBC.
- **Varnostne kopije brez pogovornega okna: »Download instead«**
  Na nekaterih računalnikih se pogovorno okno za shranjevanje v Chromu in Edgeu po Save zapre, a ne vrne datoteke, »Back up all« pa se je končal brez kopije. Sporočilo ima zdaj gumb »Download instead«: kopija gre v mapo Prenosi kot navaden prenos. Isto velja za varnostno kopiranje lastnih oblik.
- **Shapes se odpre tudi, ko pokažeš na njegovo puščico**
  Gumb Shapes ima enako puščico kot zložene skupine traka, a se je odprl samo s klikom. Zdaj se knjižnica oblik odpre tudi, ko miška za trenutek počiva na puščici, in se spet zapre, ko miška odide. Če klikneš gumb, delaš v knjižnici ali jo pripneš, ostane odprta.
- **Posnetje na skupini: mreža ostane zaprta**
  Po posnetju na skupini je imela mreža lahko luknje: Split se je ustavil s »mesh not closed«, preverjanje tiska z napako, razrezovalnik pa je izpustil dele. layerling zdaj take vrzeli zapre že ob posnetju ali zaobljenju.

Že shranjen načrt s takšno mrežo se lahko znova razdeli. Da ga izvoziš zaprtega, naredi posnetje še enkrat.
- **Črta skice se nadaljuje ob menjavi orodij**
  Preklapljanje med Line, Bezier curve in Smooth line med risanjem skice se zdaj nadaljuje od zadnje točke. Do zdaj je naslednji klik začel novo črto.
