## What's new in 1.88.0

- **A group with a fillet or chamfer can be ungrouped again**
  Ungroup stayed grey as soon as a group had got a fillet or chamfer. Now the button takes the edge features back first and then dissolves the group, in one step that Ctrl+Z undoes. And the Fillet or Chamfer tool stays open when you take a feature back.
- **The section view fills the cut face**
  In the section view the cut was left open, and a solid and a hole looked the same inside. Where the plane goes through a body, the cut is now filled with its colour, a little darker, and a hole inside it stays empty. The filled face appears a moment after the plane stops moving.
- **The edge window shows which quick button made the selection**
  In the Fillet and Chamfer window the quick buttons were too wide and gave the window a small sideways scroll bar; they wrap now. The buttons react when the pointer is over them, and the quick button whose edges are picked now (All sharp, Top, Bottom or Vertical edges) stays highlighted.
- **Japanese letters in texts and name tags**
  A Japanese name on a text or a name tag used to come out as question marks, because the built-in typefaces have no kana and no kanji. There is now a Japanese typeface that lends its characters to every other typeface and to fonts of your own. It is fetched only when a text has Japanese letters, and is there offline afterwards.
- **A long press on an iPad no longer starts a text selection**
  On an iPad a long press could start the system's text selection, for example on a word in the menu or in the toolbar. After that the touches went to the selection and no longer to layerling, and it looked as if an object stayed in edit mode. The editor can no longer be selected, but input fields can.
- **The shape library opens where you left it**
  A shape library that had been pulled narrow and placed at the right edge opened far to the left the next time, as if an invisible edge held it back. It now opens at the remembered spot, also at a size of its own.
- **Erasing in the sketch: the button erases the selection, and the tool takes a window**
  With several points and lines selected, the Erase button did not erase them, it only dropped the selection. Now it erases the selection. And with the Erase tool you can drag a window over several points and lines: everything inside is erased.
- **The ruler has a scale in inches**
  With the unit set to inches (the switch at the bottom right), the ruler carries a scale in inches: a line every sixteenth of an inch, with the whole inches numbered. In millimetres everything stays as it was.
- **A chain of lines in the sketch is easier to end**
  When drawing with Line, Bézier curve or Smooth curve, a right click without moving now ends the chain, and so does a double click on the last point; the tool stays selected. A right click that you use to pan the view does not end it. To close the shape you still click the first point.
- **The mouse wheel changes a number in a clicked field**
  When you click into a number field of the properties, the mouse wheel changes the value: one unit a notch, ten times as much with Shift, a tenth with Ctrl and a hundredth with Alt. A field that you have not clicked lets the panels scroll as before.
- **Label shelves for Gridfinity bins**
  A Gridfinity bin of 3 height units or more can now have a shelf for a label along any of its inner walls, several at once. It sits just under the stacking lip, and you set its width, length and the rounded or chamfered corners in the Properties panel.
- **A sketch can be mirrored**
  Select points and lines in the sketch and click Mirror in the bar: across the vertical axis (left-right), the horizontal one (top-bottom) or a straight line of the sketch that you click. The copy lands on the other side and nothing is linked. Points on the axis are shared, so a half shape becomes one closed outline.
- **Interactive Help hits better inside the tool windows**
  Cancel, Apply, fields and sliders in a tool window now ask about the tool itself, for example "Chamfer edges", instead of a word that the guide writes a hundred times. The shape library, the object list and the Export window answer with their section, as do the view cube and the camera bar between their buttons.

## Kaj je novega v 1.88.0

- **Skupino z zaobljenjem ali posnetkom je spet mogoče razdružiti**
  Gumb »Razdruži« je ostal siv, takoj ko je skupina dobila zaobljenje ali posnetek. Zdaj gumb najprej odstrani lastnosti robov in nato razdruži skupino, v enem koraku, ki ga Ctrl+Z razveljavi. Orodje za zaobljenje in posnetek ostane odprto, ko lastnost odstraniš.
- **Prerez zapolni prerezano ploskev**
  V prerezu je bil rez odprt, trdno telo in luknja pa sta znotraj izgledala enako. Kjer ravnina seka telo, je rez zdaj zapolnjen z njegovo barvo, malo temnejšo, luknja v njem pa ostane prazna. Zapolnjena ploskev se pokaže trenutek po tem, ko se ravnina neha premikati.
- **Okno za robove pokaže, kateri hitri gumb je naredil izbor**
  V oknu za zaobljenje in posnetek so bili hitri gumbi preširoki in so okno naredili z majhnim stranskim drsnikom; zdaj se prelomijo v novo vrstico. Gumbi se odzovejo, ko je kazalec nad njimi, hitri gumb, katerega robovi so zdaj izbrani (Vsi ostri robovi, Zgornji, Spodnji ali Navpični robovi), pa ostane poudarjen.
- **Japonske črke v besedilih in imenskih tablicah**
  Japonsko ime na besedilu ali imenski tablici je prej izpadlo kot vprašaji, ker vgrajene pisave nimajo kane in kanjija. Zdaj je na voljo japonska pisava, ki svoje znake posodi vsem drugim pisavam in tudi tvojim lastnim. Prenese se samo, ko ima besedilo japonske črke, potem pa je na voljo tudi brez povezave.
- **Dolg pritisk na iPadu ne začne več označevanja besedila**
  Na iPadu je dolg pritisk lahko začel sistemsko označevanje besedila, na primer pri besedi v meniju ali v orodni vrstici. Potem so dotiki šli v označevanje in ne več v layerling, videti pa je bilo, da predmet ostane v urejanju. Urejevalnika ni več mogoče označiti, polja za vnos pa še vedno.
- **Knjižnica oblik se odpre tam, kjer si jo pustil**
  Knjižnica oblik, ki si jo povlekel ozko in postavil ob desni rob, se je naslednjič odprla daleč na levi, kot da jo drži nevidni rob. Zdaj se odpre na zapomnjenem mestu, tudi v lastni velikosti.
- **Brisanje v skici: gumb zbriše izbor, orodje pa vzame okno**
  Pri več izbranih točkah in črtah gumb »Pobriši« ni brisal, samo spustil je izbor. Zdaj zbriše izbor. Z orodjem »Pobriši« pa lahko povlečeš okno čez več točk in črt: vse znotraj se zbriše.
- **Ravnilo ima merilo v palcih**
  Ko je enota nastavljena na palce (stikalo spodaj desno), ima ravnilo merilo v palcih: črto na vsako šestnajstino palca, cele palce pa označene s številkami. V milimetrih ostane vse, kot je bilo.
- **Verigo črt v skici je lažje končati**
  Pri risanju z orodjem »Daljica«, »Bézierova krivulja« ali »Gladka krivulja« desni klik brez premikanja zdaj konča verigo, prav tako dvoklik na zadnjo točko; orodje ostane izbrano. Desni klik, ki ga uporabiš za premikanje pogleda, je ne konča. Za zaprtje oblike še vedno klikneš prvo točko.
- **Miškino kolesce spremeni število v kliknjenem polju**
  Ko klikneš v številčno polje lastnosti, miškino kolesce spremeni vrednost: ena enota na klik, desetkrat toliko s Shiftom, desetinko s Ctrl in stotinko z Alt. Polje, v katero nisi kliknil, pusti, da se plošče drsijo kot prej.
- **Police za nalepke pri posodah Gridfinity**
  Posoda Gridfinity z višino 3 enote ali več lahko zdaj dobi polico za nalepko ob poljubni notranji steni, tudi več hkrati. Stoji tik pod obrobo za zlaganje, njeno širino, dolžino in zaobljene ali posnete vogale pa nastaviš v lastnostih.
- **Skico je mogoče zrcaliti**
  V skici izberi točke in črte ter v vrstici klikni »Zrcali«: čez navpično os (levo-desno), vodoravno (zgoraj-spodaj) ali čez ravno črto skice, ki jo klikneš. Kopija pristane na drugi strani in ni nič povezano. Točke na osi so skupne, zato je pol oblike eden sklenjen obris.
- **Interaktivna pomoč bolje zadene znotraj orodnih oken**
  »Prekliči«, »Uveljavi«, polja in drsniki v orodnem oknu zdaj sprašujejo po orodju samem, na primer »Posnemi robove«, namesto po besedi, ki jo vodnik napiše stokrat. Knjižnica oblik, seznam objektov in okno za izvoz odgovorijo s svojim razdelkom, prav tako kocka pogleda in vrstica kamere med svojimi gumbi.
