## What's new in 1.89.0

- **An exported OBJ stands upright in the slicer**
  STL and 3MF were already written with Z up, but the OBJ with Y up, and slicers such as Cura showed such a part lying on its side. Now the OBJ is written with Z up, too. layerling's own import still stands such files upright.
- **layerling now speaks Japanese**
  The whole interface is there in Japanese, written for upper primary school children and their teachers, with Tinkercad's Japanese terms where it has them. The guide chapters are still in English. You choose the language in the language menu.
- **Folders for your designs**
  On the start page, “New folder” makes folders, folders in folders too. Drag designs into them or use “Move to …” in a design's menu. Deleting a folder deletes no design, and “Back up all” keeps the folders.
- **History view: open a group and bring back its parts**
  In the History view you can now open a group (the Open group button or the E key): its parts then lie there one by one, and you bring back one or more of them without taking the whole group. Groups inside groups work the same way, and Back out closes one level.
- **iPad: a lost finger no longer locks the editor**
  On an iPad it could happen that after tapping two objects one after the other nothing answered until the page was reloaded: the editor believed a finger was still down whose end it had never heard of, and took every further touch for a second finger. Now every new first finger starts the count afresh, and leaving the app resets it, too.
- **Chamfillet: the printable foot**
  In the chamfer window you now choose at the top between Chamfer and Chamfillet. The second kind puts a chamfer at the plate on the bottom edges that blends tangentially into a fillet toward the wall: the first layers need no support, and the edge still ends soft. Bottom edges picks the whole foot in one click; the AI connection can do it too (kind chamfillet).
- **The two-finger zoom on the trackpad now works in the sketch too**
  On a Mac with Safari, spreading and pinching on the trackpad in the sketch zoomed the whole page instead of the drawing plate. Now it zooms the sketch, towards the fingers, at the speed of the settings, as in the 3D window. Restart the app once afterwards.
- **Chamfer and Fillet on a group start faster the second time**
  A group that already holds filleted or chamfered parts had to be turned from its mesh back into an exact body every time the Chamfer or Fillet tool started, about ten seconds for a pocket in a filleted body. Now layerling keeps the result as long as the mesh stays the same: cancelling and starting again, or Undo and Redo, takes a third of the time. The first time takes as long as before.
- **Changing the height of a turned body no longer makes it wander**
  A turned body, a pocket on a sloped workplane for instance, slid along the plane when its height was changed in the Properties panel, because its centre only moved straight up. Now the middle of its base stays where it was and the body grows or shrinks only in its own direction. The AI connection's command does the same.
- **On a phone your designs are right at the top**
  On a narrow screen the saved designs of the start page lay more than a screen down, behind three big tiles and the backup note. Once there are designs, the three tiles are now one short row and the backup note stands behind the designs. On a computer, and with no designs yet, everything stays as it was.
- **A pinned shape library is there again at the next start**
  Anyone who had pinned the shape library found only the pin again at the next start: it was still set, but the library itself was closed. Now a pinned library opens by itself when the editor starts, where you left it.
- **Fillet and Chamfer: several quick buttons, Horizontal edges, and the window stays open**
  The Fillet and Chamfer window has a new Horizontal edges button. With Shift or Ctrl and a click you add the edges of a quick button to the selection, Top and Bottom edges together for example. And after Apply the window stays open on the new body, with the same size and no edge picked, so you can set the next fillet with another size at once. Done, Esc or the cross closes it.
- **The revolve sketch shows the other bodies from the front and puts the body where you drew the profile**
  In the revolve sketch the other bodies used to show from above, as flat outlines, while the profile is a side view, so the two could not be lined up. Now you see them from the front, in the same view as the profile, with the axis in the middle. And the body is made where you drew the profile: the horizontal line through the middle is the ground, and a profile above a plate gives a body that stands on it. This holds on the base plate.

## Kaj je novega v 1.89.0

- **Izvožen OBJ v razrezovalniku stoji pokonci**
  STL in 3MF sta bila že zapisana z osjo Z navzgor, OBJ pa z osjo Y navzgor, zato so razrezovalniki, kot je Cura, tak del prikazali ležečega. Zdaj tudi OBJ zapiše Z navzgor. Layerlingov lasten uvoz take datoteke še vedno postavi pokonci.
- **Layerling zdaj govori japonsko**
  Ves vmesnik je na voljo v japonščini, napisan za otroke višjih razredov osnovne šole in njihove učitelje, z Tinkercadovimi japonskimi izrazi, kjer jih ta ima. Poglavja navodil so še v angleščini. Jezik izbereš v meniju jezikov.
- **Mape za tvoje osnutke**
  Na začetni strani »Nova mapa« ustvari mape, tudi mape v mapah. Osnutke povleci vanje ali uporabi »Premakni v …« v meniju osnutka. Brisanje mape ne izbriše nobenega osnutka, »Varnostno kopiraj vse« pa mape vzame zraven.
- **Pogled zgodovine: odpri skupino in vrni njene dele**
  V pogledu zgodovine zdaj lahko odpreš skupino (gumb »Odpri skupino« ali tipka E): njeni deli ležijo posamično in enega ali več jih vrneš, ne da bi vzel celotno skupino. Skupine v skupinah delujejo enako, »Nazaj« zapre eno raven.
- **iPad: izgubljen prst urejevalnika ne zaklene več**
  Na iPadu se je lahko zgodilo, da po dotiku dveh predmetov zapored nič več ni odzivalo, dokler nisi ponovno naložil strani: urejevalnik je mislil, da je en prst še na zaslonu, njegovega konca pa nikoli ni zaznal, in vsak naslednji dotik je štel za drugi prst. Zdaj se z vsakim novim prvim prstom štetje začne znova, ponastavi pa se tudi ob zapustitvi aplikacije.
- **Posnetek + zaobljenje: tiskljiva noga**
  V oknu za posnetje zdaj zgoraj izbereš med »Posnetek« in »Posnetek + zaobljenje«. Druga vrsta na spodnjih robovih naredi posnetek ob plošči, ki se tangentno prelije v zaobljenje proti steni: prve plasti ne potrebujejo podpor, rob pa se kljub temu konča mehko. »Spodnji robovi« izberejo celotno nogo z enim klikom; deluje tudi prek povezave z umetno inteligenco (kind »chamfillet«).
- **Povečava z dvema prstoma na sledilni ploščici zdaj deluje tudi v skici**
  Na Macu s Safarijem je razmik in približevanje prstov na sledilni ploščici v skici povečal celotno stran namesto risalne plošče. Zdaj poveča skico, proti prstom, s hitrostjo iz nastavitev, kot v 3D oknu. Nato aplikacijo enkrat znova zaženi.
- **Posnetek in zaobljenje na skupini se drugič zaženeta hitreje**
  Skupino, ki že vsebuje zaobljene ali posnete dele, je bilo treba ob vsakem zagonu orodja za posnetek ali zaobljenje iz mreže spet preračunati v natančno telo, pri žepu v zaobljenem telesu okoli deset sekund. Zdaj si Layerling rezultat zapomni, dokler se mreža ne spremeni: prekinitev in ponovni zagon ali razveljavitev in uveljavitev trajata tretjino časa. Prvič traja enako dolgo kot prej.
- **Višina zavrtenega telesa ga ne premika več**
  Zavrteno telo, na primer vdolbina na nagnjeni delovni ravnini, je ob spremembi višine v oknu lastnosti zdrsnilo vzdolž ravnine, ker se je njegova sredina premaknila le navpično navzgor. Zdaj sredina njegove osnovne ploskve ostane, kjer je bila, telo pa raste ali se krči samo v svoji smeri. Enako velja za ukaz povezave z umetno inteligenco.
- **Na telefonu so tvoji osnutki takoj zgoraj**
  Na ozkem zaslonu so bili shranjeni osnutki na začetni strani več kot zaslon nižje, za tremi velikimi ploščicami in opozorilom o varnostnem kopiranju. Ko osnutki obstajajo, so tri ploščice zdaj ena kratka vrstica, opozorilo pa je za osnutki. Na računalniku in brez osnutkov ostane vse po starem.
- **Pripeta knjižnica oblik je ob naslednjem zagonu spet tam**
  Kdor je knjižnico oblik pripel, je ob naslednjem zagonu našel le buciko: še vedno je bila nastavljena, knjižnica sama pa zaprta. Zdaj se pripeta knjižnica ob zagonu urejevalnika odpre sama, tam, kjer si jo pustil.
- **Posnetek in zaobljenje: več hitrih gumbov, vodoravni robovi in okno ostane odprto**
  Okno za zaobljenje in posnetek ima nov gumb »Vodoravni robovi«. Z Shift ali Ctrl in klikom robove hitrega gumba dodaš izbiri, na primer zgornje in spodnje robove skupaj. Po »Uporabi« okno ostane odprto na novem telesu, z enako velikostjo in brez izbranih robov, da lahko takoj narediš naslednje zaobljenje z drugo mero. »Končano«, Esc ali križec ga zapre.
- **Skica vrtenja prikazuje druga telesa od spredaj in postavi telo tja, kamor si narisal profil**
  V skici vrtenja so bila druga telesa doslej prikazana od zgoraj, kot ploščati obrisi, profil pa je stranski pogled, zato obojega ni bilo mogoče poravnati. Zdaj jih vidiš od spredaj, v istem pogledu kot profil, z osjo na sredini. Telo nastane tam, kjer si narisal profil: vodoravna črta skozi sredino je tla, profil nad ploščo pa da telo, ki stoji na njej. To velja na osnovni plošči.
