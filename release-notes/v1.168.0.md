## What's new in 1.168.0

- **Resize right into the corner**
  When you drag a corner handle to make a shape bigger, both of its sides now stop at the edges of other shapes and at the walls they run into. So a part fills the corner of a case in one drag.
- **3MF and OBJ: small parts of a group stay**
  In designs of several colours, very small parts of a group, such as the teeth and dots of a logo, were still missing from the 3MF and OBJ, while the STL had them. Now such parts stay in the file too.
- **No more freezing while large imported assemblies are saved**
  In designs with an imported STEP assembly, a circuit board in an enclosure for example, the editor froze for several seconds after almost every change, because the automatic save packed the whole design again each time. Now only what has changed is packed again, and the editor stays usable meanwhile.
- **3MF from a scanner: one body instead of thousands**
  A 3MF from a 3D scanner carries its own colour at almost every point. layerling made one body per colour: one scan became more than 42,000 bodies, and the import took minutes. Such a file now comes in as one body in a single colour, with a note that the colour gradient is not kept. Files with a few colours still become one body per colour.
- **Edit a sketch on plate 2**
  With several build plates, Edit sketch for a body on a further plate showed the first plate, and the body's outline lay outside the sketch area. The sketch now opens on the plate the body stands on.
- **A chamfer on a group no longer closes a tube's bore**
  A tube, ring or bent tube with "Bore cuts as a hole when grouped" opens whatever it is grouped with. A chamfer or fillet on that group closed the opening again. Now it stays open.
- **3D mouse: directions stay right after a restart**
  After the browser is restarted, layerling reads a 3D mouse as a game controller until you click Connect 3D mouse again. On some devices the directions run the other way round there, and the ticked directions no longer fitted. layerling now keeps them apart for the two ways: under Settings, Controls, 3D mouse a line says when the mouse is read as a game controller, and what you tick then holds for that only. Your settings so far stay with the connected mouse.
- **When the 3D view does not start: a clear message**
  When the browser gives layerling no access to the graphics (WebGL), all you saw was "layerling ran into an error". The window now says what is going on: the cause lies in the browser or the graphics driver, and your designs are kept. Below that it lists what usually helps: restart the browser or the computer, turn on graphics acceleration, update the browser and the graphics driver, or try another browser. You can still save the last stored state as a .lyl file from there.
- **Build plates: name on plate 1 and in the file name**
  When the first build plate has a name of its own, it is now written on the plate as on the further plates: "Plate 1: Lid", with the design's name in small above it. And when you pick a single plate in the export window, the file name starts as that plate's name instead of the design's; a plate without a name becomes "Design - Plate 2".
- **Object list: the name of a locked shape**
  When the shape of a part is locked (the shield), its name stays as it is too. The object list still offered the pencil and opened the name field, but then did not take the new name. The pencil is now left out for such parts. To rename one, click the shield in its row, which unlocks the shape.
- **User guide: search for an exact phrase with quotation marks**
  The search box of the user guide now understands quotation marks: "circular arc" finds only the sections where these words stand next to each other in this order. A single word in quotation marks is found only as a whole word. Until now a search with quotation marks found nothing at all.
- **Hide the handles with Shift+Q**
  On very small parts the squares and arrows around the selection easily cover what you are working on. Shift+Q hides all handles and shows them again, as Q does for the sizes and distances. You can still drag the part, and type its sizes at the right.

So that you see what is selected without the handles, the selected row in the object list now has a stronger colour and an orange bar at its left.
- **Error message stays in view with the buttons**
  In a long tool panel, Hollow for example, Cancel and Apply always stayed in view, but the message why something cannot be done stood at the very bottom and only showed after scrolling down. A click then seemed to do nothing. The message now stands right above the buttons and stays in view there.
- **Tape measure measures at once**
  A click on the tape measure now switches Add measurement on straight away: click the tape measure, click the first point, click the second, done. Until now you had to pick the plus after opening it.

Another click on the plus puts the tool down and leaves the buttons open, to select a body in between for example. If the measurements are hidden, the tape measure opens without the tool, so they stay hidden.
- **History is kept after a change in a field**
  If you changed a measure in its field with the mouse wheel or the arrow keys and then closed or reloaded the tab without leaving the field, the design opened without its history the next time: Undo and the History view were empty. The same happened to a .lyl file saved with Ctrl+S in that moment. The history now stays complete, and the last change is a step of its own in it.

Designs that already lost their history this way do not get it back. Their current state is unchanged, and a group can still be ungrouped or opened with Edit group.
- **Hole pattern: turn squares and hexagons**
  The Angle of the holes slider in the hole pattern now works for squares and hexagons too, not only for rectangles and slots. A square at 45° is a diamond, a hexagon at 30° lies on a flat side. The spacing follows, so a wall always stays between the turned holes.
- **Hole pattern: a hole in the middle**
  A ring of holes along the edge, or mounting holes, can now have one more hole in the middle of the face, for the drain holes in the floor of a plant pot, say. The box "A hole in the middle" is in the hole pattern under "Along the edge" and on the "Mounting holes" tab, and is off at first.

The hole is like the others (shape, hole type, depth) and does not count in their number. For plain round, square and hexagon holes its size can be set on its own. Where it does not fit, because the middle is open or it would come too close to the others, it is left out and a line says so.
- **M3.5 in the size lists**
  M3.5 is now offered between M3 and M4: for the thread (rod, screw, nut and tapped hole, pitch 0.6 mm), the nut pocket of the hexagon, the washers of the tube, and as a screw size of the countersink, the double countersink, the finishing washer and the countersunk holes of the hole pattern.

The counterbore does not offer M3.5, because there is no standard socket head cap screw of that size; there you type the measures yourself.
- **Hollow with a preview**
  The Hollow tool now shows the body hollowed while its window is open. Change the wall thickness, the open sides or one of the other settings, and the preview follows a moment later. With no side open the body is shown see-through, so you can see the cavity.

Nothing is changed until you click "Hollow"; "Cancel" leaves the body as it was. If a setting does not suit the body, a wall too thick for example, the window says so at once.
- **Select all shapes of one colour**
  In the object list, a click on the colour dot in front of a name selects every visible shape of that colour, handy when each component has a colour of its own. Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac) or Shift adds them to what is selected. "Select same colour" in the right-click menu and the command search (Ctrl+K) does the same for the colours of the selected shapes.
- **Tape measure shows the thickness under the pointer**
  With Add measurement active on the tape measure and no point placed yet, pointing at a face shows how thick the body is there: measured straight in, square to the face, to where the material ends. A dashed line shows where it measures. So you check walls and floors just by moving the mouse over the part. A click still sets the first measuring point as before.
- **A group takes the name of the named part**
  When exactly one part has a name of its own as you group or bundle, the new group is now called after it, instead of just Group or Bundle. So the name stays visible in the object list. With several named parts nothing changes; the parts keep their names inside the group.
- **Curved text all the way round the circle**
  Curved text always left a gap between its end and its start, it took at most 84 % of the circle. With the new switch "All the way round" in the inspector it closes the circle: the letters are spread evenly all the way round, and the gap between the last and the first is the same as between the others. Handy for a date ring from 1 to 31, a dial or a scale.

The letters keep their size; only a text too long for the circle gets smaller. The join lies opposite the middle of the text, turn the text to move it.
- **Polygon: flat side in front**
  An octagon used to measure from corner to corner: type 26 mm and you got only 24 mm between its flat sides. The Polygon now has the switch “Flat side in front” under “Sides”. It turns the polygon by half a side, so an octagon's length and width run from flat to flat, like the size of a wrench.

Turning the switch keeps the body as it is, only the two numbers change with it. A hexagon then has its flat sides in front and behind instead of left and right.
- **Sketch: a point's coordinates**
  With the pointer resting on a point of a sketch, a small label beside it shows the point's X and Y. It follows the point while you drag it, so you see at once where it will land. "Show measurements" hides it with the other dimensions.

Also, a point now stays where it is when you only click it: until now it could jump a little, onto the grid.
- **Edit sketch warns about lost chamfers**
  When you open the sketch of a body that already has fillets, chamfers or a hollowing, layerling now says right away that they go when you finish, because the body is built anew. Cancel keeps them. After finishing it says so again, and Undo brings them back.
- **Shapes lying on their side: the height handle stays on top**
  A shape turned 90° onto its side had its height handle pointing sideways and its corner handles standing in the air. The handles of a shape lying on its side now stand upright on the workplane, as in Tinkercad: the handle on top pulls it up, the underside stays where it is, and the handles at the bottom change the sizes along the plate. A lying cylinder stays round.
- **Image texture: PNG drawings without steps**
  A line drawing or a logo as a PNG or JPEG, pressed on once, is now cut along its outlines like an SVG: slanted lines and curves come out smooth instead of stepped. In an older design, simply load the picture again.
- **New surface texture: Hammered**
  Round, overlapping dents like on a hand-hammered copper mug, each a little different in size and depth. You find it under Surface texture; Dent size sets how much they overlap, and Arrangement lays them out anew.
- **Split a screw: filament as the pins**
  When you split a screw or threaded rod lengthwise, from M4 up, pieces of 1.75 mm filament can be the pins, even where printed pins do not fit. Tick Filament as pins; the message tells you how long to cut the pieces.
- **Mold: set the pour opening and the vents yourself**
  For the two-part mold, Set the openings myself lets you decide where the material runs in and the air escapes: a click on the master sets a sprue or a vent, a click on its post takes it away. Take over the suggested ones gives you the automatic ones as a start.
- **Magnet pockets**
  New in the shape library under Holes & cut-outs: the Magnet pocket. Click a spot and layerling cuts a pocket for a round magnet with a glue well below it, and if you like the matching one in the part that meets it. Split now offers magnets as connectors too.
- **Sketch: a held arrow key without lag**
  When you hold an arrow key in the sketch, the selection now follows the key and stops as soon as you let go. On large outlines it used to lag far behind and kept moving long after the key was released. A held run is still one step of Undo.

## Kaj je novega v 1.168.0

- **Spreminjanje velikosti čisto v kot**
  Ko povlečeš ročaj v kotu, da oblika postane večja, se zdaj obe njeni stranici ustavita na robovih drugih oblik in na stenah, v katere tečeta. Tako del v enem potegu zapolni kot ohišja.
- **3MF in OBJ: majhni deli skupine ostanejo**
  Pri načrtih v več barvah so zelo majhni deli skupine, na primer zobci in pike logotipa, v 3MF in OBJ še vedno manjkali, v STL pa so bili. Zdaj taki deli ostanejo tudi v datoteki.
- **Brez zamrznitev pri shranjevanju velikih uvoženih sestavov**
  Pri načrtih z uvoženim sestavom STEP, na primer vezje v ohišju, se je urejevalnik po skoraj vsaki spremembi za nekaj sekund zamrznil, ker je samodejno shranjevanje vsakič znova pakiralo ves načrt. Zdaj se znova pakira samo spremenjeno, urejevalnik pa vmes ostane uporaben.
- **3MF iz skenerja: eno telo namesto tisočev**
  3MF iz 3D-skenerja ima skoraj v vsaki točki svojo barvo. layerling je naredil eno telo na barvo: en sken je postal več kot 42.000 teles, uvoz pa je trajal minute. Taka datoteka zdaj pride kot eno telo v eni barvi, z opombo, da barvni prehod ni ohranjen. Datoteke z nekaj barvami še vedno postanejo eno telo na barvo.
- **Urejanje skice na plošči 2**
  Pri več gradbenih ploščah je »Edit sketch« za telo na nadaljnji plošči pokazal prvo ploščo, obris telesa pa je ležal zunaj območja skice. Skica se zdaj odpre na plošči, na kateri telo stoji.
- **Posnetje na skupini ne zapre več izvrtine cevi**
  Cev, obroč ali ukrivljena cev z »Bore cuts as a hole when grouped« odpre vse, s čimer je združena. Posnetje ali zaobljenje na tej skupini je odprtino spet zaprlo. Zdaj ostane odprta.
- **3D miška: smeri ostanejo pravilne po ponovnem zagonu**
  Po ponovnem zagonu brskalnika layerling bere 3D miško kot igralni krmilnik, dokler znova ne klikneš Connect 3D mouse. Na nekaterih napravah so smeri tam obrnjene, označene smeri pa niso več ustrezale. layerling zdaj za oba načina hrani nastavitve ločeno: v nastavitvah pod Controls, 3D mouse vrstica pove, kdaj se miška bere kot igralni krmilnik, in kar tedaj označiš, velja samo za to. Tvoje dosedanje nastavitve ostanejo pri povezani miški.
- **Ko se 3D pogled ne zažene: jasno sporočilo**
  Ko brskalnik layerlingu ne da dostopa do grafike (WebGL), si videl samo »layerling ran into an error«. Okno zdaj pove, kaj se dogaja: vzrok je v brskalniku ali grafičnem gonilniku, tvoji načrti pa so ohranjeni. Spodaj našteje, kaj običajno pomaga: znova zaženi brskalnik ali računalnik, vklopi grafično pospeševanje, posodobi brskalnik in grafični gonilnik ali poskusi drug brskalnik. Zadnje shranjeno stanje lahko od tam še vedno shraniš kot datoteko .lyl.
- **Gradbene plošče: ime na plošči 1 in v imenu datoteke**
  Če ima prva gradbena plošča svoje ime, je zdaj napisano na plošči kot pri nadaljnjih ploščah: »Plate 1: Lid«, nad njim pa z drobno pisavo ime načrta. In ko v oknu za izvoz izbereš eno ploščo, se ime datoteke začne z imenom te plošče namesto z imenom načrta; plošča brez imena postane »Design - Plate 2«.
- **Seznam predmetov: ime zaklenjene oblike**
  Ko je oblika dela zaklenjena (ščit), ostane tudi njeno ime kot je. Seznam predmetov je še ponujal svinčnik in odprl polje za ime, a novega imena ni sprejel. Svinčnika za take dele zdaj ni. Če ga želiš preimenovati, klikni ščit v njegovi vrstici, kar odklene obliko.
- **Uporabniški priročnik: iskanje točne fraze z narekovaji**
  Iskalno polje priročnika zdaj razume narekovaje: »circular arc« najde samo razdelke, v katerih te besede stojijo ena ob drugi v tem vrstnem redu. Posamezna beseda v narekovajih se najde samo kot cela beseda. Do zdaj iskanje z narekovaji ni našlo prav nič.
- **Skrij ročaje s Shift+Q**
  Pri zelo majhnih delih kvadratki in puščice okoli izbora zlahka prekrijejo to, na čemer delaš. Shift+Q skrije vse ročaje in jih spet pokaže, kot Q za mere in razdalje. Del še vedno lahko povlečeš in desno vtipkaš njegove mere.

Da vidiš, kaj je izbrano, tudi brez ročajev, ima izbrana vrstica na seznamu predmetov zdaj močnejšo barvo in oranžen trak na levi.
- **Sporočilo o napaki ostane v pogledu z gumbi**
  Pri dolgi plošči orodja, na primer Hollow, sta Cancel in Apply vedno ostala v pogledu, sporočilo, zakaj nekaj ni mogoče, pa je stalo čisto na dnu in se je pokazalo šele po pomikanju. Klik je potem videti, kot da ne naredi nič. Sporočilo zdaj stoji tik nad gumbi in tam ostane v pogledu.
- **Trakec meri takoj**
  Klik na trakec zdaj takoj vklopi Add measurement: klikni trakec, klikni prvo točko, klikni drugo, končano. Do zdaj si moral po odprtju izbrati plus.

Nov klik na plus odloži orodje in pusti gumbe odprte, na primer za vmesno izbiro telesa. Če so meritve skrite, se trakec odpre brez orodja, zato ostanejo skrite.
- **Zgodovina se ohrani po spremembi v polju**
  Če si mero v polju spremenil s kolescem miške ali s puščicami in nato zaprl ali ponovno naložil zavihek, ne da bi zapustil polje, se je načrt naslednjič odprl brez zgodovine: Undo in pogled History sta bila prazna. Isto se je zgodilo datoteki .lyl, shranjeni s Ctrl+S v tistem trenutku. Zgodovina zdaj ostane popolna, zadnja sprememba pa je v njej korak zase.

Načrti, ki so zgodovino na ta način že izgubili, je ne dobijo nazaj. Njihovo trenutno stanje je nespremenjeno, skupino pa še vedno lahko razdružiš ali odpreš z Edit group.
- **Vzorec lukenj: vrtenje kvadratov in šestkotnikov**
  Drsnik Angle of the holes pri vzorcu lukenj zdaj deluje tudi za kvadrate in šestkotnike, ne samo za pravokotnike in reže. Kvadrat pri 45° je romb, šestkotnik pri 30° leži na ravni stranici. Razmik sledi, zato med zasukanimi luknjami vedno ostane stena.
- **Vzorec lukenj: luknja na sredini**
  Venec lukenj ob robu ali montažne luknje imajo lahko zdaj še eno luknjo na sredini ploskve, na primer za odtočne luknje v dnu cvetličnega lonca. Okence »A hole in the middle« je v vzorcu lukenj pod »Along the edge« in na zavihku »Mounting holes« in je sprva izklopljeno.

Luknja je kot druge (oblika, vrsta luknje, globina) in se ne všteva v njihovo število. Pri navadnih okroglih, kvadratnih in šestkotnih luknjah lahko velikost nastaviš posebej. Kjer ne gre, ker je sredina odprta ali bi bila preblizu drugim, jo izpusti in vrstica to pove.
- **M3.5 na seznamih velikosti**
  M3.5 je zdaj ponujen med M3 in M4: za navoj (palica, vijak, matica in navojna luknja, korak 0,6 mm), žep za matico šestkotnika, podložke cevi in kot velikost vijaka pri vgrezu, dvojnem vgrezu, zaključni podložki in vgreznih luknjah vzorca lukenj.

Z zaglobitvijo (counterbore) M3.5 ni ponujen, ker standardnega vijaka z valjasto glavo te velikosti ni; tam mere vtipkaš sam.
- **Hollow s predogledom**
  Orodje Hollow zdaj, ko je njegovo okno odprto, kaže izdolbeno telo. Spremeni debelino stene, odprte strani ali katero drugo nastavitev in predogled mu čez trenutek sledi. Če ni odprta nobena stran, je telo prikazano prosojno, da vidiš votlino.

Nič se ne spremeni, dokler ne klikneš »Hollow«; »Cancel« pusti telo, kot je bilo. Če nastavitev telesu ne ustreza, na primer prevelika stena, okno to pove takoj.
- **Izberi vse oblike ene barve**
  Na seznamu predmetov klik na barvno piko pred imenom izbere vse vidne oblike te barve, kar pride prav, ko ima vsaka komponenta svojo barvo. Ctrl (Cmd na Macu) ali Shift jih doda k izboru. »Select same colour« v meniju desnega klika in v iskanju ukazov (Ctrl+K) naredi isto za barve izbranih oblik.
- **Trakec pokaže debelino pod kazalcem**
  Z aktivnim Add measurement na trakcu in brez postavljene točke kazanje na ploskev pokaže, kako debelo je tam telo: izmerjeno naravnost noter, pravokotno na ploskev, do mesta, kjer material konča. Črtkana črta kaže, kje meri. Tako stene in dna preveriš kar z miško nad delom. Klik še vedno postavi prvo merilno točko kot prej.
- **Skupina prevzame ime poimenovanega dela**
  Če ima natanko en del svoje ime, ko združiš ali povežeš v snop, se nova skupina zdaj imenuje po njem, namesto samo Group ali Bundle. Ime tako ostane vidno na seznamu predmetov. Pri več poimenovanih delih se nič ne spremeni; deli znotraj skupine obdržijo svoja imena.
- **Ukrivljeno besedilo čisto okoli kroga**
  Ukrivljeno besedilo je med koncem in začetkom vedno puščalo vrzel, zavzelo je največ 84 % kroga. Z novim stikalom »All the way round« v pregledovalniku krog sklene: črke so enakomerno razporejene čisto okoli, razmik med zadnjo in prvo pa je enak kot med drugimi. Prav pride za obroč z datumom od 1 do 31, številčnico ali skalo.

Črke obdržijo velikost; manjše postane samo besedilo, ki je predolgo za krog. Stik leži nasproti sredine besedila, zavrti besedilo, da ga premakneš.
- **Večkotnik: ravna stranica spredaj**
  Osemkotnik se je meril od oglišča do oglišča: vtipkaš 26 mm in dobiš samo 24 mm med ravnima stranicama. Večkotnik ima zdaj stikalo »Flat side in front« pod »Sides«. Večkotnik zavrti za pol stranice, zato dolžina in širina osemkotnika tečeta od ravne do ravne, kot velikost ključa.

Stikalo telo ohrani, kot je, spremenita se samo dve števili. Šestkotnik ima potem ravni stranici spredaj in zadaj namesto levo in desno.
- **Skica: koordinate točke**
  Ko kazalec počiva na točki skice, majhna oznaka ob njej pokaže X in Y točke. Sledi točki, medtem ko jo vlečeš, tako da takoj vidiš, kam bo pristala. »Show measurements« jo skrije skupaj z drugimi merami.

Poleg tega točka zdaj ostane, kjer je, če nanjo samo klikneš: do zdaj je lahko malo poskočila, na mrežo.
- **Urejanje skice opozori na izgubljena posnetja**
  Ko odpreš skico telesa, ki že ima zaobljenja, posnetja ali izdolbljenje, layerling zdaj takoj pove, da ob koncu izginejo, ker je telo zgrajeno znova. Cancel jih ohrani. Po koncu to pove še enkrat in Undo jih vrne.
- **Oblike, ki ležijo na boku: ročaj višine ostane zgoraj**
  Oblika, zasukana za 90° na bok, je imela ročaj višine obrnjen vstran, kotne ročaje pa v zraku. Ročaji oblike, ki leži na boku, zdaj stojijo pokonci na delovni ravnini, kot v Tinkercadu: ročaj zgoraj jo vleče navzgor, spodnja stran ostane, kjer je, ročaji spodaj pa spreminjajo mere vzdolž plošče. Ležeči valj ostane okrogel.
- **Slikovna tekstura: risbe PNG brez stopnic**
  Risba s črtami ali logotip kot PNG ali JPEG, enkrat odtisnjen, je zdaj izrezan po obrisih kot SVG: poševne črte in krivulje so gladke namesto stopničaste. Pri starejšem načrtu sliko preprosto naloži znova.
- **Nova površinska tekstura: Kovano**
  Okrogle, prekrivajoče se vdolbine kot na ročno kovanem bakrenem vrču, vsaka malo drugačna po velikosti in globini. Najdeš jo pod Surface texture; Dent size določa, koliko se prekrivajo, Arrangement pa jih razporedi znova.
- **Razdeli vijak: filament kot zatiči**
  Ko vijak ali navojno palico razdeliš po dolžini, od M4 navzgor, so lahko zatiči kosi filamenta 1,75 mm, tudi kjer natisnjeni zatiči ne gredo. Označi Filament as pins; sporočilo pove, kako dolge kose odrezati.
- **Kalup: odprtino za vlivanje in zračnike nastaviš sam**
  Pri dvodelnem kalupu »Set the openings myself« omogoča, da sam določiš, kje material priteče in zrak uhaja: klik na model postavi vlivnik ali zračnik, klik na njegov stebriček ga odstrani. »Take over the suggested ones« ti kot začetek da samodejne.
- **Žepi za magnete**
  Novo v knjižnici oblik pod Holes & cut-outs: Magnet pocket. Klikni mesto in layerling izreže žep za okrogel magnet z vdolbino za lepilo pod njim, če želiš pa tudi ustreznega v delu, ki se stika. Split zdaj ponuja magnete tudi kot veznike.
- **Skica: držana puščična tipka brez zaostajanja**
  Ko v skici držiš puščično tipko, izbor zdaj sledi tipki in se ustavi takoj, ko jo spustiš. Pri velikih obrisih je prej močno zaostajal in se je premikal še dolgo po spustitvi tipke. Držan niz je še vedno en korak Undo.
