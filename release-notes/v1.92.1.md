## What's new in 1.92.1

- **Narrower: move a sketch outline inwards**
  Next to "Wider", the fill of a sketch body now has "Narrower": the area stays filled and its edge moves in by the line width all round, holes grow. One sketch gives the fit hole and the part that passes through it 0.5 mm smaller, or an inner wall.
- **Set the space between the letters**
  Texts now have a letter spacing in per cent of the letter size, also negative and on curved text. That helps most with fonts of your own that stand too tight or too wide. The letters keep their size and the box follows the line.
- **Bodies under a sketch show their real outline**
  In sketch mode the bodies on the workplane now show in the background with their real outline: a polygon, a star, a heart, a wedge and the other shapes were all drawn as a square there. The sketch's snap points follow that outline as well.
- **The shortcuts print on one sheet**
  The printer button in the shortcuts window now puts the whole list, in the editor and in sketch mode, on one sheet (A4 or Letter): black on white, in three columns, as a quick reference to keep at hand.
- **Right-angle snapping and quick delete in the tape measure**
  When placing the second point of a measurement, the tape measure now snaps at a right angle to an opposing edge or face - or anywhere on it while you hold Shift, which makes measuring the clear width of a hole or the gap between two parts exact. And while adding measurements, a click with Shift or Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac) deletes a point or a line right away without switching modes.
- **Corner ruler: X and Y are the right way round**
  On the corner ruler the green numbers had the tooltips "X position" and "Y position to ruler" the wrong way round: when a body moved to the back, the number with the X tooltip changed. Now each number is named by the direction of its arm: X to the right, Y to the back, Z up, also after the ruler is turned by a quarter.
- **Snap grid reachable with several parts selected**
  With several parts selected, the panel on the right reached down to the bottom and covered the snap grid choice in the corner. The snap grid, with the switch for snapping to shapes, now sits at the foot of that panel, as it does for a single part. Move the panel away and it is back in the corner.
- **Selection frame: green for "touching"**
  Drag the frame from right to left (or the other way round with Alt) and it takes everything it touches. It is now green and dashed then, so you see at once how it selects. The frame that must enclose parts completely stays orange.

## Kaj je novega v 1.92.1

- **Ožje: obris skice premakni navznoter**
  Ob »Širše« ima zapolnitev telesa skice zdaj tudi »Ožje«: ploskev ostane zapolnjena, njen rob se premakne navznoter za širino črte, luknje pa se povečajo. Ena skica da luknjo za prileganje in del, ki gre skozi njo, 0,5 mm manjši, ali notranjo steno.
- **Nastavi razmik med črkami**
  Besedila imajo zdaj razmik med črkami v odstotkih velikosti črke, tudi negativen in na ukrivljenem besedilu. To najbolj pomaga pri lastnih pisavah, ki stojijo pretesno ali preširoko. Črke obdržijo velikost, okvir pa sledi vrstici.
- **Telesa pod skico kažejo svoj pravi obris**
  V načinu skice se telesa na delovni ravnini v ozadju zdaj kažejo s pravim obrisom: mnogokotnik, zvezda, srce, klin in druge oblike so bile tam vse narisane kot kvadrat. Tudi pritrdilne točke skice sledijo temu obrisu.
- **Bližnjice se natisnejo na en list**
  Gumb tiskalnika v oknu bližnjic zdaj celoten seznam, v urejevalniku in v načinu skice, natisne na en list (A4 ali Letter): črno na belem, v treh stolpcih, kot hitra referenca pri roki.
- **Pravokotno pritrjevanje in hitro brisanje pri merilnem traku**
  Pri postavljanju druge točke meritve se merilni trak zdaj pritrdi pravokotno na nasprotni rob ali ploskev, ali kjer koli na njej, ko držiš Shift, zaradi česar je merjenje čiste širine luknje ali reže med dvema delovoma natančno. Med dodajanjem meritev klik s Shiftom ali Ctrl (Cmd na Macu) takoj izbriše točko ali črto, ne da bi menjal način.
- **Kotno ravnilo: X in Y sta spet prav obrnjena**
  Na kotnem ravnilu sta imeli zeleni števili namige »Položaj X« in »Položaj Y do ravnila« napačno obrnjena: ko se je telo premaknilo nazaj, se je spremenila številka z namigom X. Zdaj je vsaka številka poimenovana po smeri svojega kraka: X v desno, Y nazaj, Z navzgor, tudi ko ravnilo zavrtiš za četrtino.
- **Pritrdilna mreža dosegljiva ob izbranih več delih**
  Ob izbranih več delih je plošča na desni segala do dna in prekrila izbiro pritrdilne mreže v kotu. Pritrdilna mreža s stikalom za pritrjevanje na oblike zdaj stoji na dnu te plošče, kot pri enem delu. Ploščo odmakni in vrne se v kot.
- **Okvir izbire: zelen za »dotikanje«**
  Povleci okvir z desne na levo (ali obratno z Alt) in vzame vse, česar se dotakne. Takrat je zdaj zelen in črtkan, da takoj vidiš, kako izbira. Okvir, ki mora dele povsem zajeti, ostane oranžen.
