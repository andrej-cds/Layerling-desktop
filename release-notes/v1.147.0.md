## What's new in 1.147.0

- **Hole patterns round a cylinder**
  The hole pattern now also takes the round wall of a cylinder, tube or bore: click the wall, and the holes go all the way round in rows, each pointing at the axis. Good for pen holders, lamp shades and tubes with vents. "Through the wall" cuts only the wall you clicked.
- **Split with dowels or plugs**
  When you split a part that is too big, "Connectors" in the Split panel can now add dowels or plugs that line the halves up again: "Dowels" makes a hole in each half and lays loose pins beside them, "Plugs" puts round pegs on one half and their holes in the other. Rings on the plane show where they go first, and the fit follows your printer's play.
- **Blind holes with a drill point**
  A cylinder set as a hole, a counterbore and a countersink can now end in the point of a drill instead of flat: "Bottom" offers 90°, 118° and 135° or an angle of your own. As in a drawing the depth counts to where the point begins, and the hint says how much further it reaches.
- **Half-timbering and a stone wall as surface textures**
  For model railway houses and dioramas the Surface texture card has two new patterns: "Half-timbering", beams, rails and braces over slightly rough plaster, and "Stone wall", irregular stones from broken rubble to round field stones. With "Pattern as its own part" the beams or stones print in a second colour.
- **New designs with a framing square at the origin**
  If you use the framing square nearly all the time, tick "Start new designs with a framing square at the origin" under Measurement in the settings. Every new design in this browser then starts with one at the origin. Designs you already have stay as they are.
- **Bricks without stubs at the edges**
  Small bricks showed short ticks instead of brickwork along the top, the bottom and round windows, where the border left only a sliver of a course. Such slivers now stay smooth; walls without them look as before.
- **Arrow keys move smoothly**
  A held arrow key now moves the selection as smoothly as dragging with the mouse, even in a large design. Letting go makes one step, which one Undo takes back whole.

## Kaj je novega v 1.147.0

- **Vzorec lukenj okoli valja**
  Vzorec lukenj zdaj sprejme tudi okroglo steno valja, cevi ali izvrtine: klikni steno in luknje gredo v vrstah vse naokoli, vsaka je obrnjena proti osi. Dobro za lončke za svinčnike, senčnike in cevi z zračenjem. »Skozi steno« prereže samo steno, na katero si kliknil.
- **Deljenje s čepi ali vodilnimi zatiči**
  Ko deliš preveliko telo, okno »Split« pod »Connectors« zdaj po želji doda zatiče ali čepe, ki polovici spet natančno poravnata: »Dowels« naredi luknjo v vsako polovico in poleg položi proste zatiče, »Plugs« postavi okrogle čepe na eno polovico in njihove luknje v drugo. Obroči na ravnini vnaprej pokažejo, kam pridejo, zračnost pa sledi zračnosti tvojega tiskalnika.
- **Slepe luknje s konico svedra**
  Valj kot izrez, grezilo in stopničasta izvrtina se zdaj lahko končajo s konico svedra namesto ravno: pod »Bottom« so 90°, 118° in 135° ali poljuben kot. Globina šteje kot v risbi do začetka konice, namig pa pove, koliko globlje seže.
- **Lesena konstrukcija in kamnit zid kot površina**
  Za makete hišic in diorame ima kartica Surface texture dva nova vzorca: »Half-timbering« s tramovi, prečkami in podporniki nad rahlo grobim ometom ter »Stone wall« z nepravilnimi kamni, od lomljenca do okroglega kamna. Z »Pattern as its own part« natisneš tramove ali kamne v drugi barvi.
- **Novi osnutki z lestvijo kota v izhodišču**
  Kdor lestvico kota skoraj vedno potrebuje, v nastavitvah pod »Measurement« obkljuka »Start new designs with a framing square at the origin«. Potem je v vsakem novem osnutku tega brskalnika ena že v izhodišču. Obstoječi osnutki ostanejo, kot so.
- **Opeke brez štrcljev ob robu**
  Majhne opeke so ob zgornjem in spodnjem robu ter okoli oken kazale kratke črtice namesto zidu, kjer je rob pustil le ozek ostanek vrste. Taki ostanki zdaj ostanejo gladki; zidovi brez njih izgledajo kot prej.
- **Puščične tipke premikajo tekoče**
  Držana puščična tipka zdaj premika izbor tako tekoče kot vlečenje z miško, tudi v velikih osnutkih. Ob spustu nastane en sam korak, ki ga en Undo v celoti razveljavi.
