## What's new in 1.171.0

- **Detail level: put the aids away step by step**
  Is too much drawn round your model? The view bar now has the detail level, in the editor and in the sketch. A slider puts away in five steps what you do not need right now: notes and labels, reference points and distances, dimensions, snap guide lines and axis arrows, and last the grid.

– Every aid has its own switch below the slider.
– Hold V to see everything for a moment.
– Only the view changes, never the design. Snapping and measuring go on.
– The editor and the sketch each have their own slider, and your browser remembers both.
- **Search in the settings**
  The settings window now has a search field above its areas. Type "zoom", for example, and you see every setting that has to do with it, from all areas one below the other. You can change them right there.

– Labels, hints and choices are searched, your printer for instance.
– Common other words find a setting too, such as "mouse wheel" or "dark mode".
– Each area shows how many settings were found. A click opens the whole area with those settings marked.
- **Installing as an app in Firefox**
  The start page said that Firefox cannot install apps. On Windows it can since Firefox 143: click the icon “Add tab to taskbar” at the right of the address bar. Your designs stay as they are. The hint now names this way.
- **New shape: Fan**
  Under Systems & templates there is now the Fan next to the SBC board, true to size from the makers' drawings.

– Radial blowers 5015 (also with a wide outlet), 4020 and 4010.
– Axial fans from 25×10 to 120×25.
– Screw holes, inlet and outlet can each reach through the wall, with an adjustable length and clearance.

So a fan bay or an air duct at the print head fits the first time. Also available through MCP.
- **Multicolor groups: your choice stays**
  Switch on "Multicolor" for a group (also with Shift+M), and the next groups you make start in their parts' colours too. It stays that way until you switch it off for a group again. Your browser remembers the choice.
- **Settings in a sketch too**
  The gear at the top right now opens the settings while you draw a sketch too, and you see new colours at once. In the quick guide, the link to the full user guide is now right at the top.
- **No more empty file when saving**
  When you save a design in Chrome or Edge, layerling now checks that it really arrived in the file. If it did not, because a virus scanner got in the way for example, a message says so, and "Download instead" saves it as a download. A file that stayed empty is removed again.
- **Groups of many fine shapes stay closed**
  Grouping many fine shapes with a hole, such as rings of 512 sides each, gave a surface with gaps and no notice above the limit for imported meshes. A slicer could then fill the whole body.

Shapes from the library are now always cut exactly first. If gaps remain all the same, the status bar says so. A group made before has to be grouped again from its parts.
- **Chamfer and fillet on the rim of a hole**
  The rim of a hole is often made of two arcs. On some bodies a chamfer worked only when you clicked the right one. Now it mostly does not matter which: when a chamfer or fillet cannot be built from the piece you clicked, it is tried again from the other pieces of the rim.
- **Picking edges from below**
  In the chamfer or fillet tool, a see-through body seen from below through the workplane was reduced to its bottom face. With "Only visible edges" the edges above the plate were missing there as well. Both show again now.

## Kaj je novega v 1.171.0

- **Raven podrobnosti: pomagala odlagaj korak za korakom**
  Je okoli tvojega modela narisanega preveč? Vrstica pogleda ima zdaj raven podrobnosti, v urejevalniku in v skici. Drsnik v petih korakih odloži, česar zdaj ne potrebuješ: opombe in oznake, referenčne točke in razdalje, mere, vodilne črte pripenjanja in osne puščice, nazadnje mrežo.

– Vsako pomagalo ima svoje stikalo pod drsnikom.
– Drži V, da za hip vidiš vse.
– Spremeni se samo pogled, nikoli načrt. Pripenjanje in merjenje delujeta naprej.
– Urejevalnik in skica imata vsak svoj drsnik, brskalnik si zapomni oba.
- **Iskanje v nastavitvah**
  Okno nastavitev ima zdaj iskalno polje nad področji. Vtipkaj na primer »zoom« in vidiš vse nastavitve, ki imajo kaj s tem, iz vseh področij pod seboj. Tam jih lahko tudi spremeniš.

– Išče se po oznakah, namigih in izbirah, na primer po tvojem tiskalniku.
– Najde tudi pogoste druge besede, kot »mouse wheel« ali »dark mode«.
– Vsako področje pokaže število najdenih nastavitev. Klik odpre celo področje z označenimi nastavitvami.
- **Namestitev kot aplikacija v Firefoxu**
  Začetna stran je pisala, da Firefox ne more nameščati aplikacij. V sistemu Windows od Firefoxa 143 lahko: klikni ikono »Add tab to taskbar« desno od naslovne vrstice. Tvoji načrti ostanejo, kot so. Namig zdaj imenuje to pot.
- **Nova oblika: Ventilator**
  Pod Systems & templates je zdaj Ventilator ob plošči SBC, v pravi velikosti po risbah proizvajalcev.

– Radialni ventilatorji 5015 (tudi s širokim izhodom), 4020 in 4010.
– Aksialni ventilatorji od 25×10 do 120×25.
– Luknje za vijake, vhod in izhod lahko vsak sega skozi steno, z nastavljivo dolžino in zračnostjo.

Tako ležišče ventilatorja ali zračni kanal pri tiskalni glavi ustreza že prvič. Na voljo tudi prek MCP.
- **Večbarvne skupine: tvoja izbira ostane**
  Vklopi »Multicolor« za skupino (tudi s Shift+M) in naslednje skupine, ki jih narediš, se prav tako začnejo v barvah svojih delov. Tako ostane, dokler ga za skupino spet ne izklopiš. Brskalnik si izbiro zapomni.
- **Nastavitve tudi v skici**
  Zobnik zgoraj desno zdaj odpre nastavitve tudi med risanjem skice, nove barve pa vidiš takoj. V hitrem vodniku je povezava do celotnega priročnika zdaj čisto na vrhu.
- **Ni več prazne datoteke pri shranjevanju**
  Ko načrt shraniš v Chromu ali Edgeu, layerling zdaj preveri, da je res prišel v datoteko. Če ni, na primer ker je vmes posegel protivirusni program, to sporočilo pove, »Download instead« pa ga shrani kot prenos. Datoteka, ki je ostala prazna, se spet odstrani.
- **Skupine mnogih finih oblik ostanejo zaprte**
  Združevanje mnogih finih oblik z luknjo, na primer obročev po 512 stranic, je dalo površino z vrzelmi in brez opozorila nad mejo za uvožene mreže. Razrezovalnik je potem lahko napolnil celo telo.

Oblike iz knjižnice so zdaj vedno najprej rezane natančno. Če vrzeli kljub temu ostanejo, vrstica stanja to pove. Skupino, narejeno prej, je treba znova združiti iz delov.
- **Posnetje in zaobljenje na robu luknje**
  Rob luknje je pogosto narejen iz dveh lokov. Pri nekaterih telesih je posnetje delovalo samo, če si kliknil pravega. Zdaj večinoma ni pomembno, katerega: ko posnetja ali zaobljenja ni mogoče zgraditi iz kosa, na katerega si kliknil, se poskusi znova iz drugih kosov roba.
- **Izbiranje robov od spodaj**
  V orodju za posnetje ali zaobljenje se je prosojno telo, gledano od spodaj skozi delovno ravnino, zreduciralo na spodnjo ploskev. Z »Only visible edges« so tam manjkali tudi robovi nad ploščo. Zdaj se oboje spet prikaže.
