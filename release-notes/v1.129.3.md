## What's new in 1.129.3

- **STEP export works with text**
  Exporting a design that contains text to STEP no longer stops with "The fonts are not loaded yet". Text is now exported to STEP on the page itself, where its fonts are loaded.

---

## Kaj je novega v 1.129.3

- **Izvoz v STEP deluje z besedilom**
  Izvoz projekta z besedilom v STEP se ne ustavi več z opozorilom »The fonts are not loaded yet«. Besedilo se zdaj v STEP izvozi na strani sami, kjer so pisave naložene.
