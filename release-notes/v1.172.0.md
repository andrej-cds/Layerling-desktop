## What's new in 1.172.0

- **Patties: a pattern plate**
  New in the shape library: a flat plate with one of thirteen patterns, such as dots, flowers, scales, cells or rays, or with a figure of your own from an SVG, PNG or JPG.

– As a stencil, a stamp, a mould or the figures alone
– Rectangular, round, or a fan that bends round a mug or pot
– The figures alone, made a hole and grouped with a body, cut the pattern into it
– The pattern can be saved as an SVG for a laser or cutting plotter
- **Molds: skin mold, core mold and cases**
  The Mold & die tool can do more.

– Silicone skin mold: a thin silicone mold in a printed shell that saves a lot of silicone
– Core mold for pots, cups and vases
– Two cases to pour mold halves from silicone or plaster
– Two-part mold poured through the seam, with a wick hole, a sealing lip or four side parts
– The window says what to mix, how much of it and what it costs
- **The lid follows its box, with a label for each compartment**
  When you change the compartments or the size of a hollowed box, its lid is made again in the same step. Lids can now carry a label for every compartment, outside or inside. Opened on a box that has a lid, the Lid tool changes that lid. A hinge, latches, a handle and magnets now also work on a box that stands turned.
- **Dividers as a loose insert**
  When hollowing, the dividers can now become a part of their own: an insert you lift out of the box with its contents. The box stays smooth inside, and the insert follows the box when you change it.

A click beside the body with the tool open no longer throws the hollowing away, either.
- **Check the size on paper before you print in 3D**
  The 2D drawing now comes out of the printer at its true size, as a PDF on A4, A3, US Letter or US Tabloid. Print it at 100 % and lay the real part on it, a fan or a circuit board, say: within seconds you see whether the sizes are right.
- **Ungroup all levels**
  In the right-click menu and the command search, Ungroup all levels takes apart the groups inside a group too, in one step. Bundles and locked groups inside stay whole.
- **New fans: 5020 and 50 mm**
  The Fan shape now has the 5020 radial blower and the axial fans 50 x 15 and 50 x 20 mm.
- **Detail level: handles and V**
  The list of the detail level has a switch for the handles at the selection. Holding V also shows the dimensions and handles that Q and Shift+Q have hidden.
- **Keyboard shortcuts that fit your keyboard**
  The shortcut list names the keys as the keyboard of your language does. The letter keys now also work with a Cyrillic, Greek, Arabic or Hebrew keyboard, and the views 1 to 5 on a French keyboard without Shift.
- **Object list for the current plate only**
  When a design has several build plates, a new button in the title bar of the object list shows only the objects on the current plate. Click another plate and the list follows.
- **Finger: no handles**
  On a device with a touch screen, the button with the pointing hand in the view bar has one more choice: it hides all handles at the selection, so none is caught by accident. A finger or pen on the part still moves it.
- **Icon tag from detailed pictures**
  Some detailed PNG or JPG pictures were refused as an icon tag. Now they become one.
- **Tags keep their texture**
  A new colour on a name tag or icon tag leaves textures, rounded edges and paint as they are. Any other change that would remove them asks first.
- **Rotate arrows on thin bodies**
  On thin, turned bodies the rotate arrows lay on top of each other and under the angle label. Now they move apart and can be taken safely.
- **Hollowed bodies after turning**
  When a hollowed body was turned and then resized, the wrong sides were open. Now they stay the same.
- **Blue lift arrow easier to grab**
  The blue arrow for lifting now looks like the red and green ones, shows on dark bodies too and can be grabbed along its whole length.
- **Delete face names the way out**
  When Delete face does not work on an imported mesh, the message now says that Make exact often helps.
- **The bar of build plates stays clear**
  The warning that bodies do not fit the printer's build volume lay over the bar of build plates and hid its buttons. Now it stands above the bar.
- **Export under the design's name**
  When a design has only one body, the export window now suggests the design's name, also after it was renamed. With several bodies it is still the name of the selected one.
- **A typed move works on every build plate**
  A number typed into the movement distance pulled a body on plate 2 or later back to plate 1. Now it moves the body by exactly that much on every plate.
- **Saving does not wait for the backup folder**
  When the browser's question about the backup folder went unanswered, the saved file could stay empty. Now the design is saved after 20 seconds anyway, and a message says so.

## Kaj je novega v 1.172.0

- **Vzorčna plošča (Patties)**
  Novo v knjižnici oblik: ploščata plošča z enim od trinajstih vzorcev, kot so pike, rože, luske, celice ali žarki, ali z lastno figuro iz SVG, PNG ali JPG.

– Kot šablona, štampiljka, kalup ali samo figure
– Pravokotna, okrogla ali pahljača, ki se ovija okoli skodelice ali lonca
– Samo figure, narejene v luknjo in združene s telesom, vrežejo vzorec vanj
– Vzorec lahko shraniš kot SVG za laser ali rezalni plotter
- **Kalupi: kalup kože, jedrni kalup in ohišja**
  Orodje Mold & die zmore več.

– Silikonski kalup kože: tanek silikonski kalup v natisnjeni lupini, ki prihrani veliko silikona
– Jedrni kalup za lonce, skodelice in vaze
– Dve ohišji za vlivanje polovic kalupa iz silikona ali mavca
– Dvodelni kalup, zalit skozi šiv, z luknjo za stenj, tesnilno ustnico ali štirimi stranskimi deli
– Okno pove, kaj zmešati, koliko tega in koliko stane
- **Pokrov sledi škatli, z oznako za vsak predal**
  Ko spremeniš predale ali velikost izdolbene škatle, se njen pokrov naredi znova v istem koraku. Pokrovi lahko zdaj nosijo oznako za vsak predal, zunaj ali znotraj. Odprto na škatli s pokrovom orodje Lid ta pokrov spremeni. Tečaj, zapahi, ročaj in magneti zdaj delujejo tudi na škatli, ki stoji zasukana.
- **Predelne stene kot ločen vložek**
  Pri dolbenju lahko predelne stene zdaj postanejo samostojen del: vložek, ki ga dvigneš iz škatle z vsebino. Škatla ostane znotraj gladka, vložek pa sledi škatli, ko jo spremeniš.

Klik ob telesu z odprtim orodjem tudi ne zavrže več dolbenja.
- **Preveri velikost na papirju, preden tiskaš v 3D**
  Risba 2D zdaj pride iz tiskalnika v pravi velikosti, kot PDF na A4, A3, US Letter ali US Tabloid. Natisni jo pri 100 % in nanjo položi pravi del, na primer ventilator ali vezje: v nekaj sekundah vidiš, ali mere ustrezajo.
- **Razdruži vse ravni**
  V meniju desnega klika in iskanju ukazov »Ungroup all levels« razstavi tudi skupine znotraj skupine, v enem koraku. Snopi in zaklenjene skupine znotraj ostanejo cele.
- **Novi ventilatorji: 5020 in 50 mm**
  Oblika Ventilator ima zdaj radialni ventilator 5020 in aksialna 50 x 15 in 50 x 20 mm.
- **Raven podrobnosti: ročaji in V**
  Seznam ravni podrobnosti ima stikalo za ročaje ob izboru. Držanje V pokaže tudi mere in ročaje, ki sta jih skrila Q in Shift+Q.
- **Bližnjice, ki ustrezajo tvoji tipkovnici**
  Seznam bližnjic poimenuje tipke tako, kot jih imenuje tipkovnica tvojega jezika. Črkovne tipke zdaj delujejo tudi s cirilično, grško, arabsko ali hebrejsko tipkovnico, pogledi 1 do 5 pa na francoski tipkovnici brez Shift.
- **Seznam predmetov samo za trenutno ploščo**
  Ko ima načrt več gradbenih plošč, nov gumb v naslovni vrstici seznama predmetov pokaže samo predmete na trenutni plošči. Klikni drugo ploščo in seznam sledi.
- **Prst: brez ročajev**
  Na napravi z zaslonom na dotik ima gumb s kazalno roko v vrstici pogleda še eno izbiro: skrije vse ročaje ob izboru, da se nobenega ne dotakneš po nesreči. Prst ali pero na delu ga še vedno premakneta.
- **Ikonska oznaka iz podrobnih slik**
  Nekatere podrobne slike PNG ali JPG so bile kot ikonska oznaka zavrnjene. Zdaj postanejo oznaka.
- **Oznake obdržijo teksturo**
  Nova barva na imenski ali ikonski oznaki pusti teksture, zaobljene robove in barvo, kot so. Vsaka druga sprememba, ki bi jih odstranila, vpraša najprej.
- **Puščice za vrtenje na tankih telesih**
  Pri tankih, zasukanih telesih so se puščice za vrtenje prekrivale in ležale pod oznako kota. Zdaj se razmaknejo in jih je mogoče varno prijeti.
- **Izdolbena telesa po vrtenju**
  Ko je bilo izdolbeno telo zasukano in nato spremenjeno v velikosti, so bile odprte napačne strani. Zdaj ostanejo iste.
- **Modro dvižno puščico lažje primeš**
  Modra puščica za dvigovanje zdaj izgleda kot rdeča in zelena, vidna je tudi na temnih telesih in jo je mogoče prijeti po vsej dolžini.
- **Delete face pove izhod**
  Ko Delete face na uvoženi mreži ne deluje, sporočilo zdaj pove, da pogosto pomaga Make exact.
- **Vrstica gradbenih plošč ostane prosta**
  Opozorilo, da telesa ne gredo v gradbeni prostor tiskalnika, je ležalo čez vrstico gradbenih plošč in skrilo njene gumbe. Zdaj stoji nad vrstico.
- **Izvoz pod imenom načrta**
  Ko ima načrt samo eno telo, izvozno okno zdaj predlaga ime načrta, tudi po preimenovanju. Pri več telesih je še vedno ime izbranega.
- **Vtipkan premik deluje na vsaki gradbeni plošči**
  Število, vtipkano v razdaljo premika, je telo na plošči 2 ali kasneje potegnilo nazaj na ploščo 1. Zdaj telo premakne natanko za toliko na vsaki plošči.
- **Shranjevanje ne čaka na mapo za varnostne kopije**
  Ko je vprašanje brskalnika o mapi za varnostne kopije ostalo brez odgovora, je shranjena datoteka lahko ostala prazna. Zdaj se načrt kljub temu shrani po 20 sekundah in sporočilo to pove.
