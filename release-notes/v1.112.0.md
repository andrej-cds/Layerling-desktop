## What's new in 1.112.0

- **Measurements stay with the design**
  The measurements of the tape measure are now saved with the design, also in the .lyl file, and are back when you open it. A new eye button among the tape measure's buttons hides and shows them; that is remembered too.
- **Mark the centre of a face**
  In the right-click menu of a body, "Mark the centre of this face" puts a reference point in the middle of the flat face you clicked, on a side or an end, not only on top. The tape measure snaps to it.
- **Gridfinity trays of any size**
  The Gridfinity shape now has "Tray" under "Type": a tray of any size in mm with a flat bottom and no feet or stacking lip, for board game inserts and the like. Compartments, finger scoops, label shelves and open sides work as in a bin, and the dividers run up to the top.
- **Framing square: measures for the selection only**
  With many bodies at the arms of the framing square, each shows its measures and the numbers can no longer be told apart. A new button under the square's handle switches that off: then only the selected bodies show measures. The choice is kept with the design.
- **A hinge with cones**
  The hinge now also comes with cones instead of a pin: every other knuckle ends in cones that fit into recesses of its neighbours, with no long bridge that could sag. Instead of the number of knuckles you can also give their widest width.
- **Delete several designs at once**
  The start page has a Select button above the designs: a click on a card chooses it, Select all takes every design, and Delete asks once for the whole choice. Many old designs are gone in a moment. It works the same in the server folder.

## Kaj je novega v 1.112.0

- **Meritve ostanejo pri projektu**
  Meritve merilnega traku se zdaj shranijo s projektom, tudi v datoteko .lyl, in ob odprtju spet so. Nov gumb z očesom med gumbi merilnega traku meritve skrije in pokaže; tudi to si program zapomni.
- **Označi središče ploskve**
  V meniju desnega klika na telesu »Označi središče te ploskve« postavi referenčno točko na sredino ravne ploskve, na katero si kliknil, na stranici ali na koncu, ne le zgoraj. Merilni trak se nanjo pripne.
- **Pladnji Gridfinity poljubne velikosti**
  Oblika Gridfinity ima zdaj pod »Vrsta« tudi »Pladenj«: pladenj poljubne velikosti v mm z ravnim dnom, brez nog in brez roba za zlaganje, primeren za vložke za namizne igre in podobno. Predelki, žlebovi za prste, police za oznake in odprte stranice delujejo kot pri posodi, predelne stene pa segajo do vrha.
- **Kotnik: mere samo za izbrana telesa**
  Ko je ob krakih kotnika veliko teles, vsako pokaže svoje mere in števil ni več mogoče ločiti. Nov gumb pod ročajem kotnika to izklopi: potem mere pokažejo samo izbrana telesa. Izbira se shrani s projektom.
- **Tečaj s stožci**
  Tečaj zdaj lahko namesto čepa dobi tudi stožce: vsak drugi člen se konča s stožci, ki se prilegajo v vdolbine sosednjih členov, brez dolgega mostu, ki bi se lahko povesil. Namesto števila členov lahko podaš tudi njihovo največjo širino.
- **Brisanje več projektov naenkrat**
  Začetna stran ima nad projekti gumb Izberi: klik na kartico jo izbere, Izberi vse vzame vse projekte, Izbriši pa vpraša enkrat za celotno izbiro. Veliko starih projektov izgine v hipu. Enako deluje v mapi na strežniku.
