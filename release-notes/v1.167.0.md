## What's new in 1.167.0

- **Move right up to the wall**
  When you drag a part, it now also snaps to the wall it runs into, even the inside wall of a box or case. So a part sits flush in the corner of a case, without measuring.
- **Hole pattern right to the edge**
  With the margin at 0, the hole pattern's edge now cuts the holes off instead of leaving them out, so a grille or a honeycomb runs right to the edge. A new slider "Frame along the edge" keeps a solid frame there if you want one, and the lattice is laid so as few thin strips as possible are left.
- **Hole pattern with a top layer in a second colour**
  In the hole pattern, the top layer can be a net in a second colour, with the body's own colour in the holes. So the face stays flat and both colours start on the first layers, without supports. The 3MF holds the two as separate bodies.
- **Each part's colour in the inspector**
  For a group or bundle under Multicolor, the inspector now lists the parts, each with a colour swatch. So you can recolour one part without opening the group, and the net of a two-colour hole pattern no longer vanishes into the base's colour.
- **Hinged lid: filament fits more easily**
  With a hinged lid, the pin made from a piece of filament often sat too tight in the body's holes, or would not go in at all. The holes are now a little larger: 0.1 mm per side with the default settings instead of 0.05 mm. If your printer needs more room, the hole follows the play from the fit test. The pin still sits tighter in the body than in the lid, so it does not slide out.
- **19″ rack holes**
  The mounting holes now offer "19″ rack" next to "Screws": four slots in the corners of a front panel, exactly where a rack's rails have their holes. The height is fitted to the panel or follows a standard 1U to 6U panel. A line shows where the holes came out.
- **Save only the selection as LYL**
  In the export window, the LYL file can now hold only the selected objects, as a new design without history, notes or attachments. Your design stays tied to its own file. Handy for parts you keep in a library and bring back with Insert from design.

## Kaj je novega v 1.167.0

- **Premik čisto do stene**
  Ko povlečeš del, se zdaj prilepi tudi na steno, v katero zadene, tudi na notranjo steno škatle ali ohišja. Tako del sedi poravnano v kotu ohišja, brez merjenja.
- **Vzorec lukenj čisto do roba**
  Pri robu 0 rob vzorca lukenj zdaj luknje odreže, namesto da jih izpusti, zato rešetka ali satovje teče čisto do roba. Nov drsnik »Frame along the edge« ob robu ohrani trden okvir, če ga želiš, mreža pa je položena tako, da ostane čim manj tankih pasov.
- **Vzorec lukenj z zgornjo plastjo v drugi barvi**
  Pri vzorcu lukenj je lahko zgornja plast mreža v drugi barvi, v luknjah pa je barva telesa. Ploskev tako ostane ravna, obe barvi se začneta na prvih plasteh, brez podpor. 3MF ju vsebuje kot ločeni telesi.
- **Barva vsakega dela v pregledovalniku**
  Pri skupini ali snopu pod Multicolor pregledovalnik zdaj našteje dele, vsakega z barvnim kvadratkom. Tako lahko prebarvaš en del, ne da bi odprl skupino, mreža dvobarvnega vzorca lukenj pa ne izgine več v barvo podlage.
- **Pokrov s tečajem: filament se laže vstavi**
  Pri pokrovu s tečajem je bil zatič iz kosa filamenta pogosto pretesen v luknjah telesa ali sploh ni šel noter. Luknje so zdaj nekoliko večje: 0,1 mm na stran s privzetimi nastavitvami namesto 0,05 mm. Če tvoj tiskalnik potrebuje več prostora, luknja sledi toleranci iz preizkusa ujemanja. Zatič še vedno sedi tesneje v telesu kot v pokrovu, da ne zdrsne ven.
- **Luknje za 19″ nosilec**
  Montažne luknje zdaj poleg »Screws« ponujajo »19″ rack«: štiri reže v kotih sprednje plošče, natanko tam, kjer imajo luknje tirnice nosilca. Višina se prilagodi plošči ali sledi standardni plošči od 1U do 6U. Vrstica pokaže, kje so luknje nastale.
- **Shrani samo izbor kot LYL**
  V oknu za izvoz lahko datoteka LYL zdaj vsebuje samo izbrane predmete, kot nov načrt brez zgodovine, opomb in prilog. Tvoj načrt ostane vezan na svojo datoteko. Prav pride za dele, ki jih hraniš v knjižnici in jih vrneš z Insert from design.
