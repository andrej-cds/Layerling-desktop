## Layerling Desktop 1.84.1

- **"Back up all" no longer claims it saved when you cancel.** If you cancel the "Save as" window, the green "Backed up … designs in …zip" notice is now removed, because nothing was saved.

Based on the original layerling 1.84.0.

---

## Kaj je novega v 1.84.1

- **»Varnostna kopija vsega« ne trdi več, da je shranila, ko prekličeš.** Če prekličeš okno »Shrani kot«, obvestilo »Backed up … designs in …zip« zdaj izgine, ker ni bilo shranjeno nič.
