## What's new in 1.125.0

- **Screws with Torx, Phillips or no drive**
  A screw now takes the drive you want: hex socket, Torx, Phillips or none. The drive's size and depth and the head diameter can be set, so a flat head gets a usable drive too.
- **Openings for USB, HDMI, DisplayPort and RJ45**
  The new shape "Port opening" puts the hole for a socket into a box wall: USB-C, Micro-USB, USB-A, HDMI, Mini HDMI, DisplayPort, Mini DisplayPort or RJ45. Pick the socket, move it onto the wall, group. The sizes come from data sheets and standards, with a little room for printing, and HDMI and DisplayPort get their slanted corners.
- **Offset works on every letter**
  Texts with an A, B, M, N, W or 9 could not be offset. Now their letters grow or shrink along their outlines, also as a hole with clearance and when cutting out.
- **The Hirth joint stays round**
  Instead of a length and a width, the Hirth joint now has one diameter, and every handle changes just that. So its teeth always interlock all round.
- **Tidy buttons under the workspace settings**
  On the Mac, the labels of the two buttons at the foot of the workspace settings ran out over their edges. Now each label stays on one line, and where it is too tight the buttons move below the hint.
- **Sort the object list by name**
  The A–Z button in the object list's title bar puts the shapes you have named at the top in alphabetical order, Part 2 before Part 10. The others stay below in the order you added them, the newest last.
- **Chamfers, fillets and STEP for the hinge with cones**
  Chamfers, fillets and "Delete face" now work on the hinge with cones too. The STEP export, which used to leave it out, now takes it with its true cones.
- **Sketch shapes in your default size**
  The rectangle, circle and ellipse in a sketch now start as large as you set the box and the cylinder in the shape defaults, instead of always at 20 mm.

## Kaj je novega v 1.125.0

- **Vijaki s Torxom, Phillipsom ali brez glave**
  Vijak zdaj dobi pogon, kakršnega želiš: šesterorob, Torx, Phillips ali brez. Velikost in globino pogona ter premer glave lahko nastaviš, tako da dobi uporaben pogon tudi ploščata glava.
- **Odprtine za USB, HDMI, DisplayPort in RJ45**
  Nova oblika »Port opening« naredi luknjo za vtičnico v steno škatle: USB-C, Micro-USB, USB-A, HDMI, Mini HDMI, DisplayPort, Mini DisplayPort ali RJ45. Izberi vtičnico, jo premakni na steno in združi. Mere so iz podatkovnih listov in standardov, z malo prostora za tiskanje, HDMI in DisplayPort pa dobita poševne vogale.
- **Odmik deluje pri vseh črkah**
  Besedil s črkami A, B, M, N, W ali 9 ni bilo mogoče odmakniti. Zdaj se njihove črke povečajo ali skrčijo vzdolž obrisov, tudi kot luknja z zračnostjo in pri izrezovanju.
- **Hirthov spoj ostane okrogel**
  Namesto dolžine in širine ima Hirthov spoj zdaj en premer, vsak ročaj pa spreminja samo tega. Zobje se tako vedno ujemajo po vsem obodu.
- **Urejeni gumbi pod nastavitvami delovne površine**
  Na Macu so se napisi dveh gumbov na dnu nastavitev delovne površine razlezli čez rob. Zdaj ostane vsak napis v eni vrstici, kjer je premalo prostora, pa se gumba premakneta pod namig.
- **Razvrsti seznam objektov po imenu**
  Gumb A–Z v naslovni vrstici seznama objektov postavi poimenovane oblike na vrh po abecedi, Part 2 pred Part 10. Ostale ostanejo spodaj v vrstnem redu dodajanja, najnovejša zadnja.
- **Posnetja, zaobljenja in STEP za tečaj s stožci**
  Posnetja, zaobljenja in »Delete face« zdaj delujejo tudi pri tečaju s stožci. Izvoz STEP, ki ga je doslej izpustil, ga zdaj zajame z dejanskimi stožci.
- **Skicirne oblike v tvoji privzeti velikosti**
  Pravokotnik, krog in elipsa v skici zdaj začnejo tako veliki, kot si nastavil škatlo in valj v privzetih merah oblik, ne več vedno pri 20 mm.
