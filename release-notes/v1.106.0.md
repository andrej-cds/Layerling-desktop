## What's new in 1.106.0

- **Align faces: holes on one axis**
  Align faces now has "Same axis": click the round inside of a hole or the outside of a pin, then a round face of the other part, and the part lands right on its axis. Hole over hole, pin into hole, on imported meshes too.
- **Show or hide notes with Shift+N**
  Shift+N shows or hides the notes and reference points, in the 3D editor and in a sketch, like "Show notes and points" in the Visibility menu. N alone still places a note.
- **Reference points snap while you drag them**
  Drag a reference point and it snaps to the corners, the middles of the edges and faces of bodies, and to other points. Hold Alt to let it go free.
- **The ruler measures when stood up**
  Stand the ruler up or tip it and it measures along its own direction: upright beside a body it shows the body's height instead of measuring along the plate.
- **The arrow keys change a number**
  In a number field of the properties you have clicked, the Up and Down keys now change the value by one step, with Shift a larger step and with Alt a finer one, as the mouse wheel already did. Until now the keys only moved the text cursor.
- **A chamfer on the Gridfinity lip**
  The sharp top of a Gridfinity bin's stacking lip can now be chamfered on the bin itself: set "Lip chamfer" in the Properties panel, up to 1 mm. It is the same chamfer the edge tool makes, without picking the edges.
- **Centring on a face**
  With the workplane on a face, "Center on workplane" now centres on the point you picked with W and moves only along that face. Until now it centred on the base plate, and a part on a sloped face slid off it.

## Kaj je novega v 1.106.0

- **Poravnava ploskev: luknje na isti osi**
  Poravnava ploskev ima zdaj »Ista os«: klikni okroglo notranjost luknje ali zunanjost čepa, nato okroglo ploskev drugega dela in del pristane natanko na njeni osi. Luknja nad luknjo, čep v luknjo, tudi na uvoženih mrežah.
- **Opombe pokaži ali skrij s Shift+N**
  Shift+N pokaže ali skrije opombe in referenčne točke, v 3D urejevalniku in v skici, tako kot »Pokaži opombe in točke« v meniju Prikaz. Samo N še vedno postavi opombo.
- **Referenčne točke se med vlečenjem pripnejo**
  Povleci referenčno točko in se pripne na oglišča, sredine robov in ploskev teles ter na druge točke. Z Alt jo pustiš prosto.
- **Ravnilo meri tudi postavljeno pokonci**
  Ravnilo postavi pokonci ali ga nagni in meri v svoji smeri: pokonci ob telesu pokaže višino telesa, namesto da bi merilo vzdolž plošče.
- **Puščične tipke spreminjajo število**
  V številčnem polju lastnosti, v katero si kliknil, tipki gor in dol zdaj spremenita vrednost za en korak, s Shift za večji in z Alt za manjši korak, kot je to že znalo kolesce miške. Doslej sta tipki le premikali kazalko v besedilu.
- **Posnetje na obrobi Gridfinity**
  Ostri zgornji rob obrobe za zlaganje posode Gridfinity je zdaj mogoče posneti kar na posodi: nastavi »Posnetje obrobe« v podoknu Lastnosti, do 1 mm. To je isto posnetje kot pri orodju za robove, brez izbiranja robov.
- **Centriranje na ploskvi**
  Ko je delovna ravnina na ploskvi, »Centriraj na delovni ravnini« zdaj centrira na točko, ki si jo izbral z W, in se premika samo po tej ploskvi. Doslej je centriral na osnovno ploščo in del na nagnjeni ploskvi je zdrsnil z nje.
