## What's new in 1.146.1

- **A surface texture's pattern as a part of its own**
  For a print in two colours, "Pattern as its own part" in the Surface texture card makes the pattern a part of its own, with any pattern, and "Colour of the pattern" gives it its colour. Raised, the part is what stands out; cut in, it fills the cut up to the surface. Body and pattern stay together in a bundle and reach the slicer as two parts in a 3MF.
- **Gridfinity letters in a colour of their own**
  The letters on a Gridfinity bin's label shelves can now have a colour of their own: pick it under "Letter colour" below the font. A 3MF keeps the bin one body with the letters' faces in that colour, so Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer 2.9 and the slicers built on them print them with a filament of their own, without a colour change.
- **Fonts of your own and one letter size on Gridfinity labels**
  The letters on a Gridfinity bin's label shelves can now be in a font of your own, picked under "Font" as for a text. The design keeps the outlines of the letters it uses, so it opens with the same letters on another computer. "Letter size" makes the capitals the same height on every shelf that has room for them, so labels of different lengths match.
- **Bodies of several colours in OrcaSlicer and PrusaSlicer**
  A body of several colours, such as a group with Multicolor on, came into OrcaSlicer and the slicers built on it in one colour. The 3MF export now also paints its faces the way these slicers do themselves, so every colour gets a filament of its own; PrusaSlicer 2.9 reads it too.
- **Lock a shape from the object list**
  The shield in the object list now locks a shape as well as unlocking it: it shows on the selected row and under the mouse, and a click locks or unlocks. The arrow keys on a clicked grip now move only the entry in the list, no longer the part or the view as well.

## Kaj je novega v 1.146.1

- **Vzorec površinske teksture kot svoj del**
  Za tisk v dveh barvah Pattern as its own part v kartici Surface texture naredi vzorec svoj del, s katerim koli vzorcem, Colour of the pattern pa mu da barvo. Dvignjen je del tisto, kar štrli; vtisnjen zapolni vrez do površine. Telo in vzorec ostaneta skupaj v svežnju in do slicerja v 3MF pridejo kot dva dela.
- **Črke Gridfinity v svoji barvi**
  Črke na policah za nalepke Gridfinity zabojčka lahko zdaj dobijo svojo barvo: izberi jo pod Letter colour pod pisavo. 3MF obdrži zabojček kot eno telo s ploskvami črk v tej barvi, zato jih Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer 2.9 in slicerji, zgrajeni nanje, natisnejo s svojim filamentom brez menjave barve.
- **Lastne pisave in ena velikost črk na nalepkah Gridfinity**
  Črke na policah za nalepke Gridfinity zabojčka so zdaj lahko v tvoji pisavi, izbrani pod Font kot pri besedilu. Načrt obdrži obrise črk, ki jih uporablja, zato se na drugem računalniku odpre z istimi črkami. Letter size naredi velike črke enako visoke na vsaki polici, ki ima zanje prostor, tako da se nalepke različnih dolžin ujemajo.
- **Večbarvna telesa v OrcaSlicer in PrusaSlicer**
  Večbarvno telo, na primer skupina z vklopljenim Multicolor, je v OrcaSlicer in slicerje nanj prišlo v eni barvi. Izvoz 3MF zdaj tudi pobarva ploskve tako, kot to naredijo ti slicerji sami, zato vsaka barva dobi svoj filament; PrusaSlicer 2.9 to prav tako prebere.
- **Zakleni obliko iz seznama objektov**
  Ščit v seznamu objektov zdaj obliko tudi zaklene in odklene: pokaže se na izbrani vrstici in pod miško, klik pa zaklene ali odklene. Puščične tipke na kliknjenem ročaju zdaj premaknejo samo vnos v seznamu, ne več tudi dela ali pogleda.
