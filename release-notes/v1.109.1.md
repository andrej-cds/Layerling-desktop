## What's new in 1.109.1

Only the desktop part changes in this release (layerling stays at 1.109.0).

- **Open in Fusion: the model stays in front after Fusion starts**
  When Fusion was not running, the model was imported while Fusion was still starting, and Fusion then showed its Home screen over it. Now the import waits until Fusion has finished loading, and the model is brought to the front once more a few seconds later.

---

## Kaj je novega v 1.109.1

V tej izdaji se spremeni samo namizni del (layerling ostaja pri 1.109.0).

- **Odpiranje v Fusionu: model ostane v ospredju tudi po zagonu**
  Če Fusion ni tekel, se je model uvozil, ko se je Fusion še zaganjal, nato pa je Fusion čez pokazal domači zaslon. Zdaj uvoz počaka, da se Fusion konča nalagati, model pa se čez nekaj sekund še enkrat postavi v ospredje.
