## What's new in 1.76.1

- **OBJ files with a colour gradient open**
  An OBJ file with its own colour at every point, like scans and AI-made models (from Tripo, for example), became one body per colour, often hundreds of thousands of them. The editor crashed on that. Such files now come in as one body in a single colour, with a note that the colour gradient is not kept. Files with just a few colours stay one body per colour, as before.
- **Drag a handle past the other side**
  Drag a side or corner handle of a shape or a selection, or the arrow on top, past the opposite side and the size no longer stops there: the shape keeps growing on the other side of that edge, as in Tinkercad. A selection is mirrored as it goes. A body made of a mesh still stops at the smallest height.
- **Easier on a tablet**
  One finger on empty space now turns the view, and a tap there clears the selection, so the turn switch is not needed for it; the switch also shows on an iPad that does not report itself as a touch device. The section view has "Confirm" and "Back" buttons, because a tablet has no Enter or Esc. A finger on a shape or a handle works as before.

## Kaj je novega v 1.76.1

- **Datoteke OBJ z barvnim prehodom se odprejo**
  Datoteka OBJ z lastno barvo v vsaki točki, kot jo imajo skeniranja in modeli, narejeni z umetno inteligenco (na primer iz Tripa), je postala eno telo na barvo, pogosto na stotisoče teles. Urejevalnik se je ob tem sesul. Take datoteke zdaj pridejo kot eno telo v eni barvi, z opombo, da barvni prehod ni ohranjen. Datoteke z le nekaj barvami ostanejo eno telo na barvo, kot doslej.
- **Povleci ročaj čez drugo stran**
  Povleci stranski ali kotni ročaj oblike ali izbora, ali puščico na vrhu, čez nasprotno stran in velikost se tam ne ustavi več: oblika raste naprej na drugi strani tistega roba, kot v Tinkercadu. Izbor se pri tem prezrcali. Telo iz mreže se še vedno ustavi pri najmanjši višini.
- **Lažje na tabličnem računalniku**
  En prst na praznem prostoru zdaj vrti pogled, dotik tam pa počisti izbor, zato stikalo za vrtenje za to ni več potrebno; stikalo se pokaže tudi na iPadu, ki se ne javi kot naprava na dotik. Prerez ima gumba »Potrdi« in »Nazaj«, ker tablični računalnik nima tipk Enter ali Esc. Prst na obliki ali ročaju deluje kot doslej.
