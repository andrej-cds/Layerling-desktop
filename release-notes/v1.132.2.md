## What's new in 1.132.2

- **Punch an outline through a body**
  Combine now has Punch: pick a body as the punch first, then the body that gets the opening. The punch's outline goes straight through along X, Y or Z, even when the punch only stands in front of it. The punch stays, so you can move or turn it and punch again right away.
- **The sketch plate grows with the sketch**
  A revolve profile can now go past the edge of the plate, for a bigger radius, without making the workplane bigger in the settings. The grid grows with it, points out there can be picked again, and the minus button zooms out until all of it fits.
- **Chamfers across a half-chamfered corner**
  When a chamfer or fillet fails, layerling tries once more with the short edges at the ends of the picked ones. A corner where an earlier chamfer meets a wall that is still sharp now goes through in one step; a message says how many edges were taken along.
- **A collar on a nut**
  Like the screw, a nut can now take a round collar: Collar height and Collar diameter are in the settings of the hex, square, flange, wing and star knob nuts. The collar stands on top of the nut, narrower than it, with the thread running through; the nut still prints on its bearing face, the collar without supports. The height counts the nut itself only.
- **Teapot lid: the joint as a height from the bottom**
  On the vase's teapot lid the joint can now be typed in as a height from the bottom, in the new field "Body up to · lid from" beside Lid height. The two add up to the vase's height: type one and the other takes the rest.

## Kaj je novega v 1.132.2

- **Preluknaj telo z obrisom**
  Combine ima zdaj Punch: najprej izberi telo, ki bo preluknalo, nato telo, ki dobi odprtino. Obris luknjača gre naravnost skozi po osi X, Y ali Z, tudi če luknjač stoji samo pred njim. Luknjač ostane, zato ga lahko premakneš ali obrneš in takoj preluknaš znova.
- **Plošča skice raste s skico**
  Profil za vrtenje lahko zdaj sega čez rob plošče, za večji polmer, ne da bi bilo treba v nastavitvah povečati delovne ravnine. Mreža raste z njim, točke zunaj je spet mogoče izbrati, gumb minus pa oddalji pogled, dokler ni vse vidno.
- **Posnetja čez napol posneto vogal**
  Ko posnetje ali zaobljenje ne uspe, layerling poskusi še enkrat s kratkimi robovi na koncih izbranih. Vogal, kjer se prejšnje posnetje sreča s še ostro steno, zdaj gre skozi v enem koraku; sporočilo pove, koliko robov je šlo zraven.
- **Ovratnik na matici**
  Tako kot vijak lahko tudi matica dobi okrogel ovratnik: Collar height in Collar diameter sta v nastavitvah šesterorobe, kvadratne, prirobnične, krilne in zvezdaste matice. Ovratnik stoji na vrhu matice, ožji od nje, navoj teče skozi; matica se še vedno tiska na svoji nalegalni ploskvi, ovratnik brez podpor. Višina šteje samo matico.
- **Pokrov čajnika: spoj kot višina od dna**
  Pri pokrovu čajnika za vazo lahko spoj zdaj vpišeš kot višino od dna, v novem polju »Body up to · lid from« ob višini pokrova. Obe skupaj dasta višino vaze: vpiši eno in druga vzame ostanek.
