## What's new in 1.97.0

- **Save four views of your own**
  Shift+7, 8, 9 or 0 saves the view exactly as it is - angle, position and zoom - and the key alone brings it back. Each design keeps its own four views, and a saved .lyl file carries them along.
- **Esc puts the tool down in a sketch**
  As in the 3D editor, Esc ends what you are drawing and switches back to Select. A second Esc clears the selection.
- **Take a sketch apart by its outlines**
  Separate Parts now takes a sketch body apart into one body per outline, overlapping outlines too. Each part keeps its height, colour and place and has its own sketch with the construction lines, so you can raise or lower the parts one by one and join or cut them afterwards.
- **layerling speaks Brazilian Portuguese**
  The whole interface is now in Brazilian Portuguese; pick it in the language menu. A browser that asks for "pt-BR" gets it on its own. The guide chapters will follow and show in English until then.
- **Move the key ring tab along its side**
  Name tags and icon tags have a Position along side slider under the key ring hole. It moves the tab up or down, or left or right along the top. When the middle of the side meets a gap, as beside a J, put the tab where the letter reaches out.
- **Big parts follow the mouse**
  A body you take hold of high up now stays right under the pointer while you drag it. A tall or tilted body in a design bigger than the plate used to stop on one side, and only the arrow keys moved it further.
- **Trackpad zoom in Chrome**
  Spreading two fingers on the trackpad zooms in full steps again in Chrome, Edge and other Chrome-based browsers, on a Mac as on Linux. Before, only a tenth of the movement got through there.
- **Properties grow along**
  The Toolbar size setting (Small, Medium, Large) now also makes the properties panel on the right smaller or larger, not only the ribbon on top and the camera controls on the left.

## Kaj je novega v 1.97.0

- **Shrani štiri lastne poglede**
  Shift+7, 8, 9 ali 0 shrani pogled natanko takšen, kot je: kot, položaj in povečavo. Sama tipka ga nato prikliče. Vsak osnutek hrani svoje štiri poglede, shranjena datoteka .lyl pa jih nosi s seboj.
- **Esc v skici odloži orodje**
  Tako kot v 3D urejevalniku Esc konča, kar rišeš, in preklopi nazaj na Izbiro. Drugi Esc počisti izbor.
- **Skico razstavi po obrisih**
  »Loči dele« zdaj razstavi telo iz skice na eno telo na obris, tudi prekrivajoče se obrise. Vsak del obdrži višino, barvo in mesto ter ima svojo skico s pomožnimi črtami, tako da dele lahko dvigaš ali spuščaš posamezno, nato pa jih združiš ali odrežeš.
- **Layerling govori brazilsko portugalščino**
  Celoten vmesnik je zdaj v brazilski portugalščini; izberi jo v meniju jezikov. Brskalnik, ki zahteva »pt-BR«, jo dobi sam. Poglavja vodnika sledijo, do takrat se prikazujejo v angleščini.
- **Ploščico za obesek premakni vzdolž stranice**
  Imenske ploščice in ploščice s simbolom imajo pod luknjo za obesek drsnik »Položaj vzdolž stranice«. Ploščico premakne gor ali dol oziroma levo ali desno vzdolž vrha. Če sredina stranice zadene na vrzel, kot ob črki J, ploščico postavi tja, kamor črka sega.
- **Velika telesa sledijo miški**
  Telo, ki ga primeš visoko zgoraj, zdaj med vlečenjem ostane tik pod kazalcem. Visoko ali nagnjeno telo v osnutku, večjem od plošče, se je prej ustavilo na eni strani in naprej so ga premikale samo smerne tipke.
- **Povečava s sledilno ploščico v Chromu**
  Razmik dveh prstov na sledilni ploščici spet poveča v polnih korakih v Chromu, Edgeu in drugih brskalnikih na osnovi Chroma, na Macu kot na Linuxu. Prej je tam prišla skozi samo desetina gibanja.
- **Lastnosti rastejo z njimi**
  Nastavitev velikosti orodne vrstice (Majhna, Srednja, Velika) zdaj spremeni velikost tudi plošče z lastnostmi na desni, ne samo traku na vrhu in kontrol kamere na levi.
