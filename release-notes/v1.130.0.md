## What's new in 1.130.0

- **Counterbores and nut pockets by screw size**
  The counterbore and countersink have a Screw size choice from M2 to M30 (the countersink up to M20): shaft, head diameter and depth or angle follow the standard, and the length stays. With Nut pocket the hexagon becomes the pocket for a hex nut, with a little room for printing.
- **Larger Gridfinity baseplates**
  A baseplate can now be up to 12 × 12 units instead of 6 × 6, for printers with a bed over 256 mm. Switch on "Large plate (up to 12 units)" in the Properties panel. With your printer chosen in the settings, a plate larger than the printer's bed is flagged.
- **Lids with magnets**
  In the Lid tool, "Magnets in the corners" puts a magnet in each corner: pockets in the body and the lid, and where the wall is too thin, a post fills the corner. Pick 6 × 2, 8 × 3 or 10 × 3 mm or your own size, and the play: 0.1 mm to press them in, more to glue them.

## Kaj je novega v 1.130.0

- **Povrtine in žepi za matice po velikosti vijaka**
  Povrtina in stožčasta povrtina imata izbiro Screw size od M2 do M30 (stožčasta do M20): steblo, premer glave ter globina ali kot sledijo standardu, dolžina pa ostane. Z Nut pocket postane šesterokotnik žep za šesterokotno matico, z malo prostora za tisk.
- **Večje osnovne plošče Gridfinity**
  Osnovna plošča je zdaj lahko velika do 12 × 12 enot namesto 6 × 6, za tiskalnike s ploščo nad 256 mm. Vklopi »Large plate (up to 12 units)« v plošči Properties. Če je v nastavitvah izbran tvoj tiskalnik, se plošča, večja od tiskalnikove, označi.
- **Pokrovi z magneti**
  V orodju Lid »Magnets in the corners« vstavi magnet v vsak kot: žepi v telesu in pokrovu, kjer je stena pretanka, pa kot zapolni steber. Izberi 6 × 2, 8 × 3 ali 10 × 3 mm ali svojo velikost in toleranco: 0,1 mm za vtiskanje, več za lepljenje.
