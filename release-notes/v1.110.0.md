## What's new in 1.110.0

- **Everything on the server**
  When new designs start on the server, every way in goes there now: opening or importing a file, restoring a backup, duplicating, a state from the history and New folder. With nothing left in the browser, the start page says so.
- **Open a Gridfinity side partly**
  A Gridfinity bin's open side no longer has to be open in full: in the Properties panel each open side has "Depth", "Width" and "Position" rows for its opening, for a slot to reach into or a lower wall. The corners and the lip beside the opening stay, so bins still stack on it. "Opening corners" and "Opening corner size" round or chamfer the opening's own corners.
- **Curve through points**
  Select three or more points of an outline in a sketch and click "Curve through points" in the bar: one flowing curve runs from the first point to the last, through all the points between, what other programs call "Fit curve" or "Fit point spline". The lines beyond the two ends stay as they are.
- **Hull**
  Hull under Combine wraps the selected shapes in one body, as if shrink-wrapped: a half sphere on a cylinder becomes a dome, four cylinders a box with round corners. The parts stay inside, and Edit group still moves them.
- **Read attachments**
  The eye in the list of attachments opens a .md or .txt file to read without downloading it: Markdown formatted, with headings, lists, tables and code, text exactly as written. Escape or a click beside it closes the window.
- **A copy upwards**
  Drag the black lift arrow with Alt held and the shape stays where it is while a copy lands where you let go. That stacks copies straight up, as Alt already did sideways.
- **Changing text keeps the size**
  When you type another text, the letter size now stays the same and the box follows the letters. Before, the letters were stretched into the old box: a single "a" grew and the width was wrong.
- **A bundle with wrapped text is no longer stretched**
  A bundle (or a group) holding text wrapped round a cylinder was measured as big as the whole cylinder and pulled the parts inside it wider and oval. Its size now follows the parts themselves: nothing is distorted, and the bundle sits in their middle.
- **Mac: Control and drag turns the view again**
  On a Mac the view turns again when you hold Control and drag on the trackpad (a Control click is the right click there). Cmd and drag still moves the view.

## Kaj je novega v 1.110.0

- **Vse na strežniku**
  Ko novi projekti nastajajo na strežniku, vodijo tja zdaj vse poti: odpiranje ali uvoz datoteke, obnova varnostne kopije, podvajanje, stanje iz zgodovine in Nova mapa. Ko v brskalniku ne ostane nič, to pove začetna stran.
- **Stran posode Gridfinity odpri le delno**
  Odprta stran posode Gridfinity ni več nujno odprta v celoti: v podoknu Lastnosti ima vsaka odprta stran vrstice »Globina«, »Širina« in »Položaj« za svojo odprtino, za zarezo, v katero seže prst, ali nižjo steno. Oglišča in obroba ob odprtini ostanejo, zato se posode še vedno zlagajo. »Oglišča odprtine« in »Velikost oglišč odprtine« zaobljijo ali posnamejo oglišča odprtine.
- **Krivulja skozi točke**
  Izberi tri ali več točk obrisa v skici in v vrstici klikni »Krivulja skozi točke«: ena tekoča krivulja teče od prve do zadnje točke skozi vse vmesne, kar drugi programi imenujejo »Fit curve« ali »Fit point spline«. Črte onkraj obeh koncev ostanejo, kot so.
- **Ovojnica**
  Ovojnica v razdelku Združevanje ovije izbrane oblike v eno telo, kot da bi jih skrčil v folijo: polkrogla na valju postane kupola, štirje valji kvader z zaobljenimi oglišči. Deli ostanejo znotraj, Uredi skupino pa jih še vedno premika.
- **Branje prilog**
  Oko na seznamu prilog odpre datoteko .md ali .txt za branje brez prenosa: Markdown oblikovan, z naslovi, seznami, tabelami in kodo, besedilo natanko, kot je napisano. Esc ali klik ob oknu ga zapre.
- **Kopija navzgor**
  Povleci črno puščico za dvig z držanim Alt in oblika ostane, kjer je, kopija pa pristane tam, kjer spustiš. Tako zlagaš kopije naravnost navzgor, kot je Alt že omogočal vstran.
- **Sprememba besedila ohrani velikost**
  Ko vtipkaš drugo besedilo, velikost črk ostane enaka in okvir sledi črkam. Prej so se črke raztegnile v star okvir: samotni »a« je zrasel, širina pa je bila napačna.
- **Sveženj z ovitim besedilom se ne razteguje več**
  Sveženj (ali skupina) z besedilom, ovitim okoli valja, je bil izmerjen tako velik kot ves valj in je dele v njem razvlekel in naredil ovalne. Velikost zdaj sledi samim delom: nič se ne popači, sveženj pa leži v njihovi sredini.
- **Mac: Control in vlečenje spet vrti pogled**
  Na Macu se pogled spet vrti, ko držiš Control in vlečeš po sledilni ploščici (Control klik je tam desni klik). Cmd in vlečenje pogled še vedno premika.
