## What's new in 1.73.0

- **The tape measure snaps to reference points**
  Mark the centre of a circle or the middle of a line as a reference point (the crosshair button under a measurement, or the right-click menu) and then click that point with the tape measure: you measure from there. The tape measure snaps to reference points like to corners.
- **Touch screens: a finger reaches further**
  On a touch screen a finger now hits small targets more easily. In the sketch, a press beside a point, a handle or a thin line goes to that part. The tape measure, the section measure and the edges of the chamfer and fillet tools catch a finger from a larger distance. A mouse and a stylus keep the reach they had. On a tablet a stylus remains the more precise choice.
- **Detailed SVG files import again**
  An SVG with one very detailed outline, such as a traced drawing, stopped with "Maximum call stack size exceeded" and did not come in. Building the body handed all its triangles over in one call, which has a size limit that thousands of points overrun. They are now added one at a time, and the file imports.
- **Corner ruler: no more fan of lines**
  With the camera hovering close above the corner ruler, the part of an arm behind the camera was drawn mirrored and made a fan of fine lines across the picture. The ruler's lines, ticks, numbers and distance arrows are now cut off at the camera, and only what is in front of it is drawn.

## Kaj je novega v 1.73.0

- **Merilni trak se zaskoči na referenčne točke**
  Sredino kroga ali sredino črte označi kot referenčno točko (gumb s križcem pod meritvijo ali meni z desnim klikom), nato to točko klikni z merilnim trakom: meriš od tam. Merilni trak se zaskoči na referenčne točke kakor na oglišča.
- **Na dotik: prst seže dlje**
  Na zaslonu na dotik prst zdaj lažje zadene majhne cilje. V skici pritisk ob točki, ročaju ali tanki črti gre na ta del. Merilni trak, merjenje preseka in robovi pri orodjih za posnetje in zaokrožitev ujamejo prst z večje razdalje. Miška in pisalo obdržita dosedanji doseg. Na tabličnem računalniku ostane pisalo natančnejša izbira.
- **Podrobne datoteke SVG se spet uvozijo**
  SVG z zelo podrobnim obrisom, na primer sledena risba, se je ustavil z napako »Maximum call stack size exceeded« in se ni naložil. Pri gradnji telesa so bili vsi trikotniki predani v enem klicu, ta pa ima omejitev velikosti, ki jo tisoči točk presežejo. Zdaj se dodajajo posamično in datoteka se uvozi.
- **Kotni ravnilnik: ni več šopa črt**
  Ko kamera lebdi tik nad kotnim ravnilnikom, se je del kraka za kamero narisal zrcaljeno in naredil šop tankih črt čez sliko. Črte, delilne črtice, številke in puščice mer ravnilnika se zdaj odrežejo pri kameri, nariše se samo tisto, kar je pred njo.
