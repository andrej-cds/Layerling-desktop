## What's new in 1.80.0

- **Push a face out or in**
  Making a wall thicker or cutting a pocket into a finished part took a workplane, a traced sketch and a group, and on an imported STL there was no other way. The new tool "Face" next to the hole pattern adds material on a flat face or cuts a pocket into it, set in from the edge and with rounded corners if you like. What it adds stays an ordinary sketch body in a group.
- **Restoring a backup twice no longer doubles your designs**
  If you restore a backup into a browser that already has one of its designs, that one is skipped, and the notice says how many were already there. The tooltip of "Back up all" now also names the button that brings a backup back.
- **Undo steps survive a reload**
  If the last step was made with the workplane on a face, the whole undo history was lost when the design was loaded again, for instance after an update with Save and reload. The design came back complete, but Undo, Redo and the history view were greyed out. That is fixed, the history stays.
- **Danish as a language**
  layerling now speaks Danish (Dansk): the whole interface and the 16 chapters of the guide. The language is chosen automatically for a browser set to Danish and can be changed in the language list. It was translated with AI help and read through by a Danish speaker; wrong words are fixed with a small pull request on GitHub.

## Kaj je novega v 1.80.0

- **Potisni ploskev ven ali noter**
  Odebelitev stene ali izrez žepa v dokončan kos je zahteval delovno ravnino, obrisano skico in skupino, pri uvoženem STL pa druge poti ni bilo. Novo orodje »Ploskev« ob vzorcu lukenj doda material na ravni ploskvi ali vanjo izreže žep, zamaknjen od roba in po želji z zaobljenimi vogali. Kar doda, ostane navadno telo skice v skupini.
- **Dvakratna obnovitev varnostne kopije ne podvoji načrtov**
  Če varnostno kopijo obnoviš v brskalnik, ki že ima enega od njenih načrtov, se ta preskoči, obvestilo pa pove, koliko jih je že bilo. Namig pri »Varnostna kopija vsega« zdaj imenuje tudi gumb, ki kopijo vrne nazaj.
- **Koraki razveljavitve preživijo ponovno nalaganje**
  Če je bil zadnji korak narejen z delovno ravnino na ploskvi, se je ob ponovnem nalaganju načrta, na primer po posodobitvi z »Shrani in znova naloži«, izgubila vsa zgodovina razveljavitve. Načrt se je vrnil v celoti, vendar so bili Razveljavi, Uveljavi in pogled zgodovine zatemnjeni. To je popravljeno, zgodovina ostane.
- **Danščina kot jezik**
  layerling zdaj govori danski (Dansk): ves vmesnik in 16 poglavij navodil. Jezik se izbere samodejno za brskalnik, nastavljen na danščino, spremeniš pa ga lahko na seznamu jezikov. Prevedel ga je pomočnik z umetno inteligenco, prebral pa ga je govorec danščine; napačne besede se popravijo z majhnim pull requestom na GitHubu.
