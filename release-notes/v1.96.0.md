## What's new in 1.96.0

- **Turn a sketch into a revolve**
  The badge at the top of a sketch is now a choice: switch a sketch to "Revolve sketch" there, also the sketch of an existing body. An imported SVG becomes a vase this way, without tracing it.
- **A copy set to hole cancels the part**
  Make a copy of an imported part a hole and group the two, and the part is now gone entirely. On large SVGs thin strips of wall used to stay standing.
- **Set down on a face works on a tablet**
  After a long press on a body it sometimes stayed where it was when set down, although the message came, and only Undo showed it at its place. Now it lands on the face you tap straight away.
- **Metric threads up to M24**
  The size list of the thread now goes past M12: M14, M16, M18, M20, M22 and M24, each with its standard pitch and standard head and nut sizes.
- **Show holes more clearly**
  Under Settings, Appearance, holes can now be shown darker, less see-through and with a line round them. That way you find a hole even among many see-through bodies.

## Kaj je novega v 1.96.0

- **Skico pretvori v skico za vrtenje**
  Značka na vrhu skice je zdaj izbira: tam preklopi skico na »Skica za vrtenje«, tudi skico obstoječega telesa. Uvožen SVG tako postane vaza, ne da bi ga bilo treba risati po sledeh.
- **Kopija kot luknja odstrani del**
  Naredi kopijo uvoženega dela luknjo in obe združi, pa je del zdaj popolnoma izginil. Pri velikih SVG so prej ostali tanki trakovi stene.
- **Postavi na ploskev deluje na tablici**
  Po dolgem pritisku na telo je včasih po postavitvi ostalo na mestu, čeprav je prišlo sporočilo, na svojem mestu ga je pokazala šele razveljavitev. Zdaj pristane takoj na ploskvi, ki se je dotakneš.
- **Metrični navoji do M24**
  Seznam velikosti navoja zdaj sega čez M12: M14, M16, M18, M20, M22 in M24, vsak s standardnim korakom ter standardnima velikostma glave in matice.
- **Jasnejši prikaz lukenj**
  V nastavitvah pod videzom lahko luknje zdaj prikažeš temnejše, manj prosojne in z obrobo. Tako luknjo najdeš tudi med mnogimi prosojnimi telesi.
