## What's new in 1.177.0

- **New shape: Cove**
  The basic shapes now have the Cove, right after the Wedge: a strip for an inside corner. Two flat sides lie against the walls, and the side between them is hollow.

With width and height equal the hollow is a quarter circle that runs into both walls smoothly. "Radius of the hollow" makes it flatter. "Edge thickness" cuts the two thin ends square, so they can be printed.

It is an exact body: chamfers, fillets and the STEP export work on it.
- **Change a chamfer or fillet: the newer ones stay**
  In the Chamfer and Fillet window, "Undo an edge feature" has an "Edit" beside each entry: it opens the tool with the same edges and the old size. Until now every newer chamfer and fillet was lost with it.

Now they stay: once you apply the changed size, the newer ones are laid again on their edges, in one step. A 3 mm fillet becomes a 6 mm one, and the chamfer on top of it is still there.

– A newer one that does not find its edges again, because the new size took them, is left out, and the message says so.
– If a hollowing, an offset, deleted faces or a texture lies on top, the newer ones go with it as before.
- **Edit sketch: chamfers and fillets stay**
  Opening the sketch of a body again and finishing it builds the body anew. Until now its chamfers and fillets were lost with that.

Now they are laid again on the new body, in the same step: move a point, make a side longer, pull a curve, and the edges are chamfered and filleted again afterwards.

– If the new body has a corner more or less, they are left out, and the message says so.
– A hollowing, an offset or deleted faces go as before.
– Undo brings everything back.
- **Print latch clips on the lid**
  A lid with latch clips has a new option, “Print the clips on the lid”. Each clip is made right on its pin at the edge of the lid, with room to turn: lid and clips print in one go.

Swing the clips outward before putting the lid on. The option is off to start with.
- **Compartment sizes in inches**
  With the workspace set to inches, “Change compartments” now shows inches too: in the panel, on the box and in the hints below.
- **Change a hole circle, keep the holes**
  When holes of one size in a group lie evenly on a circle, such as the bolt holes of a flange, the group now has a "Hole circle" slider.

It moves the holes towards the middle or away from it: the circle gets the new diameter and the holes keep their size. An arc needs at least four holes; grids of holes get no slider.
- **Dovetail finds its direction and size**
  On a narrow cut face, such as a beam, the dovetail often did not fit until you made it smaller and turned it by hand.

Now at direction 0 it runs lengthwise where it fits, and across where it does not. With the count at 0, a tongue too wide or too deep is made smaller until it fits. The panel says what it did.

The preview shows each tongue as a see-through body, and arrows show the way the halves slide together.
- **Paste onto the plate you are on**
  A copied part now lands on the active build plate when you paste it, at the same spot as on its old plate, in another design too.
- **SVG and picture: size by height, and kept for the next imports**
  In the Import window there are two new ticks under the field "SVG and picture: width in mm".

– "The number is the height": the drawing comes in this high instead of this wide.
– "Keep for the next imports": the number stays in the field after an import, also the next time you open layerling. So many drawings in a row come in at the same size without typing it each time.

Without the second tick the size counts for the next import only, as before.
- **Ctrl+S saves large designs too**
  With a large design, saving to a file stopped with "too big to save", although the backup could save it.

Now, as in the backup, only the newest history steps that fit go into the file, and the message says how many. The design itself is always saved whole.
- **STEP: a name tag arrives whole**
  A name tag exported as STEP arrived in the slicer with its middle layer almost empty and hundreds of open edges. Every widened text with round corners was affected.

The walls of these layers are now all in the file, and each layer is a closed body.

## Kaj je novega v 1.177.0

- **Nova oblika: Kotni žleb (Cove)**
  Med osnovnimi oblikami je za Klinom zdaj Cove: trak za notranji kot. Dve ravni strani ležita ob stenah, stran med njima je izbočena navznoter (votla).

Če sta širina in višina enaki, je vdolbina četrtina kroga, ki gladko preide v obe steni. »Radius of the hollow« jo naredi položnejšo. »Edge thickness« odreže tanka konca pravokotno, da ju je mogoče natisniti.

Telo je natančno: posnetja, zaokrožitve in izvoz STEP delujejo nanj.
- **Sprememba posnetja ali zaokrožitve: novejše ostanejo**
  V oknu Chamfer and Fillet ima »Undo an edge feature« ob vsakem vnosu gumb »Edit«: odpre orodje z istimi robovi in staro velikostjo. Doslej so se z njim izgubila vsa novejša posnetja in zaokrožitve.

Zdaj ostanejo: ko uveljaviš spremenjeno velikost, se novejša znova nanesejo na svoje robove, v enem koraku. Zaokrožitev 3 mm postane 6 mm, posnetje na njej pa je še vedno tam.

– Novejše, ki svojih robov ne najde več, ker jih je vzela nova velikost, se izpusti in sporočilo to pove.
– Če je nad njimi votlenje, odmik, izbrisane ploskve ali tekstura, gredo novejše z njimi kot doslej.
- **Urejanje skice: posnetja in zaokrožitve ostanejo**
  Ko ponovno odpreš skico telesa in jo končaš, se telo zgradi znova. Doslej so se pri tem izgubila njegova posnetja in zaokrožitve.

Zdaj se nanesejo na novo telo v istem koraku: premakni točko, podaljšaj stranico, povleci krivuljo, robovi pa so potem spet posneti in zaokroženi.

– Če ima novo telo en vogal več ali manj, se izpustijo in sporočilo to pove.
– Votlenje, odmik ali izbrisane ploskve gredo kot doslej.
– Undo vse vrne.
- **Zapaha na pokrovu natisnjena na pokrov**
  Pokrov z zapahi ima novo možnost »Print the clips on the lid«. Vsak zapah je narejen kar na svojem čepu na robu pokrova, z zračnostjo za vrtenje: pokrov in zapahi se natisnejo naenkrat.

Zapaha pred namestitvijo pokrova zasuči navzven. Možnost je na začetku izklopljena.
- **Velikosti predalov v palcih**
  Če je delovni prostor nastavljen na palce, »Change compartments« zdaj prikazuje palce tudi: v plošči, na škatli in v namigih spodaj.
- **Spremeni krog lukenj, luknje ostanejo**
  Kadar luknje iste velikosti v skupini ležijo enakomerno na krogu, na primer vijačne luknje prirobnice, ima skupina zdaj drsnik »Hole circle«.

Luknje premika proti sredini ali stran od nje: krog dobi nov premer, luknje obdržijo velikost. Lok potrebuje vsaj štiri luknje; mrežam lukenj drsnik ni dodan.
- **Lastovičji rep sam najde smer in velikost**
  Na ozki prerezani ploskvi, na primer na tramu, se lastovičji rep pogosto ni prilegal, dokler ga nisi pomanjšal in ročno zasukal.

Zdaj teče pri smeri 0 po dolžini, kjer se prilega, in počez, kjer se ne. Pri številu 0 se preširok ali pregloboko zajet jeziček zmanjša, dokler se ne prilega. Plošča pove, kaj je naredila.

Predogled vsak jeziček prikaže kot prosojno telo, puščice pa kažejo smer, v kateri se polovici zdrsneta skupaj.
- **Prilepi na ploščo, na kateri si**
  Kopiran del se ob lepljenju zdaj znajde na dejavni gradbeni plošči, na istem mestu kot na stari plošči, tudi v drugem načrtu.
- **SVG in slika: velikost po višini in ohranjeno za naslednji uvoz**
  V oknu za uvoz sta pod poljem »SVG in slika: širina v mm« dve novi kljukici.

– »The number is the height«: risba pride tako visoka namesto tako široka.
– »Keep for the next imports«: število ostane v polju po uvozu, tudi ob naslednjem odprtju layerlinga. Več risb zapored tako pride v enaki velikosti, ne da bi jo vsakič vtipkal.

Brez druge kljukice velja velikost samo za naslednji uvoz, kot doslej.
- **Ctrl+S shrani tudi velike načrte**
  Pri velikem načrtu se je shranjevanje v datoteko ustavilo z »too big to save«, čeprav je varnostna kopija lahko shranila.

Zdaj, tako kot pri varnostni kopiji, gredo v datoteko samo najnovejši koraki zgodovine, ki gredo noter, in sporočilo pove, koliko jih je. Sam načrt se vedno shrani v celoti.
- **STEP: imenska oznaka pride cela**
  Imenska oznaka, izvožena kot STEP, je prišla v rezalnik s skoraj praznim srednjim slojem in s sto odprtimi robovi. Prizadet je bil vsak razširjen tekst z zaobljenimi vogali.

Stene teh slojev so zdaj vse v datoteki in vsak sloj je sklenjeno telo.
