## What's new in 1.132.0

- **Fit a baseplate into an outline**
  A Gridfinity baseplate can now fill a drawer that is not a rectangle. Select the plate together with a shape of the drawer's inside and click "Fit baseplate to outline" in the properties panel: whole cells on one grid, placed where it holds the most, at most 6 across and 6 deep (12 with Large plate), the rest solid, so every socket still takes an ordinary bin.
- **Chamfer and fillet keep a bundle's hole open**
  A bundle of a peg and a bigger hole, grouped with a box, cuts the box but not its own peg. With a chamfer or fillet the groove round the peg came back filled in. Now it stays.
- **A collar under the screw head**
  A screw now has Collar height and Collar diameter: a round collar between head and thread, for example a spacer under a star knob. It stays inside the head and prints without supports.
- **Lids are exact bodies: their edges can be rounded**
  A lid for an exact body, such as a rounded box you hollowed, is now an exact CAD body itself. Round or chamfer its edges with the edge tool, and its curves stay round in a STEP export too.
- **Concave fillet: scoop edges out instead of rounding**
  In the fillet window, “Concave (scoop out)” cuts a quarter-round scoop out of each picked straight edge, like the notched corners of a box. Before, that took a cylinder turned into a hole and lined up by hand.
- **Turned shapes stay turned when you drag a handle**
  With the workplane on a face, a turned shape seemed to turn back when you dragged a handle, because the length went into the other side. The handles now follow the shape's turn.
- **Vase: the message shows the way to hollow and lid**
  A vase has too many triangles for Hollow. The message now names the vase's own way: “Hollow with wall” under Build, and a lid on the Accessories card.
- **Sink parts into a face, flush at the top (Shift)**
  Hold Shift when you place a new shape or a part picked up with C on a face and it sinks into the face, upright, with its top flush: good for countersinks and counterbores. Shift+D drops the selection with its top on the workplane. Countersink, counterbore and double countersink now start as a hole.
- **An open sketch survives a reload**
  If you were in the middle of a sketch, with a picture inserted and a line traced over it, a reload took it, an update included. The open sketch is now kept in this browser as you draw and is there again afterwards.
- **Undo in one click, folded too**
  In a narrow window, History folds into one button with the Undo icon. A click on it now takes back your last step; the small arrow under it opens Redo and the history.
- **The section button turns the cut off**
  With its window open, a click on the section view button now turns the cut off too. The X and Esc only hide the window and leave the cut on.
- **Designs with Chamfillet open much faster**
  Chamfillet cut its bodies far finer than needed, up to thirty times the triangles of a fillet of the same size, so a design with many such edges got huge and slow. Chamfillet is now as fine as the other edges, and older designs get light by themselves when you open them.

## Kaj je novega v 1.132.0

- **Prilagodi osnovno ploščo obrisu**
  Osnovna plošča Gridfinity zdaj zapolni predal, ki ni pravokoten. Izberi ploščo skupaj z obliko notranjosti predala in klikni »Fit baseplate to outline« v plošči lastnosti: cele celice na eni mreži, postavljene tja, kjer jih gre največ, največ 6 v širino in 6 v globino (12 z Large plate), ostalo je polno, tako da vsako gnezdo še vedno sprejme navadno posodico.
- **Posnetje in zaobljenje ohranita odprtino snopa**
  Snop moznika in večje luknje, združen s škatlo, izreže škatlo, ne pa svojega moznika. Pri posnetju ali zaobljenju se je žleb okoli moznika vrnil zapolnjen. Zdaj ostane.
- **Ovratnik pod glavo vijaka**
  Vijak ima zdaj Collar height in Collar diameter: okrogel ovratnik med glavo in navojem, na primer distančnik pod zvezdastim gumbom. Ostane znotraj glave in se natisne brez podpor.
- **Pokrovi so točna telesa: robove lahko zaobliš**
  Pokrov za točno telo, na primer zaobljeno škatlo, ki si jo izdolbel, je zdaj sam točno CAD telo. Njegove robove zaobliš ali posname z orodjem za robove, krivine pa ostanejo okrogle tudi v izvozu STEP.
- **Konkavno zaobljenje: robove izdolbi namesto zaoblitve**
  V oknu zaobljenja »Concave (scoop out)« izreže četrtkrožno izdolbino iz vsakega izbranega ravnega roba, kot zarezani koti škatle. Prej je to terjalo valj, spremenjen v luknjo in ročno poravnan.
- **Obrnjene oblike ostanejo obrnjene pri vlečenju ročaja**
  Ko je delovna ravnina na ploskvi, se je obrnjena oblika ob vlečenju ročaja zdela, kot da se obrne nazaj, ker je dolžina šla na drugo stran. Ročaji zdaj sledijo obratu oblike.
- **Vaza: sporočilo pokaže pot do izdolbine in pokrova**
  Vaza ima preveč trikotnikov za Hollow. Sporočilo zdaj navede vazino lastno pot: »Hollow with wall« pod Build in pokrov na kartici Accessories.
- **Potopi dele v ploskev, poravnano na vrhu (Shift)**
  Drži Shift, ko postaviš novo obliko ali del, pobran s C, na ploskev, in se potopi v ploskev, pokonci, z vrhom poravnano: dobro za povrtine in stožčaste povrtine. Shift+D spusti izbor z vrhom na delovni ravnini. Stožčasta povrtina, povrtina in dvojna stožčasta povrtina zdaj začnejo kot luknja.
- **Odprta skica preživi osvežitev**
  Če si bil sredi skice, z vstavljeno sliko in črto, narisano čez njo, je osvežitev, tudi zaradi posodobitve, vse vzela. Odprta skica se zdaj med risanjem hrani v tem brskalniku in je po osvežitvi spet tam.
- **Razveljavi z enim klikom, tudi zložen**
  V ozkem oknu se Zgodovina zloži v en gumb z ikono Undo. Klik nanj zdaj vzame nazaj tvoj zadnji korak; majhna puščica pod njim odpre Redo in zgodovino.
- **Gumb za prerez izklopi rez**
  Ko je okno odprto, klik na gumb pogleda prereza zdaj rez tudi izklopi. X in Esc okno le skrijeta in pustita rez vklopljen.
- **Projekti s Chamfillet se odprejo veliko hitreje**
  Chamfillet je telesa rezal veliko pregosto, do trideset krat toliko trikotnikov kot zaobljenje enake velikosti, zato je bil projekt z veliko takih robov ogromen in počasen. Chamfillet je zdaj enako fin kot drugi robovi, starejši projekti pa se ob odprtju sami olajšajo.
