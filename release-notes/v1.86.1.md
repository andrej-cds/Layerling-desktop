## What's new in 1.86.1

- **A number on the edge of a body lying on its side changes the right measure**
  With a workplane on a face and a body lying on its side (a Round Roof turned by 90°, for example), the number on the vertical edge went into the wrong field: type a new height and another side changed. The number now belongs to the side it stands at, as it already does for a box turned by a quarter.

---

## Kaj je novega v 1.86.1

- **Številka na robu telesa, ki leži na boku, spremeni pravo mero**
  Pri delovni ravnini na ploskvi in telesu, ki leži na boku (na primer Okroglo streho, obrnjeno za 90°), je šla številka na navpičnem robu v napačno polje: vpisal si novo višino in spremenila se je druga stranica. Številka zdaj pripada stranici, ob kateri stoji, kot že pri škatli, obrnjeni za četrtino.
