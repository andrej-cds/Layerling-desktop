## What's new in 1.81.0

- **Restore backup: a button of its own**
  Next to “Back up all” there is a “Restore backup” button now. You pick the backup file, and all designs and custom shapes come back; whatever is already there is skipped. The button is also there on a new computer with no designs yet.
- **Large circles from an SVG are round**
  A large circle or a long curve in an imported SVG used to arrive with visible corners, because every curve was cut into the same few pieces. Now the number of pieces follows the length of the curve: large circles come out round, and small curves stay as light as before. No setting is needed.
- **Edge numbers on turned bodies**
  When a body is turned by 90° and the workplane is on a face, the number on the long edge used to change the other side. Now the number on an edge changes the side it stands at, and the edge that was held stays where it was.
- **Tiny loose pieces stay out of the export**
  A cut and the colour separation of a 3MF export can leave many tiny loose pieces behind, small grains in or on a layer. You do not see them in the editor, but a slicer shows them as spikes, and they can lift a flat design off the plate. STL, 3MF and OBJ now leave them out, and the notice says how many. The body itself and small parts you drew stay.
- **Heights above 220 mm**
  The field “Z (height)” used to jump back at 220 mm, also in the mode “No printer – free size”. Now any number can be typed in, up and down, and the same for X and Y. The slider reaches as far as the plate and the printer height.
- **STL, 3MF and OBJ over the AI connection**
  The MCP connection can now write the model as STL, 3MF or OBJ as well, from the same bodies as the export window. An AI can build a design and have the print file written straight to a path on the computer.
- **2D drawing of smooth, round parts**
  The 2D drawing refused small parts with many curved faces because they had too many triangles. The limit is four times as high now, and the message says how many triangles the views really need.
- **Grouping says when parts were only laid together**
  Above the triangle limit, grouping imported bodies does not merge them but only lays them together, and a slicer then reports faulty edges. The status line now says so, with the triangle count and the limit.
- **A picture with a transparent edge is traced as its figure**
  A black sticker on a transparent background came in as loose pieces instead of one outline. When most of the edge of the picture is transparent, the transparency now counts as the background, and the figure is traced.

## Kaj je novega v 1.81.0

- **Obnovitev varnostne kopije: svoj gumb**
  Poleg »Varnostna kopija vsega« zdaj obstaja gumb »Obnovi varnostno kopijo«. Izbereš datoteko kopije in vsi načrti ter lastne oblike se vrnejo; kar že obstaja, se preskoči. Gumb je na voljo tudi na novem računalniku brez načrtov.
- **Veliki krogi iz SVG so okrogli**
  Velik krog ali dolga krivulja v uvoženem SVG je doslej prispela z vidnimi robovi, ker je bila vsaka krivulja razrezana na enako malo kosov. Zdaj število kosov sledi dolžini krivulje: veliki krogi so okrogli, majhne krivulje pa ostanejo tako lahke kot prej. Nastavitev ni potrebna.
- **Mere na zavrtenih telesih**
  Če je telo zavrteno za 90° in je delovna ravnina na ploskvi, je številka na dolgem robu spremenila drugo stran. Zdaj številka na robu spremeni stran, ob kateri stoji, zadržani rob pa ostane tam, kjer je bil.
- **Drobni ločeni koščki ne pridejo v izvoz**
  Rez in barvna ločitev pri izvozu 3MF lahko pustita za sabo veliko drobnih ločenih koščkov, majhnih zrnc v sloju ali na njem. V urejevalniku jih ne vidiš, v slicerju pa se prikažejo kot konice in lahko ploski načrt dvignejo s plošče. STL, 3MF in OBJ jih zdaj izpustijo, obvestilo pa pove, koliko jih je bilo. Telo samo in majhni deli, ki si jih narisal, ostanejo.
- **Višine nad 220 mm**
  Polje »Z (višina)« je pri 220 mm skočilo nazaj, tudi v načinu »Brez tiskalnika – poljubna velikost«. Zdaj lahko vpišeš poljubno število, navzgor in navzdol, enako za X in Y. Drsnik sega do roba plošče in višine tiskalnika.
- **STL, 3MF in OBJ prek povezave z umetno inteligenco**
  Povezava MCP zdaj lahko model zapiše tudi kot STL, 3MF ali OBJ, iz istih teles kot okno za izvoz. Umetna inteligenca lahko zgradi načrt in tiskalno datoteko zapiše neposredno na pot v računalniku.
- **2D risba gladkih, okroglih delov**
  2D risba je zavračala majhne dele z mnogo ukrivljenimi ploskvami, ker so imeli preveč trikotnikov. Omejitev je zdaj štirikrat višja, sporočilo pa pove, koliko trikotnikov pogledi res potrebujejo.
- **Združevanje pove, kdaj so deli samo položeni skupaj**
  Nad omejitvijo trikotnikov združevanje uvoženih teles ne zlije, ampak jih samo položi skupaj, slicer pa nato javi napačne robove. Statusna vrstica zdaj to pove, skupaj s številom trikotnikov in omejitvijo.
- **Slika s prosojnim robom se sledi kot lik**
  Črna nalepka na prosojnem ozadju je prišla kot ločeni koščki namesto kot en obris. Kadar je večina roba slike prosojna, prosojnost zdaj velja za ozadje in lik se sledi.
