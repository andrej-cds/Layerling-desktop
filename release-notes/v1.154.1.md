## What's new in 1.154.1

- **Move the ribbon's groups and buttons**
  The settings have a tab of their own, Ribbon: it holds the control size and the buttons you can take out. Beside every group and every button are two arrows. With them Arrange can stand right after Combine, say, and a button can move to the front of its group. Only the Shapes group keeps its place.
- **The pinned Shapes panel stays below the toolbar**
  A Shapes panel that had been moved or pulled larger slipped under the toolbar with its title bar and search once pinned. Now it opens where you left it, and follows the toolbar when it grows or shrinks.
- **Slotted screw holes**
  The counterbore, the countersink and the double countersink have a switch, Slotted: the head and the shaft then run along a slot, so the part can still be lined up before the screw is tightened. Set its length just below.

## Kaj je novega v 1.154.1

- **Premikanje skupin in gumbov v traku**
  V nastavitvah je poseben zavihek »Ribbon«: tam sta velikost elementov in gumbi, ki jih lahko odstraniš. Ob vsaki skupini in vsakem gumbu sta dve puščici. S tem lahko na primer »Arrange« stoji takoj za »Combine«, gumb pa se premakne na začetek svoje skupine. Samo skupina Shapes obdrži svoje mesto.
- **Pripeto okno oblik ostane pod orodno vrstico**
  Premaknjeno ali povečano okno oblik je po pripenjanju zdrsnilo pod orodno vrstico z naslovom in iskanjem. Zdaj se odpre tam, kjer si ga pustil, in sledi orodni vrstici, ko se poveča ali zmanjša.
- **Podolgovate luknje za vijake**
  Izvrtina, posnetje in dvojno posnetje imajo stikalo »Slotted«: glava in steblo tečeta vzdolž reže, tako da lahko del še poravnaš, preden vijak zategneš. Dolžino nastaviš takoj pod njim.
