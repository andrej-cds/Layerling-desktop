## What's new in 1.102.0

- **No stray strip when rounding an imported part**
  Rounding an imported part with a narrow bay could leave a thin strip over the bay at a large radius. Now layerling says the radius is too large for that edge instead of showing the strip.
- **The SVG footprint is cut at the workplane**
  With "Footprint on the workplane" in the SVG export, one part reaching below the workplane could pull the cut down with it, and the parts standing on the workplane were missing from the file. The cut now lies just above the workplane whenever a part reaches through it.
- **Reference images to line parts up on**
  The import window now has Reference image: a picture on the plate or on the workplane's face, a photo of a board under its case, say. Make it see-through, calibrate it by two points and their real distance, and lock it. It is never printed or exported.
- **Fillets on leaning bodies**
  A fillet or chamfer on a body with taper and lean or twist no longer shifts it: the lean was applied a second time after the edge work, now the body stays as it was before the edge.
- **Visible edges in the SVG: rounded edges and closed outlines**
  In the SVG export's "Visible edges from above", a rounded top edge now gets its outer outline, where the rounding runs into the wall below it – and so does the 2D drawing's top view. Outlines also stay closed at the sharp corners of text and where a bore goes through two parts lying on each other, so a laser program can engrave every area.
- **Sweep: put an edge of the section on the path**
  In the sweep sketch the section is no longer only centred on the path: in the menu next to the mode badge you can put its left or right edge on the path instead. A rail then follows the edge of a part without you having to move the path by half the width.
- **3MF and OBJ export much faster**
  A finely traced model with many triangles kept the page busy while the file was written, on slow computers until Chrome asked whether to wait. Welding the points, the biggest single item, is about thirty times faster.

## Kaj je novega v 1.102.0

- **Brez zaplate pri zaokrožitvi uvoženega dela**
  Zaokrožitev uvoženega dela z ozko vdolbino je pri velikem polmeru lahko pustila tanek trak čez vdolbino. Zdaj layerling raje pove, da je polmer za ta rob prevelik, namesto da bi pokazal trak.
- **Odtis v SVG je prerezan na delovni ravnini**
  Pri »Odtis na delovni ravnini« v izvozu SVG je en del, ki sega pod delovno ravnino, lahko potegnil rez navzdol, deli, ki stojijo na delovni ravnini, pa so v datoteki manjkali. Rez zdaj leži tik nad delovno ravnino, kadar del sega skoznjo.
- **Referenčne slike, po katerih poravnaš dele**
  Okno za uvoz ima zdaj Referenčno sliko: sliko na plošči ali na ploskvi delovne ravnine, na primer fotografijo plošče pod ohišjem. Naredi jo prosojno, umeri z dvema točkama in njuno pravo razdaljo ter jo zakleni. Nikoli se ne natisne ali izvozi.
- **Zaokrožitve na nagnjenih telesih**
  Zaokrožitev ali posnetek na telesu z zožitvijo in nagibom ali zasukom ga ne premakne več: nagib se je po obdelavi roba uporabil drugič, zdaj telo ostane tako, kot je bilo pred robom.
- **Vidni robovi v SVG: zaobljeni robovi in zaprti obrisi**
  Pri »Vidni robovi od zgoraj« v izvozu SVG zaobljen zgornji rob zdaj dobi svoj zunanji obris, kjer zaobljenje preide v steno pod njim, enako pogled od zgoraj v 2D risbi. Obrisi ostanejo zaprti tudi na ostrih vogalih besedila in kjer vrtina poteka skozi dva dela, ki ležita drug na drugem, tako da laserski program lahko vgravira vsako površino.
- **Skica s potjo: rob preseka na poti**
  Pri skici s potjo presek ni več samo središčen na poti: v meniju ob znački načina lahko namesto tega postaviš njegov levi ali desni rob na pot. Tirnica tako sledi robu dela, ne da bi pot moral premakniti za polovico širine.
- **Izvoz 3MF in OBJ je precej hitrejši**
  Fino posnet model z veliko trikotniki je med pisanjem datoteke zaposlil stran, na počasnih računalnikih, dokler ni Chrome vprašal, ali naj počaka. Spajanje točk, največja posamezna postavka, je približno trideset krat hitrejše.
