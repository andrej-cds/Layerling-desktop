## What's new in 1.131.0

- **Flexi links that fold up and down**
  Flexi joints now let you choose how the links move: to the sides as before, or up and down. Up and down gives bands like a flexi keychain, an egg or a bracelet: every cut gets a flat hinge that is printed in place.
- **Drag values, undo right away**
  In the properties panel you change a value by dragging its name, such as "Width", left or right. And a message about a step just taken, such as "3 shapes deleted", now has an Undo button.
- **Look straight onto the workplane**
  Press P or click the new button next to Place workplane in the view bar: the view turns until you see the workplane square on, on a sloped face too, with its origin in the middle.
- **Button names above the button**
  In the installed app, a button's name now appears above the button instead of below it, so the mouse pointer no longer covers it, even if you made the pointer larger in your system settings.
- **Wing nuts, star knobs, finishing washers**
  A nut now also comes square, with a flange, with wings or as a star knob, and a screw with a square, flange or wing head or a star knob. Wings and knobs lie flat and print without supports. New too is the finishing washer: the domed washer under a countersunk screw, in M2 to M20.
- **Check clearance**
  Combine has Check clearance: before you print, it shows which parts overlap, touch or sit too close together, such as a flexi toy, a hinge printed in place or a lid in its box.
- **Several surface textures**
  A body can now carry several surface textures, each on faces of its own: bricks all round and knurling on top, for example.
- **Raspberry Pi true to size**
  New in the shapes: the board of a Raspberry Pi 5, 4, 3 B+ or Zero 2 W or a BIGTREETECH Pi, with its holes and connectors. Grouped with a case as a hole, it cuts the openings for its connectors.

## Kaj je novega v 1.131.0

- **Flexi členki, ki se gibljejo gor in dol**
  Flexi spoji zdaj omogočajo izbiro, kako se členki gibljejo: vstran kot doslej ali gor in dol. Gor in dol da trakove kot pri flexi obesku za ključe, jajcu ali zapestnici: vsak rez dobi ploščat tečaj, ki se natisne že sestavljen.
- **Vleci vrednosti, takoj razveljavi**
  V plošči lastnosti spremeniš vrednost tako, da povlečeš njeno ime, na primer »Width«, levo ali desno. Sporočilo o pravkar narejenem koraku, na primer »3 shapes deleted«, ima zdaj gumb Undo.
- **Pogled naravnost na delovno ravnino**
  Pritisni P ali klikni nov gumb ob Place workplane v vrstici pogleda: pogled se zavrti, dokler ne gledaš delovne ravnine naravnost, tudi na poševni ploskvi, z izhodiščem na sredini.
- **Imena gumbov nad gumbom**
  V nameščenem programu se ime gumba zdaj pokaže nad gumbom in ne pod njim, tako da ga kazalec miške ne pokriva, tudi če si v sistemskih nastavitvah povečal kazalec.
- **Krilne matice, zvezdasti gumbi, okrasne podložke**
  Matica je zdaj tudi kvadratna, z robom, s krilci ali kot zvezdasti gumb, vijak pa s kvadratno, robato ali krilno glavo ali kot zvezdasti gumb. Krilca in gumbi ležijo ravno in se natisnejo brez podpor. Novost je tudi okrasna podložka: izbočena podložka pod vijakom s stožčasto glavo, od M2 do M20.
- **Preveri zračnost**
  Combine ima Check clearance: pred tiskom pokaže, kateri deli se prekrivajo, dotikajo ali so preblizu, na primer pri flexi igrači, tečaju, natisnjenem sestavljenem, ali pokrovu v škatli.
- **Več površinskih tekstur**
  Telo lahko zdaj nosi več površinskih tekstur, vsako na svojih ploskvah: na primer opeke povsod naokoli in narebričenje na vrhu.
- **Raspberry Pi v pravi velikosti**
  Novo med oblikami: plošča Raspberry Pi 5, 4, 3 B+ ali Zero 2 W ali BIGTREETECH Pi z luknjami in priključki. Če jo združiš z ohišjem kot luknjo, izreže odprtine za njene priključke.
