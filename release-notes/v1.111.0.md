## What's new in 1.111.0

- **Changing text keeps the baseline**
  When you type other words into the text, the baseline the letters stand on stays where it was: a "p" reaches below the line with its tail, instead of every text sitting on the bottom of its box.
- **Corner radius for Gridfinity baseplates**
  A Gridfinity baseplate's outer corners no longer have to be rounded by 4 mm: set "Corner radius" in the Properties panel, down to 0 for square corners that sit in a drawer's corners. The sockets stay as they are, so bins fit as before.
- **Pictures and PDF as attachments**
  A design can now also keep pictures (.png, .jpg, .webp) and PDF files, such as a photo of the printed part or a datasheet. The eye shows a picture right in the window; a PDF is downloaded.
- **Paste in the middle**
  The settings have a new switch, "Paste in the middle and drop": a paste then sets the parts together in the middle of the workplane and stands them on it, instead of where they came from. Handy for parts from other programs, which can otherwise land far from the plate.
- **A larger workplane**
  The workplane can now be set up to 5000 x 5000 mm; it stopped at 2000 mm before. You set it under the custom size in the settings.

## Kaj je novega v 1.111.0

- **Sprememba besedila ohrani osnovnico**
  Ko v besedilo vtipkaš druge besede, osnovnica, na kateri stojijo črke, ostane tam, kjer je bila: »p« z repom seže pod črto, namesto da bi vsako besedilo sedelo na dnu svojega okvirja.
- **Polmer oglišč osnovnih plošč Gridfinity**
  Zunanja oglišča osnovne plošče Gridfinity ne smejo biti več nujno zaobljena s 4 mm: v podoknu Lastnosti nastavi »Polmer oglišč«, vse do 0 za ostra oglišča, ki se prilegajo kotom predala. Ležišča ostanejo, kot so, zato posode pasejo kot prej.
- **Slike in PDF kot priloge**
  Projekt lahko zdaj hrani tudi slike (.png, .jpg, .webp) in datoteke PDF, na primer fotografijo natisnjenega dela ali podatkovni list. Oko pokaže sliko kar v oknu, PDF se prenese.
- **Lepljenje na sredino**
  Nastavitve imajo novo stikalo »Prilepi na sredino in spusti«: lepljenje nato postavi dele skupaj na sredino delovne ravnine in jih postavi na njo, namesto na mesto, od koder so prišli. Priročno za dele iz drugih programov, ki sicer lahko pristanejo daleč od plošče.
- **Večja delovna ravnina**
  Delovno ravnino je zdaj mogoče nastaviti do 5000 x 5000 mm; prej se je ustavila pri 2000 mm. Nastaviš jo pod lastno velikostjo v nastavitvah.
