## What's new in 1.107.1

Only the desktop part changes in this release (layerling stays at 1.107.0).

- **Open in Fusion survives Fusion updates**
  Fusion moves to a new folder with every update, so the saved path to Fusion360.exe stopped working ("program does not exist"). Now the newest Fusion is found by itself; if none is found, the program set for .step in Windows opens the file.
- **STEP export of a pocket with scallops**
  A hole made of a solid that has holes of its own (a round pocket with scallops taken out of it) was exported as just the inner holes, so the part in Fusion had twelve round holes instead of the pocket. It is now one cutter, as in layerling.
- **Thumbnails of designs on the server**
  Designs in a shared folder that is also served by store.php (for example on an iPad) showed "No snapshot yet" in the desktop program. The thumbnails are now found by both.

## Kaj je novega v 1.107.1

V tej izdaji se spremeni samo namizni del (layerling ostaja pri 1.107.0).

- **Odpiranje v Fusionu preživi posodobitve Fusiona**
  Fusion se ob vsaki posodobitvi preseli v novo mapo, zato shranjena pot do Fusion360.exe ni več delovala (»program ne obstaja«). Zdaj program najnovejši Fusion najde sam; če ga ni, datoteko odpre program, ki je v Windowsih nastavljen za .step.
- **Izvoz STEP za izdolbino z žlebovi**
  Luknja, sestavljena iz telesa z lastnimi luknjami (okrogla izdolbina z odgriznjenimi žlebovi), se je izvozila samo kot notranje luknje, zato je bil del v Fusionu poln dvanajstih okroglih lukenj namesto izdolbine. Zdaj je en sam rezalnik, kot v layerlingu.
- **Sličice načrtov na strežniku**
  Načrti v skupni mapi, ki jo streže tudi store.php (npr. na iPadu), so v namiznem programu kazali »No snapshot yet«. Sličice zdaj najdeta oba.
