## What's new in 1.176.0

- **LayerLab by CDS: new name and icon**
  The program is now called LayerLab by CDS and has a new icon: gold on black in a thin gold frame, the same style as the other CDS programs. Your settings and designs stay where they were.

- **Sketch: helping lines, open ends and more**
  A point you draw or drag close to the height or the side of another point lines up with it; a dashed blue line shows it. Alt keeps it on the grid alone.

If the outline is not closed when you finish, the open ends are ringed in red.

– An empty sketch can be finished; an emptied sketch removes its body (Undo brings it back).
– Curve line follows the corner instead of bowing against it.
– The Stroke button switches the stroke on straight away.
– The parts seen through can be left out altogether.
– A click inside the frame of a selection lets it go.
- **3MF: colour changes by height**
  Some 3MF projects from Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer or PrusaSlicer get their colours from filament changes at set heights. Layerling now reads these changes and asks on import whether to cut the bodies there into a body per colour.

“Keep in one piece” imports the file as before.
- **The view as a line drawing**
  Under View, the SVG export now also draws the design as you are looking at it: the visible edges, the hidden ones dashed as well, or only the outline, at 1:1 and each body in its colour. For illustrations, manuals and pen plotters.
- **Tablet: sliders and handles more precise**
  With a finger or a pen, a slider in the settings now moves only when you take its knob and drag it sideways. A swipe that starts elsewhere on the bar scrolls the panel and leaves the value as it is.

A pen such as an S Pen or Apple Pencil takes the handles at a selection only where they are, as a mouse does. With a mouse everything stays as before.
- **Speed for the 3D mouse**
  A SpaceMouse now has a slider of its own in the settings under Controls: 3D mouse speed, beneath the directions to turn round. It makes turning, shifting and zooming with the 3D mouse slower or faster, from a quarter to four times; in the middle everything stays as before.

Zoom speed and Pan speed are for the regular mouse only.
- **Gridfinity: half cells in a baseplate's padding**
  Switch on "Half cells in the padding (21 mm)" in a baseplate's Properties panel and every side with 21 mm of padding or more gets a row of 21 mm sockets against the grid, for bins on the half-size grid. The rest of the padding stays solid, and the plate keeps its size.
- **Lids: a sloping rim is named as such**
  For a body with its top cut off at an angle, the lid tool now says that the rim slopes, even when the low side is below half the height. Before, it said the body had no opening at the top.
- **Lids: no more lines across the plate**
  On some lids, such as those of the letters O and B, the editor showed thin lines across the plate. They are gone. Prints were never affected.
- **Back text: only the tag turns over**
  Pick "Back" on a name tag or icon tag and only the tag is now shown turned over, in its place. The plate and the view stay as they are. "Front" or a click elsewhere turns it back. Only the view changes, not the tag.
- **Make exact: a whole sphere works**
  A finely divided sphere, exported as STL and read back, now becomes one exact sphere face with "Make exact". Before, it failed. Hemispheres and spheres with a flat underside or a bore work more reliably too.
- **Keys, shortcut list and small fixes**
  Ctrl+Shift+S made the selection a solid, Ctrl+M opened the mirror tool: the letters H, S, M, L and N now only count alone. In the history view, Page Up and Page Down also work after a click in the view.

The shortcut list names more keys: Split, Paint, the tape measure, Delete face and the sketch.

Importing settings keeps your own profiles, and the plant pot, the lampshade and other shapes from the library are named in your language.

## Kaj je novega v 1.176.0

- **LayerLab by CDS: novo ime in ikona**
  Program se zdaj imenuje LayerLab by CDS in ima novo ikono: zlato na črnem v tanki zlati obrobi, v enakem slogu kot drugi programi CDS. Tvoje nastavitve in načrti ostanejo, kjer so bili.

- **Skica: pomožne črte, odprti konci in več**
  Točka, ki jo narišeš ali povlečeš blizu višine ali stranice druge točke, se z njo poravna; črtkana modra črta to pokaže. Alt jo drži samo na mreži.

Če obris ob koncu ni sklenjen, so odprti konci obkroženi z rdečo.

– Prazno skico je mogoče končati; izpraznjena skica odstrani svoje telo (Undo ga vrne).
– Ukrivljena črta sledi vogalu, namesto da se upogne proti njemu.
– Gumb Stroke takoj vklopi potezo.
– Dele, vidne skozi, je mogoče izpustiti povsem.
– Klik znotraj okvirja izbora ga izpusti.
- **3MF: barvne spremembe po višini**
  Nekateri projekti 3MF iz Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer ali PrusaSlicer dobijo barve iz menjav filamenta na določenih višinah. Layerling te spremembe zdaj prebere in ob uvozu vpraša, ali naj telesa tam prereže na telo za vsako barvo.

»Keep in one piece« uvozi datoteko kot prej.
- **Pogled kot črtna risba**
  Pod View SVG izvoz zdaj nariše tudi načrt, kot ga gledaš: vidne robove, skrite tudi črtkane ali samo obris, v 1:1 in vsako telo v svoji barvi. Za ilustracije, priročnike in peresne plotterje.
- **Tablica: drsniki in ročaji natančnejši**
  S prstom ali peresom se drsnik v nastavitvah zdaj premakne samo, ko prime gumb in ga povleče vstran. Poteg, ki se začne drugje na vrstici, pomika ploščo in pusti vrednost, kot je.

Pero, kot sta S Pen ali Apple Pencil, prime ročaje ob izboru samo tam, kjer so, kot miška. Z miško ostane vse kot prej.
- **Hitrost za 3D miško**
  SpaceMouse ima zdaj svoj drsnik v nastavitvah pod Controls: 3D mouse speed, pod smermi za obračanje. Vrtenje, premikanje in približevanje s 3D miško naredi počasnejše ali hitrejše, od četrtine do štirikrat; na sredini ostane vse kot prej.

Zoom speed in Pan speed sta samo za navadno miško.
- **Gridfinity: pol celice v obrobi osnovne plošče**
  Vklopi »Half cells in the padding (21 mm)« v lastnostih osnovne plošče in vsaka stran z 21 mm obrobe ali več dobi vrsto vtičev 21 mm ob mreži, za posode na mreži polovične velikosti. Preostala obroba ostane polna, plošča obdrži velikost.
- **Pokrovi: poševen rob je imenovan kot tak**
  Pri telesu z zgornjim delom, odrezanim pod kotom, orodje za pokrov zdaj pove, da je rob poševen, tudi ko je nizka stran pod polovico višine. Prej je pisalo, da telo zgoraj nima odprtine.
- **Pokrovi: brez črt čez ploščo**
  Na nekaterih pokrovih, na primer črk O in B, je urejevalnik kazal tanke črte čez ploščo. Izginile so. Tiski niso bili nikoli prizadeti.
- **Besedilo zadaj: obrne se samo oznaka**
  Izberi »Back« na imenski ali ikonski oznaki in zdaj je na svojem mestu prikazana obrnjena samo oznaka. Plošča in pogled ostaneta kot sta. »Front« ali klik drugje jo obrne nazaj. Spremeni se samo pogled, ne oznaka.
- **Make exact: cela krogla deluje**
  Fino razdeljena krogla, izvožena kot STL in prebrana nazaj, zdaj z »Make exact« postane ena natančna ploskev krogle. Prej ni uspelo. Polkrogle in krogle z ravno spodnjo stranjo ali izvrtino delujejo tudi zanesljiveje.
- **Tipke, seznam bližnjic in majhni popravki**
  Ctrl+Shift+S je izbor spremenil v trdno telo, Ctrl+M je odprl orodje za zrcaljenje: črke H, S, M, L in N zdaj štejejo samo same. V pogledu zgodovine delujeta Page Up in Page Down tudi po kliku v pogledu.

Seznam bližnjic imenuje več tipk: Split, Paint, trakec, Delete face in skica.

Uvoz nastavitev ohrani tvoje profile, cvetlični lonec, senčnik in druge oblike iz knjižnice pa so poimenovani v tvojem jeziku.
