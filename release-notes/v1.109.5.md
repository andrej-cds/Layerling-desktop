## What's new in 1.109.5

Only the desktop part changes in this release (layerling stays at 1.109.0).

- **Export window: new "Program" row with a "Fusion" button**
  Choose "Fusion" to export the design as STEP and open it in Fusion. The "STEP" button now only saves the STEP file, without opening any program.
- **File menu cleaned up**
  "Open in Fusion after STEP export" and "Choose Fusion program" are gone, since the Fusion program is found automatically.

---

## Kaj je novega v 1.109.5

V tej izdaji se spremeni samo namizni del (layerling ostaja pri 1.109.0).

- **Izvozno okno: nova vrstica »Program« z gumbom »Fusion«**
  Z izbiro »Fusion« se načrt izvozi v STEP in odpre v Fusionu. Gumb »STEP« zdaj samo shrani datoteko STEP, brez odpiranja programa.
- **Meni File počiščen**
  »Po izvozu STEP odpri v Fusionu« in »Izberi program Fusion« sta odstranjena, ker se Fusion najde samodejno.
