## What's new in 1.151.0

- **A framing square as long as the plate**
  The framing square's arms can now be dragged longer or shorter at their ends. A button under the handle makes them as long as your printer's build plate.
- **A lid that screws in**
  The Lid tool has a new kind, Screw in: the body gets the thread inside its opening and the lid a threaded plug below it. The outside of the body stays smooth, for a round pen holder, say. Both parts print without support.
- **Turned slots in a hole pattern**
  Slots and rectangles in a hole pattern can now be turned: with Angle of the holes each hole stands at a slant, ventilation slots at 45° for example, while the grid stays straight. The wall between the holes is kept.
- **Hull with open holes**
  A hull fills whatever is hollow in its parts: a tube, a hollowed cylinder, the holes in a group. With Keep the parts' holes under Properties they stay open, and the hull only fills the space between the parts.
- **A slide-in lid**
  The Lid tool has a new kind, Slide-in, like a wooden pencil box's lid: it runs on tongues in grooves in three walls and slides in over the fourth. The box prints upright and the lid turned over, both without support.
- **Flip-top and tether**
  Screw and bayonet caps can get a pouring opening with a printed flap, like a shampoo bottle's, and a tether that keeps the cap on the body. Both print without support.
- **Standard bottle and jar threads**
  The screw lid can take a standard's thread (PCO 1881, PCO 1810, GL 45, mason jars) so it fits a bottle from the shop. The bayonet lid's turn can be set, and a rounded rim is no longer refused.
- **Smoother image textures**
  A picture on a large or round vase no longer comes out in steps of the grid, and Fine is really finer than Medium.

## Kaj je novega v 1.151.0

- **Ogli, dolgi kot plošča**
  Kraka ogli lahko zdaj na koncih povlečeš daljša ali krajša. Gumb pod ročajem ju nastavi na dolžino tiskalne plošče tvojega tiskalnika.
- **Pokrov, ki se privije**
  Orodje »Lid« ima novo vrsto »Screw in«: telo dobi navoj znotraj odprtine, pokrov pa navojni čep pod seboj. Zunanjost telesa ostane gladka, na primer pri okroglem držalu za svinčnike. Oba dela se tiskata brez podpor.
- **Zasukane reže v vzorcu lukenj**
  Reže in pravokotnike v vzorcu lukenj lahko zdaj zasukaš: z »Angle of the holes« vsaka luknja stoji poševno, na primer prezračevalne reže pod 45°, mreža pa ostane ravna. Stena med luknjami se ohrani.
- **Ovojnica z odprtimi luknjami**
  Ovojnica zapolni vse votlo v svojih delih: cev, izdolben valj, luknje v skupini. S »Keep the parts' holes« pod »Properties« ostanejo luknje odprte, ovojnica pa zapolni samo prostor med deli.
- **Pokrov, ki ga potisneš noter**
  Orodje »Lid« ima novo vrsto »Slide-in«, kot pokrov lesene škatle za svinčnike: teče po jezičkih v utorih v treh stenah in zdrsne noter čez četrto. Škatla se tiska pokonci, pokrov obrnjen, oba brez podpor.
- **Pokrov na preklop in pritrditev**
  Navojni in bajonetni pokrovi lahko dobijo odprtino za točenje s tiskanim pokrovčkom, kot pri steklenički šampona, in pritrditev, ki pokrov drži na telesu. Oboje se tiska brez podpor.
- **Standardni navoji za steklenice in kozarce**
  Navojni pokrov lahko prevzame navoj po standardu (PCO 1881, PCO 1810, GL 45, kozarci za vlaganje), da se prilega steklenici iz trgovine. Zasuk bajonetnega pokrova je nastavljiv, zaobljen rob pa ni več zavrnjen.
- **Gladkejše slikovne teksture**
  Slika na veliki ali okrogli vazi ne izpade več v stopnicah mreže, »Fine« pa je zares finejše od »Medium«.
