## What's new in 1.178.0

- **New: Sketch from face**
  Next to the workplane button there is now "Sketch from face". Click it, then a flat face of a part: a new sketch starts on it with its outline and every hole in it, the holes as true circles. Only the part you clicked counts.

Finish the sketch and the new body grows out of the face. A plate or a lid then lines up exactly with the other part's bolt holes, without going through the section view. Esc cancels.
- **The cut turns with the view**
  With the section view's "Axes from" on "View", the cut now turns along when you turn the view. No more clicking again, and zooming or panning leaves it where it is.

Switch off "Turn with the view" to hold the cut still and look at the cut face from the side.
- **Section view next to the workplane**
  The section view's button now sits below the workplane buttons instead of at the bottom with the measuring tools.
- **New: lines parallel or at a right angle**
  Select a straight line in a sketch: the bar now has "Parallel" and "Right angle". Click one, then another straight line, and the selected line turns until it runs parallel or square to it, at any angle. It keeps its length, and the other line stays where it is.

It is a one-time step: nothing stays linked, so either line can be moved freely afterwards. Esc cancels.
- **Scrollbars in the sketch too**
  The button "Show scrollbars" is now in the sketch's view bar as well. It puts a bar along the bottom and one down the right edge, as in the 3D view: drag a bar, click an arrow or click beside the bar.

That helps most on a laptop without a middle mouse button. The switch is one for the sketch and the 3D view.
- **Section view: the diameter is easier to measure**
  When the first point of a measurement in the section view lies on a circle, a dashed ring shows the point right across, with a dashed line through the centre. It catches the pointer from farther away, and the number reads Ø, the diameter.
- **New: a latch as a shape of its own**
  The shape library's enclosure parts now include the "Latch", a clip latch as on a camera battery box: two pins and a clip that snaps over both. Any box and lid can get one.

Place "Pins and clip" on the wall with the seam between the pins; "Separate Parts" makes three parts of it. You set the width, grip and pin spacing, and a clip also fits the latches of the Lid tool.
- **Lid: a preview beside the box**
  While the Lid panel is open, a grey preview beside the box shows the lid your settings would make, with its loose parts and what goes onto the box, such as a hinge's parts. It follows a moment after each change, and "Look at it" turns the view to it.

Nothing is made until "Make lid". The preview can be switched off.
- **Lids with snap hooks**
  Under the lid's latches there are now “Snap hooks” too. They are printed with the lid, so nothing prints loose, and click under small catches on the box. To open, pull the tab at a hook's foot outward.

The box needs enough wall below the rim for them, at least 8 mm.
- **Several shelf ledges**
  Hollow can now lay up to five shelf ledges one above the other, with the distance you set, for example for the floors of a model railway building.

Each higher one is 1 mm narrower than the one below, so a floor for a lower ledge still slides in from the top.
- **Clips printed on the lid close in a ring**
  When the clips are printed on the lid, a clip now goes round the lid's pin as a closed ring. It cannot come off, still turns with room to spare, and has no jaws there to break.
- **Gridfinity: drag a bin's dividers**
  Select a Gridfinity bin with dividers: each divider has a grip on its top in the view, where the compartments show their sizes between the walls and dividers once they are large enough, rounded as in Hollow. Drag a grip and the divider moves in steps of 0.5 mm, 0.1 mm with Alt; Esc puts it back.

It works as the dividers set by hand in Hollow do.
- **Gridfinity: Swallowtail at the corners only**
  Switch on "Corner pockets only" under Swallowtail in a baseplate's Properties panel and the plate keeps its pockets only under the first and last cell of each side: a 5 x 5 plate needs 4 keys instead of 10. "Split to fit the printer" keeps it on its pieces and lays out keys only for those pockets.
- **Dovetail on thin plates**
  Split a thin plate, from about 3 mm, with a dovetail, and the tongue now goes right through it at the size set. The halves slide together from above, like puzzle pieces.
- **Select all bodies of a plate**
  A new button in the plate bar, beside the arrows, selects every visible body on the active plate. That is how a whole plate goes into another design: Ctrl+C or Ctrl+X, open the other design, click the plate it should go on and press Ctrl+V. The parts land there laid out as they were. Delete empties the plate instead.
- **The view as a triangle mesh**
  Under View, the SVG export now also has "Current view: triangle mesh": it draws every triangle edge you can see, the mesh the bodies are printed from, in thin lines and each body in its colour. For illustrations and pen plotters. On round parts it gets busy.
- **Hidden notes show their number**
  When the notes are switched off with the eye, the eye button on the left below the note button now shows a small number: that many notes are in the project. So you don't miss any, even when you open a project while the notes are off. A click on the eye shows them again.
- **Align faces shows the faces you picked**
  In Align faces the faces you click now stay marked until you press Align or Cancel: the face of the part that moves in orange, the face it moves to on the other part in green. So you can see before the click which two faces will come together.
- **Fewer false overhang warnings**
  Faces that lie inside another body, or rest on one, no longer count as overhangs, such as text sunk into a name plate printed upside down. That goes for the print check and the line under the view. The hatching in the view stays as before.
- **Make exact handles more**
  Rounded boxes from a mesh, even with a rounding of only 1 mm, now come back as their few true faces, and coarsely meshed fused spheres are no longer refused. When time runs out, layerling says so.

## Kaj je novega v 1.178.0

- **Novo: skica iz ploskve**
  Poleg gumba delovne ravnine je zdaj »Sketch from face«. Klikni ga, nato ravno ploskev dela: na njej se začne nova skica z njenim obrisom in vsemi luknjami v njej, luknje kot prave krožnice. Šteje samo del, na katerega si kliknil.

Skico končaj in iz ploskve zraste novo telo. Plošča ali pokrov se tako natančno ujame z vijačnimi luknjami drugega dela, ne da bi šel prek prereza. Esc prekliče.
- **Prerez se vrti s pogledom**
  Pri »Axes from« na »View« se prerez zdaj vrti skupaj s pogledom, ko ga zavrtiš. Ni več ponovnega klikanja, povečava in premik ga pustita, kjer je.

Izklopi »Turn with the view«, da prerez obdrži pri miru in si prerezano ploskev ogledaš s strani.
- **Prerez ob delovni ravnini**
  Gumb za prerez je zdaj pod gumbi delovne ravnine, ne več spodaj z merilnimi orodji.
- **Novo: črte vzporedno ali pravokotno**
  Izberi ravno črto v skici: vrstica ima zdaj »Parallel« in »Right angle«. Klikni enega, nato drugo ravno črto in izbrana črta se zavrti, da je vzporedna ali pravokotna nanjo, pod poljubnim kotom. Obdrži dolžino, druga črta ostane, kjer je.

To je enkraten korak: nič ni povezano, zato lahko potem vsako črto prosto premakneš. Esc prekliče.
- **Drsniki tudi v skici**
  Gumb »Show scrollbars« je zdaj tudi v vrstici pogleda v skici. Doda vodoravni drsnik spodaj in navpičnega ob desnem robu, kot v 3D pogledu: povleci drsnik, klikni puščico ali klikni ob drsniku.

Najbolj pomaga na prenosniku brez srednje tipke miške. Stikalo je skupno za skico in 3D pogled.
- **Prerez: premer se lažje meri**
  Če prva točka meritve v prerezu leži na krožnici, črtkan obroč pokaže točko čez celoten premer, s črtkano črto skozi sredino. Kazalec ujame z večje razdalje, število pa kaže Ø, premer.
- **Novo: zapah kot lastna oblika**
  Med deli ohišij v knjižnici oblik je zdaj »Latch«, zapah s sponko kot na škatli za baterijo fotoaparata: dva čepa in sponka, ki se zaskoči čez oba. Vsaka škatla in pokrov ga lahko dobita.

»Pins and clip« namesti na steno s šivom med čepoma; »Separate Parts« iz njega naredi tri dele. Nastaviš širino, prijem in razmak čepov, sponka pa se prilega tudi zapahom orodja Lid.
- **Pokrov: predogled ob škatli**
  Dokler je plošča Lid odprta, siv predogled ob škatli kaže pokrov, ki bi nastal po tvojih nastavitvah, z ločenimi deli in tem, kar gre na škatlo, na primer deli tečaja. Sledi trenutek za vsako spremembo, »Look at it« pa obrne pogled nanj.

Nič se ne naredi, dokler ne klikneš »Make lid«. Predogled je mogoče izklopiti.
- **Pokrovi z zaskočnimi kljukami**
  Pod zapahi pokrova so zdaj tudi »Snap hooks«. Natisnejo se skupaj s pokrovom, zato nič ni ločeno, in se zaskočijo pod majhne zatiče na škatli. Za odpiranje potegni jeziček ob vznožju kljuke navzven.

Škatla potrebuje pod robom dovolj stene zanje, vsaj 8 mm.
- **Več polic**
  Hollow zdaj lahko naredi do pet polic ena nad drugo, z razdaljo, ki jo nastaviš, na primer za nadstropja stavbe modelne železnice.

Vsaka višja je 1 mm ožja od spodnje, zato nadstropje za nižjo polico še vedno zdrsne noter od zgoraj.
- **Zapaha na pokrovu se zaprta v obroč**
  Ko sta zapaha natisnjena na pokrovu, gre zapah zdaj kot zaprt obroč okrog čepa pokrova. Ne more se odpeti, se še vedno vrti z zračnostjo in nima čeljusti, ki bi se zlomile.
- **Gridfinity: povleci pregrade predala**
  Izberi Gridfinity predal s pregradami: vsaka pregrada ima na vrhu oprimek, nad katerim se prikažejo velikosti predelkov med stenami in pregradami, ko so dovolj veliki, zaokrožene kot v Hollow. Povleci oprimek in pregrada se premika v korakih po 0,5 mm, z Alt po 0,1 mm; Esc jo vrne.

Deluje kot pregrade, nastavljene ročno v Hollow.
- **Gridfinity: Lastovičji rep samo v vogalih**
  V Properties osnovne plošče vklopi »Corner pockets only« pod Swallowtail in plošča ohrani žepe samo pod prvo in zadnjo celico vsake stranice: plošča 5 x 5 potrebuje 4 ključe namesto 10. »Split to fit the printer« to ohrani na kosih in ključe razporedi samo za te žepe.
- **Lastovičji rep na tankih ploščah**
  Razdeli tanko ploščo, od približno 3 mm, z lastovičjim repom in jeziček zdaj gre skozi njo v nastavljeni velikosti. Polovici zdrsneta skupaj od zgoraj, kot koščka sestavljanke.
- **Izberi vsa telesa plošče**
  Nov gumb v vrstici plošče, ob puščicah, izbere vsa vidna telesa dejavne plošče. Tako gre cela plošča v drug načrt: Ctrl+C ali Ctrl+X, odpri drug načrt, klikni ploščo, na katero naj gre, in pritisni Ctrl+V. Deli pristanejo tam, razporejeni, kot so bili. Delete namesto tega izprazni ploščo.
- **Pogled kot mreža trikotnikov**
  Pod View ima izvoz SVG zdaj tudi »Current view: triangle mesh«: nariše vsak vidni rob trikotnika, mrežo, iz katere se telesa natisnejo, s tankimi črtami in vsako telo v njegovi barvi. Za ilustracije in risalnike s peresom. Na okroglih delih postane gosto.
- **Skrite opombe kažejo število**
  Ko so opombe z očesom izklopljene, gumb očesa levo pod gumbom za opombe zdaj prikaže majhno številko: toliko opomb je v projektu. Tako nobene ne spregledaš, tudi če odpreš projekt, ko so opombe izklopljene. Klik na oko jih spet pokaže.
- **Align faces pokaže izbrani ploskvi**
  V Align faces ploskvi, ki ju klikneš, ostaneta označeni, dokler ne pritisneš Align ali Cancel: ploskev dela, ki se premika, oranžno, ploskev, k kateri se premika, na drugem delu zeleno. Tako pred klikom vidiš, kateri dve ploskvi se bosta združili.
- **Manj lažnih opozoril o previsih**
  Ploskve, ki ležijo znotraj drugega telesa ali počivajo na njem, se ne štejejo več kot previsi, na primer tekst, potopljen v imensko ploščico, natisnjeno narobe obrnjeno. To velja za preverjanje tiska in vrstico pod pogledom. Šrafura v pogledu ostane kot prej.
- **Make exact zna več**
  Zaobljene škatle iz mreže, tudi z zaobljenostjo samo 1 mm, se zdaj vrnejo kot nekaj pravih ploskev, grobo mrežne zlite krogle pa niso več zavrnjene. Ko čas poteče, layerling to pove.
