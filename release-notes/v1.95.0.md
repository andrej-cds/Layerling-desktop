## What's new in 1.95.0

- **Vanished designs come back**
  With many designs the browser's storage could fill up, and the start page then showed only empty folders. The designs themselves were not gone: the start page now brings them back by itself, and the card pictures no longer take room in the list.
- **Backups with card pictures**
  "Back up all" now takes the card pictures along. After restoring a backup the pictures are back at once, without opening every design.
- **Compartments in Gridfinity bins**
  A Gridfinity bin can now be divided into compartments: set "Columns" and "Rows" under "Compartments", and move each divider to where you need it. Finger scoops repeat in every compartment, and each compartment can have a label shelf of its own.
- **Ctrl or Cmd + drag moves the view**
  Holding Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac) and dragging with the left button moves the view instead of turning it. On a MacBook trackpad: hold Cmd, press and drag.
- **Fillets no longer leave gaps**
  A fillet could leave a mesh with fine gaps at a corner. The body looked hollow in the section view, and the next cut or split stopped with "Not manifold". Such meshes are now closed right away.
- **Lock outlines in the sketch**
  Select an outline in the sketch, and "Lock" in the bar or the key L locks it: it can then no longer be moved, resized or changed until you unlock it. Next to it, "Keep proportions" holds the aspect ratio when you drag the handles or type a size, without Shift.

## Kaj je novega v 1.95.0

- **Izginuli osnutki se vrnejo**
  Pri veliko osnutkih se je lahko shramba brskalnika napolnila in začetna stran je pokazala samo prazne mape. Osnutki sami niso izginili: začetna stran jih zdaj sama vrne, slike na karticah pa ne zasedajo več prostora na seznamu.
- **Varnostne kopije s slikami na karticah**
  »Varnostno kopiraj vse« zdaj vzame s seboj tudi slike na karticah. Po obnovi kopije so slike takoj spet tam, ne da bi bilo treba odpreti vsak osnutek.
- **Predali v Gridfinity posodah**
  Gridfinity posodo lahko zdaj razdeliš na predale: pod »Predali« nastavi »Stolpci« in »Vrste« ter vsako pregrado premakni tja, kjer jo potrebuješ. Žlebovi za prste se ponovijo v vsakem predalu, vsak predal pa ima lahko svojo poličko za nalepko.
- **Ctrl ali Cmd + vlečenje premakne pogled**
  Če držiš Ctrl (Cmd na Macu) in vlečeš z levim gumbom, se pogled premakne namesto da bi se vrtel. Na sledilni ploščici MacBooka: drži Cmd, pritisni in vleci.
- **Zaobljenja ne puščajo več lukenj**
  Zaobljenje je lahko v vogalu pustilo mrežo s finimi režami. Telo je v pogledu prereza videti votlo, naslednji rez ali delitev pa se je ustavil z »Not manifold«. Takšne mreže se zdaj zaprejo takoj.
- **Zakleni obrise v skici**
  Izberi obris v skici, »Zakleni« v vrstici ali tipka L ga zakleni: dokler ga ne odkleneš, ga ni mogoče več premikati, spreminjati velikosti ali oblike. Ob njem »Ohrani razmerja« drži razmerje stranic pri vlečenju ročic ali vpisu velikosti, brez Shifta.
