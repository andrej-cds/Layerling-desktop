## What's new in 1.170.0

- **Hollow: dividers by hand, finger scoops and shaped pockets**
  For a box hollowed with its top open (without a Gridfinity floor), the Hollow window has three new things.

– Dividers: next to "Even grid" there is "By hand". The window shows the box from above; you click a compartment and put a wall into it, drag it, pull one of its ends, set it at a slant, turn it or mirror all of them. The first switch takes the even walls over. On the box every compartment shows its size, and with "By hand" the walls have grips there too.
– Finger scoop: tap a compartment and choose the side its floor runs up into in a quarter round, so small parts can be pushed out with a finger. Works without dividers too.
– Shaped pocket: the outline of another part, seen from above where it lies over the box, is cut into a filling of the box.

The dividers now also follow a box that is turned on the workplane; before, they stood askew to its walls. Through MCP: `dividerWalls`, `scoops`, `scoopRadius`, `pockets` and `pocketFill` in `layerling_hollow_object`.
- **layerling in Hebrew**
  The interface now also comes in Hebrew. Choose the language at the top right. Some new texts and the guide are still in English there.
- **Drawing: number, revision and who drew it in the title block**
  In the export window's 2D tab there are three fields below PDF or SVG: Drawing number, Revision and Drawn by. They go into the drawing's title block; a field left empty shows a dash as before.

The number and revision stay with the design, also in its .lyl file, and layerling keeps your name in this browser for every design. The date is still the day of the export. Through MCP: drawingNumber, revision and drawnBy in layerling_export_drawing.
- **Framing square: new designs with it at the corner of the plate**
  In the settings under Measurement, "Start new designs with a framing square" now lets you choose where it goes: at the origin as before, or at the front left corner of the build plate, with both arms as long as the plate of the printer you chose.

So the square spans the whole plate in every new design right away. The × removes it from a design that doesn't need it.
- **Gridfinity baseplates joined with clips**
  Switch on "Clip notches" in a baseplate's Properties panel and the rim gets a notch at the middle of each cell along every side without padding, for the small wall clips of Gridfinity Extended. So the plate also joins plates made there. "Add clips" lays the clips out in front of the plate, ready to print, and "Clips" sets how many. Press a clip over the two low walls across each seam from above. "Split to fit the printer" cuts a plate with clip notches into pieces with notches on their cut sides too, and lays out the clips for the seams.
- **View cube: Top and Bottom keep the view's turn**
  In the settings under Appearance there is a new switch, "Top and Bottom keep the view's turn". Switched on, a click on Top or Bottom of the view cube, or the keys 5 and 6, looks straight down or up but keeps the view turned as it stood, rounded to the nearest quarter turn.

If you were looking at the plate from the right, the right side stays at the bottom, instead of the view always swinging back to the front.
- **Equal gaps for nested shapes too**
  In Align, the equal-gaps dot now also spreads the shapes when there is not enough room between the outer two. The gap then becomes negative and the shapes overlap evenly, as nested cups do.

The status bar names the gap. On a board or the plate the shapes still have to fit.
- **Fillet and chamfer: no dead tool after a crash**
  On some bodies a certain size crashes the CAD kernel. The tool now opens again by itself afterwards, with your edges and your size, and tells you to try another one. The search for the largest size that fits carries on past such a crash.

## Kaj je novega v 1.170.0

- **Hollow: predelne stene ročno, mulde za prst in oblikovani žepi**
  Pri škatli, izdolbeni z odprtim vrhom (brez dna Gridfinity), ima okno Hollow tri novosti.

– Predelne stene: ob »Even grid« je »By hand«. Okno kaže škatlo od zgoraj; klikneš predal in vanj postaviš steno, jo povlečeš, potegneš enega njenih koncev, jo nagneš, zasučeš ali prezrcališ vse. Prvo stikalo prevzame enakomerne stene. Na škatli vsak predal kaže svojo velikost, pri »By hand« imajo stene tam tudi prijemala.
– Mulda za prst: tapni predal in izberi stran, kamor se njegovo dno dvigne v četrtinskem krogu, da majhne dele potisneš ven s prstom. Deluje tudi brez predelnih sten.
– Oblikovani žep: obris drugega dela, gledan od zgoraj, kjer leži čez škatlo, se izreže v polnilo škatle.

Predelne stene zdaj sledijo tudi škatli, zasukani na delovni ravnini; prej so stale poševno glede na njene stene. Prek MCP: »dividerWalls«, »scoops«, »scoopRadius«, »pockets« in »pocketFill« v »layerling_hollow_object«.
- **layerling v hebrejščini**
  Vmesnik je zdaj na voljo tudi v hebrejščini. Jezik izbereš zgoraj desno. Nekaj novih besedil in priročnik sta tam še v angleščini.
- **Risba: številka, revizija in avtor v glavi risbe**
  Na zavihku 2D izvoznega okna so pod PDF ali SVG tri polja: Drawing number, Revision in Drawn by. Gredo v glavo risbe; prazno polje kaže črtico kot prej.

Številka in revizija ostaneta pri načrtu, tudi v datoteki .lyl, tvoje ime pa layerling hrani v tem brskalniku za vsak načrt. Datum je še vedno dan izvoza. Prek MCP: drawingNumber, revision in drawnBy v layerling_export_drawing.
- **Kotnik: novi načrti z njim v kotu plošče**
  V nastavitvah pod Measurement zdaj »Start new designs with a framing square« omogoča izbiro, kam gre: v izhodišče kot prej ali v sprednji levi kot gradbene plošče, z obema krakoma tako dolgima kot plošča izbranega tiskalnika.

Kotnik tako v vsakem novem načrtu takoj zajame celo ploščo. Križec ga odstrani iz načrta, ki ga ne potrebuje.
- **Osnovne plošče Gridfinity, spete s sponkami**
  Vklopi »Clip notches« v plošči Properties in rob dobi zarezo na sredini vsake celice ob vsaki strani brez roba, za male stenske sponke Gridfinity Extended. Tako se plošča spne tudi s ploščami, narejenimi tam. »Add clips« razporedi sponke pred ploščo, pripravljene za tisk, »Clips« določi njihovo število. Sponko pritisni od zgoraj čez dve nizki steni čez vsak šiv. »Split to fit the printer« razreže ploščo z zarezami na dele z zarezami tudi na rezanih straneh in razporedi sponke za šive.
- **Kocka pogleda: Top in Bottom ohranita zasuk pogleda**
  V nastavitvah pod Appearance je novo stikalo »Top and Bottom keep the view's turn«. Ko je vklopljeno, klik na Top ali Bottom na kocki pogleda ali tipki 5 in 6 gledata naravnost navzdol ali navzgor, a ohranita zasuk pogleda, zaokrožen na najbližjo četrtino obrata.

Če si gledal ploščo z desne, desna stran ostane spodaj, namesto da bi se pogled vedno vrnil na sprednjo stran.
- **Enake vrzeli tudi za ugnezdene oblike**
  Pri Align pika enakih vrzeli zdaj razporedi oblike tudi takrat, ko med skrajnima dvema ni dovolj prostora. Vrzel postane negativna in se oblike enakomerno prekrivajo, kot ugnezdene skodelice.

Vrstica stanja imenuje vrzel. Na plošči ali kot del plošče se oblike še vedno morajo prilegati.
- **Zaobljenje in posnetje: orodje ne odpove več po zrušitvi**
  Pri nekaterih telesih določena velikost zruši jedro CAD. Orodje se zdaj po tem samo znova odpre z izbranimi robovi in velikostjo ter te opozori, naj poskusiš drugo. Iskanje največje velikosti, ki ustreza, se nadaljuje mimo take zrušitve.
