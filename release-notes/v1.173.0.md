## What's new in 1.173.0

- **Vase: relief patterns, your own picture or text**
  The vase now has a Pattern card that shows every pattern as a tile.

– Relief patterns such as petals, scales, honeycomb, bricks, knit or leaves in eleven shapes
– Your own picture or a text as a relief: all over, as a band or once
– The same pattern size even where the vase narrows, and print-friendly edges at 45°
– Vase mode is a switch and can be shown as it will be printed
– A line on the Print card says how well the vase prints
- **Vase as a plant pot**
  New in the shape library: a plant pot with everything that belongs to it.

– Drain holes and a saucer
– A bowl docked flush under the pot, with pins or magnets
– An inner pot and a wall mount the pot slides into
– Show assembled puts all parts in their place and checks them for collisions
- **Vase as a lampshade**
  New in the shape library: a lampshade in forms that print without supports.

– A carrier with two to six spokes that lets light and warm air through
– A base with an LED disc or a screw socket
– As a wall light with a back plate
– The print check says whether the bulb fits
- **Move turned shapes along their own sides**
  Hold J: the arrow keys, dragging with the mouse and the red, green and up/down handles then move a turned or tilted shape along its own axes instead of along the grid, so a tilted shape goes up its slope. In the settings under Controls you can make this the normal way.
- **Split: filament as dowels**
  When splitting with dowels you can choose Filament as dowels (1.75 mm). The holes then take pieces of filament, even in plates from 3 mm, and no loose dowels are printed. The window says how long to cut the pieces.
- **Lid leaves through openings open**
  If a box has a tube standing in it that is open at both ends, the lid now gets the same opening, and its lip goes round the tube. You can turn this off in the Lid window.
- **Hollow: a ledge that prints without supports**
  The shelf ledge in a hollowed body can now slope at 45° underneath, so it prints without supports.
- **Vase: round or chamfered rim**
  The vase's rim and its bottom edge can now be rounded or chamfered, each with a size of its own.
- **Delete face: the whole wall with Shift**
  Hold Shift and click a face in Delete face: the click takes the whole wall - the face and every face it runs smoothly into, such as all the faces of a hole's wall, even where a round wall is made up of many small faces.
- **Surface texture: kaleidoscope and clearer groups**
  – Image tiles can now be a kaleidoscope: any picture becomes a seamless ornament
– The chosen texture group stays chosen after Change texture
– Show texture clearly draws the body grey, so the relief can be seen on any colour
– Hammered and knurling round cylinders come out cleaner
- **Make exact after an import**
  If an imported mesh works well as an exact body, layerling offers that with a button after the import. Such a body often has far fewer triangles.
- **SVG import: strokes that meet**
  Open strokes of one path whose ends meet are now joined into a closed outline on SVG import. Hair-thin leftovers in a drawing no longer become open bodies.
- **Hole pattern in the floor of a screw lid**
  A hole pattern set on the inside floor of a screw lid now goes all the way through. Until now the thread could make the floor count as a round wall, and the holes ended inside the material. Please make holes set earlier anew.
- **Chamfer on a group that did not work as a hole**
  If two parts of a group were off by a few hundredths of a millimetre, the group could not be used as a hole after a chamfer. Such fine gaps are now closed.
- **Making a lid a second time**
  A new lid for a box with magnets or screws now replaces the old posts and pockets instead of standing pegs in them.
- **Finger scoops in every compartment**
  With low dividers every compartment now gets its finger scoop, not just the first row.
- **Focus mode in a narrow window**
  In a narrow window, such as 9:16, the small bar no longer covers the properties.

## Kaj je novega v 1.173.0

- **Vaza: reliefni vzorci, tvoja slika ali besedilo**
  Vaza ima zdaj kartico Pattern, ki prikaže vsak vzorec kot ploščico.

– Reliefni vzorci, kot so cvetni listi, luske, satovje, opeke, pletenina ali listi, v enajstih oblikah
– Tvoja slika ali besedilo kot relief: čez vse, kot pas ali enkrat
– Enaka velikost vzorca tudi tam, kjer se vaza oži, in robovi pod 45°, primerni za tisk
– Način vaze je stikalo in ga je mogoče prikazati, kot bo natisnjen
– Vrstica na kartici Print pove, kako dobro se vaza tiska
- **Vaza kot cvetlični lonec**
  Novo v knjižnici oblik: cvetlični lonec z vsem, kar sodi zraven.

– Odtočne luknje in podstavek
– Skleda, vpeta poravnano pod lonec, s zatiči ali magneti
– Notranji lonec in stenski nosilec, v katerega lonec zdrsne
– »Show assembled« postavi vse dele na mesto in preveri trke
- **Vaza kot senčnik svetilke**
  Novo v knjižnici oblik: senčnik v oblikah, ki se tiskajo brez podpor.

– Nosilec z dvema do šestimi špicami, ki prepušča svetlobo in topel zrak
– Podnožje z diskom LED ali navojno objemko
– Kot stenska svetilka z zadnjo ploščo
– Preverjanje tiska pove, ali žarnica ustreza
- **Premik zasukanih oblik vzdolž njihovih stranic**
  Drži J: puščične tipke, vlečenje z miško ter rdeči, zeleni in navzgor/navzdol ročaji zdaj premikajo zasukano ali nagnjeno obliko vzdolž njenih osi namesto vzdolž mreže, zato nagnjena oblika gre navzgor po svojem naklonu. V nastavitvah pod Controls lahko to narediš za običajen način.
- **Split: filament kot zatiči**
  Pri razdelitvi z zatiči lahko izbereš »Filament as dowels« (1,75 mm). Luknje potem sprejmejo kose filamenta, tudi v ploščah od 3 mm, in ne natisneš nobenih ločenih zatičev. Okno pove, kako dolge kose odrezati.
- **Pokrov pusti odprtine skozi**
  Če v škatli stoji cev, odprta na obeh koncih, pokrov zdaj dobi enako odprtino, njegov rob pa teče okoli cevi. To lahko izklopiš v oknu Lid.
- **Hollow: polica, ki se tiska brez podpor**
  Polica v izdolbenem telesu je zdaj lahko spodaj poševna pod 45°, zato se tiska brez podpor.
- **Vaza: okrogel ali posnet rob**
  Rob vaze in njen spodnji rob sta zdaj lahko zaobljena ali posneta, vsak s svojo velikostjo.
- **Delete face: cela stena s Shift**
  Drži Shift in klikni ploskev v orodju Delete face: klik vzame celo steno, ploskev in vse ploskve, v katere gladko teče, na primer vse ploskve stene luknje, tudi kjer je okrogla stena sestavljena iz mnogih majhnih ploskev.
- **Površinska tekstura: kaleidoskop in jasnejše skupine**
  – Slikovne ploščice so zdaj lahko kaleidoskop: vsaka slika postane brezšiven okras
– Izbrana skupina tekstur ostane izbrana po Change texture
– »Show texture clearly« nariše telo sivo, da je relief viden na vsaki barvi
– Kovano in narebričenje okroglih valjev je čistejše
- **Make exact po uvozu**
  Če uvožena mreža dobro deluje kot natančno telo, layerling to po uvozu ponudi z gumbom. Tako telo ima pogosto precej manj trikotnikov.
- **Uvoz SVG: poteze, ki se stikajo**
  Odprte poteze ene poti, katerih konca se srečata, se pri uvozu SVG zdaj združijo v sklenjen obris. Tanke ostanke v risbi ne postanejo več odprta telesa.
- **Vzorec lukenj v dnu privitega pokrova**
  Vzorec lukenj na notranjem dnu privitega pokrova zdaj gre čisto skozi. Do zdaj je navoj lahko naredil, da je dno štelo za okroglo steno, luknje pa so se končale v materialu. Prosim, luknje, nastavljene prej, naredi znova.
- **Posnetje na skupini, ki ni delovala kot luknja**
  Če sta se dva dela skupine razlikovala za nekaj stotink milimetra, skupine po posnetju ni bilo mogoče uporabiti kot luknjo. Take fine vrzeli so zdaj zaprte.
- **Izdelava pokrova drugič**
  Nov pokrov za škatlo z magneti ali vijaki zdaj nadomesti stare stebričke in žepe, namesto da bi vanje postavil zatiče.
- **Mulda za prst v vsakem predalu**
  Pri nizkih predelnih stenah vsak predal zdaj dobi svojo muldo za prst, ne samo prva vrsta.
- **Način fokusa v ozkem oknu**
  V ozkem oknu, na primer 9:16, mala vrstica ne prekriva več lastnosti.
