## What's new in 1.84.0

- **Pinch to zoom on the trackpad in Safari**
  In Safari on a Mac, spreading and pinching two fingers on the trackpad did nothing. Now it zooms the view like the mouse wheel, at the zoom speed of the settings and towards the pointer.
- **Two colours stay when grouped with a hole**
  A group of a red and a white part that you then grouped with a hole came out in one colour, and Multicolor only switched between the two. Now a group of several colours opens into its parts when grouped, and Multicolor shows both colours.
- **The grid stays when you switch mm/in**
  Switching from millimetres to inches and back set the plate's grid to 5 mm again, even if it was 1 mm before. Now layerling remembers the grid of the unit system you leave and restores it when you switch back.
- **Esc cancels a drag**
  If you move a body or pull a handle and press Esc while still holding the mouse button, the drag is taken back at once: everything stands as before, no step is added to the history and the selection stays. Before, you had to undo the change.
- **An SVG with touching outlines can be cut with**
  A drawing made of panels that share edges, such as a football with its panels, came in as a body that was not cleanly closed: grouped as a hole it did nothing, after about ten seconds. Now the exact kernel builds the body, and touching or overlapping outlines come out as one clean body. Please import SVGs that are already in a design again.
- **Gridfinity bins and baseplates**
  The new shape "Gridfinity" in the shape library makes bins and baseplates for the open storage system on a 42 mm grid: units across, deep and high, the stacking lip and magnet holes if you like. The measures follow the specification, so the parts fit bins and baseplates made with other generators.
- **A hinge without the printed pin**
  A new switch in the hinge settings: the printed pin is left out and the bore runs through every knuckle, the ends included, for a pin of your own made of filament or wire.
- **An empty design on the server is asked about too**
  When you go back to the start page from an empty design, layerling now also asks about a design in the shared server folder, as it already did for one in the browser. "No" removes the empty file from the server.

## Kaj je novega v 1.84.0

- **Povečava s ščipanjem na sledilni ploščici v Safariju**
  V Safariju na Macu razmikanje in ščipanje z dvema prstoma na sledilni ploščici ni naredilo ničesar. Zdaj pogled povečuje kot kolesce miške, s hitrostjo povečave iz nastavitev in proti kazalcu.
- **Dve barvi ostaneta pri združevanju z luknjo**
  Skupina rdečega in belega dela, ki si jo nato združil z luknjo, je prišla v eni barvi, »Večbarvno« pa je preklapljalo samo med dvema. Zdaj se skupina z več barvami ob združevanju razpre na dele, »Večbarvno« pa pokaže obe barvi.
- **Mreža ostane ob preklopu mm/in**
  Preklop z milimetrov na palce in nazaj je mrežo plošče spet nastavil na 5 mm, tudi če je bila prej 1 mm. Zdaj layerling zapomni mrežo merskega sistema, ki ga zapustiš, in jo ob vrnitvi obnovi.
- **Esc prekliče vlečenje**
  Če premikaš telo ali vlečeš ročaj in pritisneš Esc, medtem ko še držiš tipko miške, se vlečenje takoj prekliče: vse stoji kot prej, v zgodovino se ne doda noben korak, izbor ostane. Prej si moral spremembo razveljaviti.
- **SVG s stikajočimi se obrisi je mogoče uporabiti za rez**
  Risba iz plošč, ki si delijo robove, na primer nogometna žoga s ploskvami, je prišla kot telo, ki ni čisto zaprto: združeno kot luknja ni naredilo ničesar, po približno desetih sekundah. Zdaj telo zgradi natančno jedro, stikajoči se ali prekrivajoči se obrisi pa dajo eno čisto telo. SVG-je, ki so že v načrtu, uvozi znova.
- **Gridfinity zabojčki in osnovne plošče**
  Nova oblika »Gridfinity« v knjižnici oblik naredi zabojčke in osnovne plošče za odprti sistem shranjevanja na mreži 42 mm: enote v širino, globino in višino, rob za zlaganje in luknje za magnete po želji. Mere sledijo specifikaciji, zato deli ustrezajo zabojčkom in osnovnim ploščam iz drugih generatorjev.
- **Tečaj brez natisnjenega zatiča**
  Novo stikalo v nastavitvah tečaja: natisnjeni zatič se izpusti, izvrtina pa poteka skozi vse členke, tudi skrajna, za lasten zatič iz filamenta ali žice.
- **Tudi prazen načrt na strežniku se vpraša**
  Ko se z praznega načrta vrneš na začetno stran, layerling zdaj vpraša tudi o načrtu v skupni mapi na strežniku, kot je že pri načrtu v brskalniku. »Ne« izbriše prazno datoteko s strežnika.
