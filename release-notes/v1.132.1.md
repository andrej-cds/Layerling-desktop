## What's new in 1.132.1

- **New name and logo: LayerLing by CDS**
  The program, its installer, the web version and the app icons now carry the new LayerLing by CDS logo and name. The round CDS badge next to the logo in the top bar is gone.

---

## Kaj je novega v 1.132.1

- **Novo ime in logotip: LayerLing by CDS**
  Program, namestitveni program, spletna različica in ikone aplikacije imajo zdaj nov logotip in ime LayerLing by CDS. Okrogli znak CDS ob logotipu v zgornji vrstici je odstranjen.
