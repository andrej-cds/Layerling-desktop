## What's new in 1.75.0

- **Icon tag from a picture (PNG, JPG)**
  The icon tag window now takes PNG and JPG files too. The picture is traced into an outline, the preview shows it, and you choose whether its dark or its light part becomes the icon. An SVG that holds nothing but a picture is traced the same way.
- **Sketch: show the parts seen through**
  In the sketch there is a new button with layers among the view buttons on the left: it draws all the edges of the bodies on the sketch plane, thin and faint, at any height, as if you looked through them. That lets you trace round existing work; on an upright plane set on a front face it is the front view of the part. The choice stays in the browser.
- **Extrude and Revolve: two buttons**
  In the Sketch tab, Extrude sketch and Revolve sketch now stand side by side as two buttons in the Create group instead of in a drop-down. The hint is the tooltip of the button, the question mark beside them. Switching between the two takes one click less.
- **Importing pictures (PNG, JPG)**
  The import window now takes PNG and JPG files too. The picture is traced into an outline and comes in as a sketch body 50 mm wide, which you can resize afterwards. An SVG that holds nothing but a picture is traced the same way.
- **Keys beside the names, and Shift+M for Multicolor**
  The settings of a shape now show the keys next to the names: Solid (S), Hole (H), Transparent (T) and Edit group (E). Multicolor gets a key, Shift+M, and is in the shortcuts list: it switches the selected groups between each part in its own colour and one colour for the group. Shift+M used to start the mirror mode; now only M alone does.
- **French as a language**
  layerling now speaks French: the whole interface and the 16 chapters of the guide. The language is chosen automatically for a browser set to French and can be changed in the language list. It was translated with an AI assistant; wrong words are fixed with a small pull request on GitHub.
- **A custom snap grid, whole only**
  Each custom snap grid in the settings has a switch “Also half and quarter”. With it off, the menu offers just the size you typed, not its half and quarter as well. For the grids you already have, it stays on.
- **Center view beyond the plate**
  A part that lies beyond the edge of the plate can now be brought to the middle of the screen with “Center view on selection” (Shift+F). The house button brings the whole plate back.
- **Arrow keys: 0.01 mm with the grid off**
  With the snap grid off, an arrow key moves the selection by 0.01 mm instead of 0.1 mm, and Shift five times that. With a grid on, the arrows still follow the grid.
- **A text with an outline can be cut out**
  A text with a line round its letters (the Outline fill) could not be cut out of a plate with some fonts (“Could not cut this imported mesh cleanly”). Its mesh is always closed now, and the cut works.

## Kaj je novega v 1.75.0

- **Simbolna oznaka iz slike (PNG, JPG)**
  Okno simbolne oznake zdaj sprejme tudi datoteke PNG in JPG. Slika se izsledi v obris, predogled ga pokaže, ti pa izbereš, ali simbol postane njen temen ali svetel del. SVG, ki vsebuje samo sliko, se izsledi enako.
- **Skica: pokaži dele, ki jih vidiš skozi**
  V skici je med gumbi pogleda na levi nov gumb s plastmi: nariše vse robove teles na ravnini skice, tanke in blede, na kateri koli višini, kot da bi gledal skozi. Tako slediš obstoječemu delu; na pokončni ravnini, postavljeni na sprednjo ploskev, je to pogled na del od spredaj. Izbira se shrani v brskalniku.
- **Izvleci in Zavrti: dva gumba**
  V zavihku Skica sta Izvleci skico in Zavrti skico zdaj drug ob drugem kot dva gumba v skupini Ustvari, namesto v spustnem meniju. Namig je v opisu gumba, vprašaj ob njiju. Preklop med njima je en klik manj.
- **Uvoz slik (PNG, JPG)**
  Okno za uvoz zdaj sprejme tudi datoteke PNG in JPG. Slika se izsledi v obris in pride kot skicirano telo, široko 50 mm, ki ga lahko pozneje spremeniš. SVG, ki vsebuje samo sliko, se izsledi enako.
- **Tipke ob imenih in Shift+M za Več barv**
  Nastavitve oblike zdaj kažejo tipke ob imenih: Telo (S), Luknja (H), Prosojno (T) in Uredi skupino (E). Več barv dobi tipko Shift+M in je na seznamu bližnjic: izbrane skupine preklopi med vsak del v svoji barvi in ena barva za skupino. Shift+M je prej zagnal zrcalni način; zdaj ga zažene samo M.
- **Francoščina kot jezik**
  layerling zdaj govori francosko: ves vmesnik in 16 poglavij navodil. Jezik se izbere samodejno za brskalnik, nastavljen na francoščino, spremeniš pa ga lahko na seznamu jezikov. Prevedel ga je pomočnik z umetno inteligenco; napačne besede se popravijo z majhnim pull requestom na GitHubu.
- **Lastna mreža pripenjanja, samo cela**
  Vsaka lastna mreža pripenjanja v nastavitvah ima stikalo »Tudi polovica in četrtina«. Ko je izklopljeno, meni ponudi samo vpisano mero, ne tudi njene polovice in četrtine. Pri mrežah, ki jih že imaš, ostane vklopljeno.
- **Središči pogled zunaj plošče**
  Del, ki leži zunaj roba plošče, lahko zdaj pripelješ na sredino zaslona z »Središči pogled na izbor« (Shift+F). Gumb s hišo spet pokaže celo ploščo.
- **Smerne tipke: 0,01 mm pri izklopljeni mreži**
  Pri izklopljeni mreži za pripenjanje smerna tipka premakne izbor za 0,01 mm namesto za 0,1 mm, s Shiftom petkrat toliko. Pri vklopljeni mreži smerne tipke še vedno sledijo mreži.
- **Besedilo z obrisom je mogoče izrezati**
  Besedila s črto okoli črk (polnilo Obris) pri nekaterih pisavah ni bilo mogoče izrezati iz plošče (»Te uvožene mreže ni bilo mogoče čisto izrezati«). Njegova mreža je zdaj vedno zaprta in rez deluje.
