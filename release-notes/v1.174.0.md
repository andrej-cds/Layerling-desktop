## What's new in 1.174.0

- **Gridfinity: join a split baseplate with GridFlock's puzzle**
  Switch on GridFlock puzzle on a baseplate, and Split to fit the printer gives each piece puzzle tabs on its cut sides at the back and right and sockets for them at the front and left. The pieces press together from above, with no keys or clips to print. Puzzle fit sets how tightly they sit. The tabs are small, so the joined plates are meant to go into a drawer once and stay there.
- **Patties: pegboard, sloped openings, print chamfer, polygon and star**
  The pattern plate can do more:

– Pattern "Pegboard": upright slots on the grid of the common pegboards, as many as fit
– Slope: the openings run through the plate at an angle, −60° to 60°
– Print chamfer: the top corners of every opening are cut at 45°, so the plate prints standing without support
– For the motif: every second row mirrored or upside down
– Outline polygon and star, with 3 to 12 corners or points
– Going back from Fan to Round gives a circle again, not an oval
- **Wrap around a cylinder: faster and lighter**
  A plate with many holes became a huge mesh when wrapped: a Patties plate had over 650,000 triangles afterwards. The same body now has about 16,000, and wrapping is several times faster.
- **Wrap around a cylinder: a hint about the settings**
  Wrapping turns a shape into a mesh, and its own settings are gone afterwards. The card "Wrap around a cylinder" now says so beforehand. Undo brings the settings back.
- **Tube: the size you typed last stays**
  Outer diameter, inner diameter and wall thickness: type two of them and the third follows, in any order. Wall 1 mm and then outer 30 mm gives an inner diameter of 28 mm. The ring does the same.
- **A measure bar, and a plate exported in one click**
  Under Workspace settings > Appearance the measuring tools can have a bar of their own that you move where you like, and the view bar can be left out altogether. A folded bar shows its name. In the plate bar, "Export plate" writes the active plate as a 3MF in one click.
- **A lid on either end**
  Turn a body over, and the Lid tool makes a second lid for its other end and leaves the first one as it is. Until now it made the first lid again for the wrong end.
- **Lid latches: the clip holds better**
  The clip's sockets now reach round the pin, and the way in is narrower than the pin. A lid that is already there gets the new clip when it is made again.
- **Edges: a note when a rounded edge needs supports**
  A rounded edge on the plate hangs over in its first layers. The chamfer and fillet window now says how steep, and the overhang line already reads the preview.
- **The name stays after cutting out**
  A body you named keeps its name in the object list after Cut out, Punch, the Face tool, a hole pattern, a magnet pocket or a thread insert. It was called "Group" afterwards before.
- **Reset a rotation, or take the position as zero**
  In the Rotation card, "Reset to 0" sets all three angles to 0. "Take as zero" does the opposite for an imported mesh (STL, OBJ, 3MF): it stays as you turned it, its angles are 0 from then on, and its frame and handles follow the plate again.
- **A click beside a number no longer changes it**
  A click just beside a number, on the "mm" for example, set a changed value back to its default: 1 mm became 20 mm. It now goes into the number field, and only the small arrow sets the value back.
- **Choose the colour of imported bodies**
  In the import window, "Colour of imported bodies" sets the colour of bodies whose file gives them none, such as an STL. The browser remembers the choice.
- **Make exact: clean seams on rounded meshes**
  A fine mesh with rounded edges now becomes its real faces instead of thousands of small ones: a rounded box comes out with 26 faces, a ring as a single face.
- **A slash that prints**
  In Multilanguage, the default font of Gridfinity labels, and in Serif and Rounded, the "/" was so thin that a 0.4 mm nozzle could leave it out: 1-1/2" printed as 1-1 2". It is now as thick as the other strokes; a label with a slash may come out a little smaller to make room for it.

## Kaj je novega v 1.174.0

- **Gridfinity: razdeljeno osnovno ploščo spni z GridFlockovo sestavljanko**
  Vklopi »GridFlock puzzle« na osnovni plošči in »Split to fit the printer« da vsakemu kosu na rezanih straneh zadaj in desno zatiče sestavljanke, spredaj in levo pa vtiče zanje. Kosi se spojijo s pritiskom od zgoraj, brez ključev ali sponk za tisk. »Puzzle fit« določa, kako tesno sedijo. Zatiči so majhni, zato je spete plošče namenjeno enkrat dati v predal in tam pustiti.
- **Patties: luknjasta plošča, poševne odprtine, posnetje za tisk, večkotnik in zvezda**
  Vzorčna plošča zmore več:

– Vzorec »Pegboard«: pokončne reže na mreži običajnih luknjastih plošč, kolikor jih gre
– Naklon: odprtine tečejo skozi ploščo pod kotom, od −60° do 60°
– »Print chamfer«: zgornji vogali vsake odprtine so rezani pod 45°, zato se plošča tiska stoje brez podpore
– Za motiv: vsaka druga vrsta prezrcaljena ali obrnjena
– Obris večkotnik in zvezda, s 3 do 12 vogali ali konicami
– Vrnitev iz Fan na Round da spet krog, ne elipse
- **Ovitje okoli valja: hitrejše in lažje**
  Plošča z mnogimi luknjami je po ovitju postala ogromna mreža: plošča Patties je imela potem več kot 650.000 trikotnikov. Isto telo ima zdaj okoli 16.000, ovitje pa je večkrat hitrejše.
- **Ovitje okoli valja: namig o nastavitvah**
  Ovitje spremeni obliko v mrežo in njene lastne nastavitve potem izginejo. Kartica »Wrap around a cylinder« to zdaj pove že vnaprej. Undo nastavitve vrne.
- **Cev: velikost, ki si jo vtipkal zadnjo, ostane**
  Zunanji premer, notranji premer in debelina stene: vtipkaj dva od njih in tretji sledi, v poljubnem vrstnem redu. Stena 1 mm in nato zunanji 30 mm da notranji premer 28 mm. Obroč dela enako.
- **Merilna vrstica in plošča, izvožena z enim klikom**
  Pod Workspace settings > Appearance imajo merilna orodja lahko svojo vrstico, ki jo premakneš, kamor želiš, vrstico pogleda pa je mogoče sploh izpustiti. Zložena vrstica pokaže svoje ime. V vrstici plošč »Export plate« z enim klikom zapiše aktivno ploščo kot 3MF.
- **Pokrov na vsakem koncu**
  Obrni telo in orodje Lid naredi drugi pokrov za njegov drugi konec ter prvega pusti, kot je. Do zdaj je znova naredilo prvi pokrov za napačen konec.
- **Zapahi pokrova: sponka bolje drži**
  Vtiči sponke zdaj segajo okoli zatiča, vhod pa je ožji od zatiča. Pokrov, ki že obstaja, dobi novo sponko, ko ga narediš znova.
- **Robovi: opomba, ko zaobljen rob potrebuje podpore**
  Zaobljen rob na plošči v prvih plasteh visi čez. Okno posnetja in zaobljenja zdaj pove, kako strmo, črta previsa pa že bere predogled.
- **Ime ostane po izrezu**
  Telo, ki si ga poimenoval, obdrži ime na seznamu predmetov po Cut out, Punch, orodju Face, vzorcu lukenj, žepu za magnet ali navojnem vložku. Prej se je potem imenovalo »Group«.
- **Ponastavi vrtenje ali vzemi položaj kot nič**
  Na kartici Rotation »Reset to 0« postavi vse tri kote na 0. »Take as zero« naredi nasprotno za uvoženo mrežo (STL, OBJ, 3MF): ostane, kot si jo zasukal, njeni koti so od tam 0, njen okvir in ročaji pa spet sledijo plošči.
- **Klik ob številki je ne spremeni več**
  Klik tik ob številki, na primer na »mm«, je spremenjeno vrednost postavil nazaj na privzeto: 1 mm je postal 20 mm. Zdaj gre v polje za številko, vrednost pa nazaj postavi samo majhna puščica.
- **Izberi barvo uvoženih teles**
  V oknu za uvoz »Colour of imported bodies« določi barvo teles, ki jim datoteka ne da nobene, na primer STL. Brskalnik si izbiro zapomni.
- **Make exact: čisti šivi na zaobljenih mrežah**
  Fina mreža z zaobljenimi robovi zdaj postane svoje prave ploskve namesto tisočev majhnih: zaobljena škatla ima 26 ploskev, obroč eno samo ploskev.
- **Poševnica, ki se natisne**
  Pri Multilanguage, privzeti pisavi oznak Gridfinity, ter pri Serif in Rounded je bila »/« tako tanka, da jo je šoba 0,4 mm lahko izpustila: 1-1/2" se je natisnilo kot 1-1 2". Zdaj je tako debela kot druge poteze; oznaka s poševnico je lahko nekoliko manjša, da ji naredi prostor.
