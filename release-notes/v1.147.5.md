## What's new in 1.147.5

- **New gold icon**
  The program icon and all LayerLing by CDS icons are now gold with a light-to-dark gradient, the same as the SolidCraft icon (program, installer, browser tab, iPad shortcut, header logo).

---

## Kaj je novega v 1.147.5

- **Nova zlata ikona**
  Ikona programa in vse ikone LayerLing by CDS so zdaj zlate s prelivom od svetle do temne, enako kot ikona SolidCraft (program, namestitev, zavihek brskalnika, bližnjica na iPadu, logotip v glavi).
