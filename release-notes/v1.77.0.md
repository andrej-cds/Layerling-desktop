## What's new in 1.77.0

- **Hungarian as a language**
  layerling now speaks Hungarian (Magyar): the whole interface and the 16 chapters of the guide. The language is chosen automatically for a browser set to Hungarian and can be changed in the language list. It was translated with an AI assistant; wrong words are fixed with a small pull request on GitHub.
- **The mouse wheel zooms alike everywhere**
  A notch of the mouse wheel zoomed only about an eighth as much in Safari on a Mac with a plain mouse as in Chrome on Windows, even with Zoom speed at 10. A single small step now counts like a whole notch. A trackpad, a smooth wheel and the pinch on a trackpad stay as they were.
- **Stretch letters to the frame**
  A straight text used to keep the proportions of its letters and stay in the middle of its frame, even when the frame was pulled wider or deeper. The new switch "Stretch letters to the frame" in the text's settings fills the whole frame in both directions: a capital can be made lower or narrower, as in Tinkercad. The outlines for the edge tool and the fill modes follow.
- **Tube and ring with an inner diameter**
  The tube and the ring had only the wall thickness. Now the inner diameter stands beside it: type the size of the shaft and the wall follows from the width, handy for a bushing. Set the width first; if you change it later, the wall stays and the inner diameter changes with it.

## Kaj je novega v 1.77.0

- **Madžarščina kot jezik**
  layerling zdaj govori madžarsko (Magyar): ves vmesnik in 16 poglavij navodil. Jezik se izbere samodejno za brskalnik, nastavljen na madžarščino, spremeniš pa ga lahko na seznamu jezikov. Prevedel ga je pomočnik z umetno inteligenco; napačne besede se popravijo z majhnim pull requestom na GitHubu.
- **Miškino kolesce povečuje povsod enako**
  Zarez miškinega kolesca je v Safariju na Macu z navadno miško povečal le okoli osmino toliko kot v Chromu na Windowsih, tudi pri hitrosti približevanja 10. Zdaj en majhen korak šteje kot cel zarez. Sledilna ploščica, gladko kolesce in stisk na sledilni ploščici ostanejo, kot so bili.
- **Raztegni črke na okvir**
  Raven tekst je obdržal razmerja črk in ostal na sredini okvirja, tudi ko si okvir razširil ali poglobil. Novo stikalo »Raztegni črke na okvir« v nastavitvah besedila zapolni ves okvir v obeh smereh: veliko črko je mogoče narediti nižjo ali ožjo, kot v Tinkercadu. Obrisi za orodje za robove in načini polnila sledijo.
- **Cev in obroč z notranjim premerom**
  Cev in obroč sta imela samo debelino stene. Zdaj je ob njej še notranji premer: vpiši velikost gredi in stena sledi iz širine, kar je priročno za puš. Najprej nastavi širino; če jo pozneje spremeniš, stena ostane, notranji premer pa se spremeni z njo.
