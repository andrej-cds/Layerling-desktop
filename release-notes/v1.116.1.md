## What's new in 1.116.1

- **STEP assemblies: every body in its colour**
  A STEP file from another CAD program now brings the colours of its bodies along. Where several bodies sit under one entry, all the screws of a part say, each becomes a part of its own. And a STEP export no longer makes the colours paler in the file.
- **Backup restored to the server: with its picture**
  When you restore a backup and the designs go to the server, as in the Docker version, they now keep their picture. Until now the card stayed blank until the design was saved once in the editor.
- **Position from the middle or from the edge**
  In the Position card you now choose what X and Y measure from: the middle of the body as before, or its left and front edges. So you put an edge exactly on a measure without working out half the width.
- **Hole pattern: narrow holes close together**
  Rectangle and slot holes now have their own spacing between the rows. Narrow, tall slots stand as close side by side as the wall allows, instead of as far apart as they are high.
- **Group or bundle at a glance**
  In the Objects panel a group or bundle now shows the same mark as the button that made it, with “Group · 3 parts” or “Bundle · 3 parts” below. That stays right after you rename it.
- **Opening a server design shows on its card**
  A large design from the server takes a few seconds. While it loads, a circle now turns on its card, and further clicks wait until it is open.
- **Server store: a folder for each user**
  If you run layerling behind a proxy with sign-in, you can give each user a server folder of their own with the setting LAYERLING_USER_HEADER. Without it everything stays as it was. Large designs also come from the server without filling its memory.

## Kaj je novega v 1.116.1

- **Sestavi STEP: vsako telo v svoji barvi**
  STEP datoteka iz drugega CAD programa zdaj prinese tudi barve teles. Kjer je več teles pod enim vnosom, na primer vsi vijaki enega dela, postane vsako svoj del. STEP izvoz pa barv v datoteki ne bledi več.
- **Obnovljena varnostna kopija na strežniku: s sličico**
  Ko obnoviš varnostno kopijo in projekti gredo na strežnik, kot v različici Docker, zdaj obdržijo svojo sličico. Doslej je kartica ostala prazna, dokler projekta nisi enkrat shranil v urejevalniku.
- **Položaj od sredine ali od roba**
  V kartici Položaj zdaj izbereš, od česa merita X in Y: od sredine telesa kot doslej ali od njegovega levega in sprednjega roba. Tako rob postaviš natančno na mero, ne da bi računal polovico širine.
- **Vzorec lukenj: ozke luknje blizu skupaj**
  Pravokotne luknje in reže imajo zdaj svoj razmik med vrstami. Ozke, visoke reže stojijo ena ob drugi tako blizu, kot dovoljuje stena, namesto tako narazen, kot so visoke.
- **Skupina ali sveženj na prvi pogled**
  V podoknu Objekti skupina ali sveženj zdaj kaže enako oznako kot gumb, ki ju je naredil, pod njo pa »Skupina · 3 deli« ali »Sveženj · 3 deli«. To ostane pravilno tudi po preimenovanju.
- **Odpiranje projekta s strežnika se vidi na kartici**
  Velik projekt s strežnika se odpira nekaj sekund. Med nalaganjem se zdaj na kartici vrti krožec, dodatni kliki pa počakajo, da se odpre.
- **Strežniška shramba: mapa za vsakega uporabnika**
  Če Layerling poganjaš za posrednikom s prijavo, lahko vsakemu uporabniku daš svojo mapo na strežniku z nastavitvijo LAYERLING_USER_HEADER. Brez nje ostane vse, kot je bilo. Veliki projekti prihajajo s strežnika tudi, ne da bi mu polnili pomnilnik.
