## What's new in 1.156.2

- **Stretch: make a part longer in one place**
  The Split tool now has Stretch beside Split in two: place the plane, type a length, and the body gets longer right there, the cut at the plane filling the gap. A downloaded case grows through its straight walls while compartments and rounded corners stay as they were. A minus value makes it shorter.
- **Revolve sketch: no ghost shape when editing**
  Editing a revolved body that starts above the plate showed a filled shape behind the side view: the level cut through the parts beside it, as seen from above. It stays away now; the body itself was never affected.

## Kaj je novega v 1.156.2

- **Stretch: podaljšaj del na enem mestu**
  Orodje Split ima ob »Split in two« zdaj še »Stretch«: postavi ravnino, vtipkaj dolžino in telo se tam podaljša, rez na ravnini zapolni vrzel. Prenesena škatla zraste skozi ravne stene, predelki in zaobljeni vogali ostanejo, kot so bili. Negativna vrednost ga skrajša.
- **Vrtenje skice: brez prikazni pri urejanju**
  Pri urejanju vrtenega telesa, ki se začne nad ploščo, se je za stranskim pogledom videla zapolnjena oblika: nivo je rezal dele ob njem, gledano od zgoraj. Zdaj je ni več; samo telo ni bilo nikoli prizadeto.
