## What's new in 1.111.6

- **STEP export: a group stays one body**
  A group is now exported to STEP as a single fused body named after the group, instead of several overlapping bodies. In Fusion the edges between the parts of the group exist, so Chamfer and Fillet can be applied to them.

---

## Kaj je novega v 1.111.6

- **Izvoz STEP: skupina ostane eno telo**
  Skupina se zdaj izvozi v STEP kot eno združeno telo z imenom skupine, ne več kot več prekrivajočih se teles. V Fusionu zato obstajajo robovi med deli skupine in nanje lahko uporabite Chamfer in Fillet.
