## What's new in 1.98.0

- **Hollow inside in one click**
  When hollowing, "Closed (cavity inside)" now sits below the sides: one click closes every side, and the cavity stays sealed all round.
- **Thin floors stay when you cut**
  A hole a little above the bottom of a body now leaves just as much floor as there is below it. Before, a floor up to 0.05 mm vanished completely, and a thicker one came out 0.05 mm too thin.
- **The key ring hole wherever you want**
  On the name tag and the icon tag there is now Free beside Left, Right and Top: a tab of its own with the hole lies beside the tag, to move and turn like any shape. "Fix to the tag" makes it part of the tag just where it is, the hole through every layer. Free brings it out again.
- **Deleted designs stay deleted**
  If you had restored a backup after the list was lost, deleted designs came back without a picture at the next start. The rescue now runs only once, and deleting a design removes its hidden copies too.
- **Browser translation works again**
  If layerling does not speak your language yet, the browser may translate the page again. Your own names for designs, folders and shapes stay as they are.
- **Gears with up to 150 teeth**
  Gears, ring gears and racks now take up to 150 teeth instead of 64, for large wheels with a small module, say.
- **Through the history with the mouse wheel**
  In the history view the mouse wheel over the slider goes one state per notch, up to an earlier one. Page Up and Page Down jump ten states, Home and End go to the oldest and to the current state.
- **A fillet with a growing radius**
  Filleting now has "Variable radius": one edge is rounded small at one end and large at the other, evenly in between. "Swap ends" turns it round.
- **SVG with only the footprint**
  The SVG export now has the view "Footprint on the workplane": it writes only the outlines where the objects stand on the workplane, as lines in millimetres. The silhouette from above stays the default.
- **Renamed stays renamed**
  A design renamed on the start page now keeps its new name in a backup and when it is moved to the server folder.
- **A minus key on phone and tablet**
  Fields that also take negative values, such as position, rotation or a move, now show a keyboard with a minus key on phones and tablets.

## Kaj je novega v 1.98.0

- **Izdolbi navznoter z enim klikom**
  Pri izdolbljenju je »Zaprto (votlina znotraj)« zdaj pod stranmi: en klik zapre vse strani, votlina pa ostane zaprta okrog in okrog.
- **Tanka dna ostanejo pri rezanju**
  Luknja malo nad dnom telesa zdaj pusti toliko dna, kot ga je spodaj. Prej je dno do 0,05 mm povsem izginilo, debelejše pa je bilo 0,05 mm pretanko.
- **Luknja za obesek kjer želiš**
  Pri imenski ploščici in ploščici s simbolom je zdaj poleg Levo, Desno in Zgoraj tudi »Prosto«: samostojen jeziček z luknjo leži ob ploščici in ga premakneš in zavrtiš kot vsako obliko. »Pritrdi na napis« ga naredi del ploščice natanko tam, kjer je, z luknjo skozi vse plasti. »Prosto« ga spet izvleče ven.
- **Izbrisani osnutki ostanejo izbrisani**
  Če si po izgubi seznama obnovil varnostno kopijo, so se izbrisani osnutki ob naslednjem zagonu vrnili brez slike. Reševanje zdaj teče samo enkrat, brisanje osnutka pa odstrani tudi njegove skrite kopije.
- **Prevajanje brskalnika spet deluje**
  Če Layerling še ne govori tvojega jezika, lahko brskalnik stran spet prevede. Tvoja lastna imena osnutkov, map in oblik ostanejo, kot so.
- **Zobniki do 150 zob**
  Zobniki, notranji zobniki in zobate letve zdaj sprejmejo do 150 zob namesto 64, na primer za velika kolesa z majhnim modulom.
- **Po zgodovini s kolescem miške**
  V pogledu zgodovine kolesce miške nad drsnikom skoči za eno stanje na zarezo, do starejšega. Page Up in Page Down skočita za deset stanj, Home in End na najstarejše in na trenutno stanje.
- **Zaobljenje s spremenljivim polmerom**
  Zaobljenje ima zdaj »Spremenljiv polmer«: en rob je na enem koncu zaobljen majhno, na drugem veliko, vmes enakomerno. »Zamenjaj konca« ga obrne.
- **SVG samo z odtisom**
  Izvoz SVG ima zdaj pogled »Odtis na delovni ravnini«: zapiše samo obrise, s katerimi predmeti stojijo na delovni ravnini, kot črte v milimetrih. Silhueta od zgoraj ostane privzeta.
- **Preimenovano ostane preimenovano**
  Osnutek, preimenovan na začetni strani, zdaj obdrži novo ime v varnostni kopiji in ob prestavitvi v mapo na strežniku.
- **Tipka minus na telefonu in tablici**
  Polja, ki sprejmejo tudi negativne vrednosti, na primer položaj, vrtenje ali premik, zdaj na telefonih in tablicah pokažejo tipkovnico s tipko minus.
