## What's new in 1.169.0

- **Painting: a brush with pen pressure, a magnetic lasso, a height band**
  Painting for multi-colour printing has new tools.

– Brush: paints a line while the mouse button or the pen is held down. With a pen or a drawing tablet, pressure sets the width, down to a fifth of the diameter. The circle paints a line too when it is dragged; a click still lays one patch.
– See-through ball: shows where the brush or the circle paints. Ctrl + wheel or the keys [ and ] resize it.
– As a ball: the circle and the brush paint everything inside the ball, round edges and into hollows, as the slicers' sphere does.
– Magnetic lasso: click roughly along an edge and the line finds it: a bend of the surface, the body's outline or the border of paint already there. Enter or a click on the first point closes it, Backspace takes a point back.
– Smoothing: evens the line of both lassos out, corners stay.
– Height band: one click paints a band level round the whole body at that height.
– Fill gaps: patches smaller than the area set take the colour round them, so stray specks go.

A new stroke is laid onto the mesh the strokes before it left instead of all strokes again, so painting stays quick with many strokes. Through MCP, `layerling_paint` takes the brush, the ball, the height band and fill gaps.
- **Gridfinity: split single compartments further**
  In the compartments card of the Gridfinity box, the Row and Column menus pick a compartment that can be split. With "Split across" and "Split deep" (1 to 6 each) it is divided into evenly spaced parts with dividers as thick and as high as the box's.

Each part stays at least 6 mm wide, and the box keeps its limit of 40 compartments in all. Over that, compartments are taken from the back left row by row, their parts across first, so a file or an MCP call always builds the same. A compartment's label stands in the part along its side that is furthest left or back. A box without splits is built as before.
- **Gridfinity: bins and trays as large as your print bed**
  Gridfinity bins and trays used to stop at 6 units (252 mm), even when the printer had more room. With your printer chosen in the workspace settings, "Units across" and "Units deep" now offer as many units as fit the longer side of its bed, for example 7 units on a 330 × 320 mm bed. A tray's width and length go up to that side.

A hint under the field says how far it goes. Without a printer chosen it stays at 6 units and 252 mm. If a bin is larger than the bed, the print check says so.
- **Hollow: screw holes in the Gridfinity floor**
  Under Hollow > Gridfinity floor, below "Magnet holes", there is a new tick box "Screw holes". It cuts a hole through the middle of each socket, to screw the box down in a drawer or onto a board: 4.5 mm wide for a 4 mm countersunk screw, sunk at 45 degrees so the head (up to 8.5 mm) lies level with the socket's bottom. A bin still stands in the socket.

The floor is 8.6 mm thick, the same as with magnet holes, so the two go together without making it thicker again. The sizes are fixed. Through MCP: `gridScrews` in `layerling_hollow_object`.
- **Name tag and icon tag: Flat inlay**
  On the cards "Name tag" and "Icon tag" the switch "Flat inlay" sits under the Layers table. With it on, the letters (or the icon) and the rim sink into the bottom layer, and all colours end on one flat face. So the tag can be printed face down on the plate, with the plate's texture on the face.

The bottom layer is the carrier: its height is the thickness of the whole tag, and the key ring ear sits on it. The slider "Inlay depth" sets how deep the layers above it go, all together. It starts at 0.8 mm (four 0.2 mm layers), enough for most PLA. A more translucent filament (PETG, some whites) needs more: print a test first.

While the switch is on, the heights of the upper layers are greyed out. Switched off, the tag is stacked again. In a STEP export the inlaid layers are meshes, 3MF and STL are not affected.
- **New shape: Roof**
  The basic shapes now have a Roof next to the Round roof: a triangle lying on the workplane, its corner on top. The handle on top changes the height of the triangle while its base stays on the plate; the handles at the bottom change its width and length.

Its three sides stay as they are; for another number of sides take the Polygon.
- **A picture for every folder**
  When the name of a folder on the start page starts with an emoji, such as "💡 Ideas", the folder shows that emoji big in place of the folder icon. So you find your folders at a glance.

The emoji is part of the name, so it goes wherever the folder goes. Rename the folder to change it or take it off.
- **Sketch: the arrow keys respond at once**
  In the sketch a press of an arrow key showed the move only after a short moment, and with the key held the selection jumped in big steps. Now it moves at once and evenly. A whole run with the arrow keys is still one step of Undo.
- **Parts land where their file puts them**
  If you have the parts of one model as separate files, such as the body, arms and legs of a miniature, switch on "STL, OBJ, 3MF: keep the position from the file" in the import window. Each part then stands where its file puts it, and everything fits together, even when you import the files one after the other.

The switch stays on until you turn it off. Without it every file comes in on its own near the middle of the plate, as before.
- **Empty designs come back from your backup**
  After a crash the cards on the start page can still show their names and how many shapes they have, while the designs open empty. Until now "Restore backup" skipped exactly those designs, because it took them for ones that are already here.

Now the backup brings their content back into the same card. Designs that still have their shapes stay as they are.
- **Menu bar: no more buttons on top of each other**
  At some window widths, above all in focus mode, groups of the menu bar were squeezed narrower than their buttons. Then the Outliner button of Visibility, for one, stuck out into Shapes.

Now every group keeps its full width, and where there is not enough room, one more group folds into a single button.
- **Smooth fillets on large round edges**
  A fillet of 1 mm or more on a round edge, such as the rim of a large disc or ring, could show slanted dents in the editor and in the slicer. The fillet itself was exact; only the triangle mesh for the view and the export ran across the curve in places, up to a few tenths of a millimetre below the surface.

layerling now checks the mesh on every fillet along a round edge and meshes it more finely where needed. An edge you already filleted comes out smooth when you apply the fillet once more.
- **Honeycomb and cells: no more spikes at the joints' edges**
  Cutting honeycomb or cells into a face and taking the pattern as a part of its own left small spikes at the joints' edges that reached into the cells. They are gone: the pattern now covers only the joints.
- **The letter edge on the bottom too**
  On a Text you now choose where the letter edge goes: on the top, the bottom or both ends. The bottom is for inset letters you cut out of a body with the text as a hole: a chamfer as large as the cut is deep makes V-shaped grooves that print without bridges, even on a side wall.
- **Safari on Intel Macs: large parts drawn correctly again**
  In Safari on a Mac with an Intel processor and its own graphics card, large parts were drawn as a tangle of spikes across the view, such as a pattern wrapped around a cylinder or a large imported mesh. Small parts looked right, and the export was always fine.

The cause is a bug in Safari: of large blocks of data, only the first megabyte reaches the graphics card there. layerling now sends large blocks to Safari in smaller pieces.
- **Mouse buttons as in the slicer: turning in tools too**
  With the mouse buttons set "Like Bambu Studio and OrcaSlicer", a left drag on empty space did not turn the view while a tool such as Split, Fillet or Chamfer was open. Now it turns the view there too. A plain click on empty space still does what the tool always did there.
- **QR code and board: a move is no longer lost while they are rebuilt**
  If you had a QR code made again, or a board rebuilt with a new setting, and moved, locked or renamed it in the meantime, that change was lost without a word. Now the part stays as you changed it, and a notice tells you to start the rebuild once more.
- **Chamfillet faster on bodies with many edges**
  On bodies with a great many edges, such as a large honeycomb plate, the Chamfillet is now done up to twice as fast.

## Kaj je novega v 1.169.0

- **Barvanje: čopič s pritiskom peresa, magnetni lasso, višinski pas**
  Barvanje za večbarvni tisk ima nova orodja.

– Čopič: riše črto, dokler držiš gumb miške ali pero. Pri peresu ali risalni tablici pritisk določa širino, do petine premera. Krog zdaj riše črto tudi, ko ga vlečeš; klik še vedno položi eno liso.
– Prosojna krogla: kaže, kje čopič ali krog barva. Ctrl + kolesce ali tipki [ in ] spreminjata njeno velikost.
– Kot krogla: krog in čopič pobarvata vse znotraj krogle, tudi zaobljene robove in votline, kot krogla v razrezovalnikih.
– Magnetni lasso: klikaj približno vzdolž roba in črta ga sama najde: pregib površine, obris telesa ali rob že obstoječe barve. Enter ali klik na prvo točko ga sklene, Backspace vzame točko nazaj.
– Glajenje: izravna črto obeh lassov, vogali ostanejo.
– Višinski pas: en klik pobarva pas v enaki višini okoli vsega telesa.
– Zapolni vrzeli: lise, manjše od nastavljene površine, prevzamejo barvo okoli sebe, zato zablodeli madeži izginejo.

Nov potez se položi na mrežo, ki so jo pustile prejšnje poteze, namesto da bi se znova uporabile vse, zato barvanje ostane hitro tudi z veliko potezami. Prek MCP »layerling_paint« sprejme čopič, kroglo, višinski pas in zapolnjevanje vrzeli.
- **Gridfinity: razdeli posamezne predale naprej**
  Na kartici predalov škatle Gridfinity meni Row in Column izbereta predal, ki ga je mogoče razdeliti. Z »Split across« in »Split deep« (vsak od 1 do 6) se razdeli na enakomerno razporejene dele s predelnimi stenami, enako debelimi in visokimi kot pri škatli.

Vsak del ostane širok vsaj 6 mm, škatla pa obdrži omejitev skupaj 40 predalov. Čez to se predali jemljejo od zadaj levo po vrstah, najprej njihovi deli počez, tako da datoteka ali klic MCP vedno zgradi enako. Oznaka predala stoji v delu ob njegovi strani, ki je najbolj levo ali zadaj. Škatla brez delitev je zgrajena kot prej.
- **Gridfinity: posode in pladnji tako veliki kot tvoja tiskalna plošča**
  Posode in pladnji Gridfinity so se ustavili pri 6 enotah (252 mm), tudi ko je imel tiskalnik več prostora. Z izbranim tiskalnikom v nastavitvah delovnega prostora »Units across« in »Units deep« zdaj ponudita toliko enot, kolikor jih gre na daljšo stranico njegove plošče, na primer 7 enot na plošči 330 × 320 mm. Širina in dolžina pladnja gresta do te stranice.

Namig pod poljem pove, do kod seže. Brez izbranega tiskalnika ostane pri 6 enotah in 252 mm. Če je posoda večja od plošče, preverjanje tiska to pove.
- **Hollow: luknje za vijake v dnu Gridfinity**
  Pod Hollow > Gridfinity floor je pod »Magnet holes« novo polje »Screw holes«. Izreže luknjo skozi sredino vsakega vtiča, da škatlo privijačiš v predalu ali na desko: 4,5 mm široko za 4 mm vijak z grezno glavo, vgreznjeno pod 45 stopinj, da glava (do 8,5 mm) leži v ravnini z dnom vtiča. Posoda še vedno stoji v vtiču.

Dno je debelo 8,6 mm, enako kot pri luknjah za magnete, zato gresta skupaj, ne da bi bilo spet debelejše. Mere so nespremenljive. Prek MCP: »gridScrews« v »layerling_hollow_object«.
- **Imenska in ikonska oznaka: ravna vložitev**
  Na karticah »Name tag« in »Icon tag« je stikalo »Flat inlay« pod tabelo plasti. Ko je vklopljeno, se črke (ali ikona) in rob pogreznejo v spodnjo plast in vse barve končajo na eni ravni ploskvi. Oznako je tako mogoče tiskati z licem navzdol na plošči, s teksturo plošče na licu.

Spodnja plast je nosilec: njena višina je debelina cele oznake, uho za obesek pa sedi na njej. Drsnik »Inlay depth« določa, kako globoko gredo plasti nad njo, vse skupaj. Začne pri 0,8 mm (štiri plasti po 0,2 mm), kar zadošča za večino PLA. Bolj prosojen filament (PETG, nekatere bele barve) potrebuje več: najprej natisni preizkus.

Medtem ko je stikalo vklopljeno, so višine zgornjih plasti zasivljene. Izklopljeno je oznaka spet naložena. Pri izvozu STEP so vložene plasti mreže, 3MF in STL ostaneta nespremenjena.
- **Nova oblika: Streha**
  Osnovne oblike zdaj imajo Streho ob Okrogli strehi: trikotnik, ki leži na delovni ravnini, z vogalom zgoraj. Ročaj zgoraj spreminja višino trikotnika, medtem ko osnovnica ostane na plošči; ročaji spodaj spreminjajo širino in dolžino.

Njene tri stranice ostanejo take, kot so; za drugo število stranic vzemi Večkotnik.
- **Slika za vsako mapo**
  Ko se ime mape na začetni strani začne z emojijem, na primer »💡 Ideas«, mapa namesto ikone mape pokaže ta emoji velik. Tako mape najdeš na prvi pogled.

Emoji je del imena, zato gre kamor gre mapa. Mapo preimenuj, da ga spremeniš ali odstraniš.
- **Skica: puščične tipke se odzovejo takoj**
  V skici se je ob pritisku puščične tipke premik pokazal šele po kratkem trenutku, pri držani tipki pa je izbor skakal v velikih korakih. Zdaj se premika takoj in enakomerno. Cel niz s puščičnimi tipkami je še vedno en korak Undo.
- **Deli pristanejo tam, kamor jih postavi njihova datoteka**
  Če imaš dele enega modela kot ločene datoteke, na primer telo, roke in noge miniature, v oknu za uvoz vklopi »STL, OBJ, 3MF: keep the position from the file«. Vsak del potem stoji tam, kamor ga postavi njegova datoteka, in vse se ujema, tudi če datoteke uvoziš eno za drugo.

Stikalo ostane vklopljeno, dokler ga ne izklopiš. Brez njega vsaka datoteka pride sama blizu sredine plošče, kot prej.
- **Prazni načrti se vrnejo iz varnostne kopije**
  Po zrušitvi lahko kartice na začetni strani še vedno kažejo imena in število oblik, načrti pa se odprejo prazni. Do zdaj je »Restore backup« natanko te načrte preskočil, ker jih je imel za že obstoječe.

Zdaj kopija njihovo vsebino vrne v isto kartico. Načrti, ki še imajo svoje oblike, ostanejo, kot so.
- **Menijska vrstica: gumbi se ne prekrivajo več**
  Pri nekaterih širinah okna, predvsem v načinu fokusa, so bile skupine menijske vrstice stisnjene ožje od svojih gumbov. Takrat je na primer gumb Outliner pri Visibility segal v Shapes.

Zdaj vsaka skupina obdrži polno širino, kjer pa ni dovolj prostora, se še ena skupina zloži v en sam gumb.
- **Gladka zaobljenja na velikih okroglih robovih**
  Zaobljenje 1 mm ali več na okroglem robu, na primer na robu velikega diska ali obroča, je lahko v urejevalniku in razrezovalniku kazalo poševne vdolbine. Zaobljenje samo je bilo natančno; samo trikotna mreža za pogled in izvoz je mestoma tekla počez krivulje, do nekaj desetink milimetra pod površino.

layerling zdaj preveri mrežo pri vsakem zaobljenju ob okroglem robu in jo po potrebi razdeli finejše. Rob, ki si ga že zaobljil, bo gladek, ko zaobljenje uporabiš še enkrat.
- **Satovje in celice: brez špic ob robovih spojev**
  Rezanje satovja ali celic v ploskev in jemanje vzorca kot samostojnega dela je pustilo majhne špice ob robovih spojev, ki so segale v celice. Izginile so: vzorec zdaj pokriva samo spoje.
- **Rob črk tudi spodaj**
  Pri Text zdaj izbereš, kam gre rob črk: zgoraj, spodaj ali na obeh koncih. Spodnji je za vgravirane črke, ki jih iz telesa izrežeš z besedilom kot luknjo: posnetje, tako veliko kot je globok rez, naredi žlebove v obliki črke V, ki se tiskajo brez mostov, tudi na stranski steni.
- **Safari na Macih Intel: veliki deli spet pravilno narisani**
  V Safariju na Macu s procesorjem Intel in lastno grafično kartico so bili veliki deli narisani kot zmešnjava špic čez pogled, na primer vzorec, ovit okoli valja, ali velika uvožena mreža. Majhni deli so bili videti pravilni, izvoz pa je bil vedno v redu.

Vzrok je napaka v Safariju: od velikih blokov podatkov do grafične kartice tam pride samo prvi megabajt. layerling zdaj velike bloke pošilja Safariju v manjših kosih.
- **Gumbi miške kot v razrezovalniku: vrtenje tudi v orodjih**
  Z gumbi miške, nastavljenimi »Like Bambu Studio and OrcaSlicer«, levi vlek po praznem prostoru ni vrtel pogleda, ko je bilo odprto orodje, kot so Split, Fillet ali Chamfer. Zdaj pogled vrti tudi tam. Navaden klik na prazen prostor še vedno naredi, kar je tam vedno naredil.
- **Koda QR in plošča: premik se ne izgubi med ponovno gradnjo**
  Če si kodo QR naredil znova ali ploščo ponovno zgradil z novo nastavitvijo in jo vmes premaknil, zaklenil ali preimenoval, se je ta sprememba brez besed izgubila. Zdaj del ostane, kot si ga spremenil, obvestilo pa pove, da naj ponovno gradnjo začneš še enkrat.
- **Chamfillet hitrejši pri telesih z veliko robovi**
  Pri telesih z zelo veliko robovi, na primer velika plošča satovja, je Chamfillet zdaj narejen do dvakrat hitreje.
