## What's new in 1.150.0

- **Stencil bridges**
  Cut text through a plate and the middle of o, a and b falls out of the print. The new Stencil bridges tool holds it to the plate with small bridges. You set their width, number and height, and lower bridges sit at the bottom, in the middle or at the top. Each bridge is an ordinary box you can move or delete.
- **Arrows for X and Y**
  Beside the black lift arrow there are now a red and a green arrow. Pull one and the selection moves along X or along Y only, following the mouse along that line, even when you look at the plate from low down. It snaps to other bodies as a drag does, and afterwards you can click the distance and type it exactly.
- **Bayonet lid for round bodies**
  The Lid tool has a sixth kind, Bayonet. A round body gets lugs on the outside of its top and the cap matching L-shaped slots: set it on, push down and turn it a short way clockwise, and it clicks shut. You set the number of lugs, the rim depth, the gaps and the click yourself.
- **Dovetail when splitting**
  Split has the dovetail as a fourth kind of connector: a tongue that widens away from the cut on one half fits a groove in the other, so the halves hold together without glue and cannot be pulled apart across the joint. You set width, depth, flank angle, direction and number; the clearance comes from your fit test.
- **Snap menu with a middle click**
  A short click with the middle mouse button opens the snap grid menu right at the pointer, in the 3D editor and in a sketch. It stays open until you pick a step or click beside it. Dragging with the middle button still moves the view.
- **Support rib at a rounded edge**
  Round or chamfer the edge a tilted part stands on, so the slicer finds its first layer, and the support rib still goes on. Click the flat face above the rounding: it may end up to 3 mm above the plate, and the rib still reaches down to it.
- **The right count after deleting**
  Delete a design in the server store and the count above the designs is right at once. Until now it still counted the deleted design until the page was reloaded.

## Kaj je novega v 1.150.0

- **Mostički za šablone**
  Ko skozi ploščo izrežeš besedilo, sredina črk o, a in b pade iz tiska. Novo orodje »Stencil bridges« jo s tankimi mostički drži na plošči. Nastaviš širino, število in višino, nižji mostički pa so spodaj, na sredini ali zgoraj. Vsak mostiček je navadna škatla, ki jo lahko premakneš ali izbrišeš.
- **Puščici za X in Y**
  Poleg črne puščice za dvig sta zdaj rdeča in zelena. Povleci eno in se izbor premakne samo po X ali samo po Y, za miško po tej premici, tudi ko ploščo gledaš od nizko. Prilepi se na druga telesa kot pri vlečenju, nato pa lahko klikneš razdaljo in jo natančno vpišeš.
- **Bajonetni pokrov za okrogla telesa**
  Orodje »Lid« ima šesto vrsto: bajonet. Okroglo telo dobi zunaj na vrhu izbokline, pokrov pa ustrezne reže v obliki črke L: nataknješ, pritisneš in zavrtiš za kratek kot v smeri urnega kazalca, pa se zaskoči. Število izboklin, globino roba, reže in zaskok nastaviš sam.
- **Lastovičji rep pri deljenju**
  »Split« ima kot četrto vrsto spoja lastovičji rep: jezik, ki se od reza navzven širi, na eni polovici ustreza utoru v drugi, tako da polovici držita skupaj brez lepila in ju čez spoj ni mogoče potegniti narazen. Nastaviš širino, globino, kot bokov, smer in število; režo določi tvoj preizkus prileganja.
- **Meni pripenjanja s srednjim klikom**
  Kratek klik s srednjim gumbom miške odpre meni mreže pripenjanja kar ob kazalcu, v 3D urejevalniku in v skici. Ostane odprt, dokler ne izbereš koraka ali klikneš ob njem. Vlečenje s srednjim gumbom še vedno premika pogled.
- **Podporno rebro ob zaobljenem robu**
  Zaobli ali prirezi rob, na katerem stoji nagnjen del, da rezalnik najde prvo plast, in podporno rebro še vedno pride. Klikni ravno ploskev nad zaobljenjem: lahko je do 3 mm nad ploščo, rebro pa še vedno sega do nje.
- **Pravo število po brisanju**
  Ko izbrišeš načrt v strežniškem shrambi, je število nad načrti takoj pravo. Doslej je izbrisani načrt štel, dokler nisi znova naložil strani.
