## What's new in 1.119.2

- **Cookie cutter: the inside stays**
  The inside of an e or an a now gets a wall of its own, held by bridges at the rim. A text gives its letters' real curves for it.

---

## Kaj je novega v 1.119.2

- **Modelček za piškote: notranjost ostane**
  Notranjost črke e ali a zdaj dobi svojo steno, ki jo ob robu držijo mostički. Besedilo zanjo uporabi prave krivulje svojih črk.
