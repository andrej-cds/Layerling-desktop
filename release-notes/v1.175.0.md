## What's new in 1.175.0

- **Text on the back of name tags and icon tags**
  A phone number or an e-mail address for a lost key ring now fits on the underside. Switch between Front and Back at the top of the card: the view turns with it.

Set the letter height and depth. The letters are sunk in or, if you like, filled flush with a colour of their own for a multicolour print.
- **Icon tag: you give the finished width**
  When you make a tag and on its card, the width now means the finished tag without the key ring tab: 50 mm gives a tag 50 mm wide, and the icon is worked out to fit. The card also shows the finished size, and short lines explain Extra width per side, No holes and Flat inlay.
- **Keep selections under a name**
  Keep selection at the top of the object list keeps the selected objects under a name. A click selects them again, for example to regroup parts after ungrouping. The selections stay with the design and in the .lyl file.
- **Select multiple in the object list on a touch screen**
  On a tablet the object list could only pick one body at a time, as adding needs Ctrl, Cmd or Shift. Now the list follows the round Select multiple button: while it is locked, a tap in the list adds a body or takes it out. A long press on a row starts Select multiple and adds that row. With a mouse and keyboard nothing changes.
- **The rotation pivot snaps to reference points**
  A reference point in the middle of another part was no help to the pivot. Now the red marker snaps to visible reference points as you drag it, and a click on one with Set rotation pivot puts the pivot exactly there. With Shift the drag still takes the centre of a face.
- **Spiral spring as in a clock**
  The spring has a third form: Spiral, a band wound flat. Set the windings, the band, the inner diameter, the direction and the ends; the gap between the windings is shown.
- **A double click adds a point**
  In the sketch, a double click on a line with Select adds a point there, without changing the tool.
- **A reference image shows in the sketch too**
  A reference image of the 3D editor now shows in a sketch as its picture instead of a filled rectangle, with its place, size, turn and see-through setting, so you can draw over it. Tilted pictures and sketches on a face still show the outline only.
- **Build plates in several rows**
  In the plate bar, "Plates in a row" sets how many plates stand side by side: eight plates can stand as two rows of four. The bodies move with their plates.
- **Profiles for the settings**
  At the bottom of the settings window you can save up to twelve profiles, one for each printer for example. Use profile puts it on the open design, with the printer and its plate.
- **Long slider names wrap**
  A slider name that did not fit beside its value field ended in “…”. It now runs onto a second line, in every panel with such sliders.
- **Gridfinity: padding to one side in one click**
  Under a padded baseplate's settings, "All at the back", "All at the front", "All on the left" and "All on the right" each move the whole padding of its direction to its side, so the grid sits against the other wall of the drawer. The plate keeps its size and its place.
- **Hollow a loft**
  The loft from the library, a rectangle at the bottom and a circle at the top, can now be hollowed with walls of 1 or 2 mm too, open at the top, the bottom or both ends, or closed all round.
- **Make exact shows that it is still working**
  The message of Make exact went after a few seconds while the work went on. It now stays until the result or an error takes its place.

When Delete face cannot close a gap although every face is picked, the message now names a way round: fill it with a body of your own, or cut away what sticks out with a hole, and group the two.
- **Your AI opens saved .lyl files by their path**
  With layerling_open_project an AI opens a .lyl file on your computer just by its path, as a new design. Your files can stay in your own folders, and layerling_save_project writes the changes back there.

## Kaj je novega v 1.175.0

- **Besedilo na hrbtni strani imenskih in ikonskih oznak**
  Telefonska številka ali e-naslov za izgubljen obesek za ključe zdaj gre na spodnjo stran. Med Front in Back preklopiš zgoraj na kartici: pogled se obrne z njo.

Nastavi višino in globino črk. Črke so vdelane ali, če želiš, zapolnjene poravnano s svojo barvo za večbarvni tisk.
- **Ikonska oznaka: ti določiš končno širino**
  Ko narediš oznako in na njeni kartici širina zdaj pomeni končno oznako brez ušesa za obesek: 50 mm da oznako 50 mm široko, ikona pa se prilagodi. Kartica kaže tudi končno velikost, kratke vrstice pa razložijo Extra width per side, No holes in Flat inlay.
- **Ohrani izbore pod imenom**
  »Keep selection« na vrhu seznama predmetov ohrani izbrane predmete pod imenom. Klik jih spet izbere, na primer za ponovno združitev delov po razdružitvi. Izbori ostanejo pri načrtu in v datoteki .lyl.
- **Izbira več predmetov v seznamu na zaslonu na dotik**
  Na tabličnem računalniku je seznam predmetov lahko izbral samo eno telo naenkrat, ker dodajanje potrebuje Ctrl, Cmd ali Shift. Zdaj seznam sledi okroglemu gumbu »Select multiple«: dokler je zaklenjen, dotik v seznamu doda telo ali ga vzame ven. Dolg pritisk na vrstico začne Select multiple in doda to vrstico. Z miško in tipkovnico se nič ne spremeni.
- **Vrtišče vrtenja se pripne na referenčne točke**
  Referenčna točka na sredini drugega dela vrtišču ni pomagala. Zdaj se rdeča oznaka pri vlečenju pripne na vidne referenčne točke, klik nanjo z »Set rotation pivot« pa postavi vrtišče natanko tja. S Shiftom vlečenje še vedno vzame središče ploskve.
- **Spiralna vzmet kot v uri**
  Vzmet ima tretjo obliko: Spiral, trak, navit ploščato. Nastavi zavoje, trak, notranji premer, smer in konca; vrzel med zavoji je prikazana.
- **Dvoklik doda točko**
  V skici dvoklik na črto z orodjem Select tam doda točko, brez spremembe orodja.
- **Referenčna slika se vidi tudi v skici**
  Referenčna slika 3D-urejevalnika se v skici zdaj pokaže kot slika namesto zapolnjenega pravokotnika, z njenim mestom, velikostjo, zasukom in prosojnostjo, tako da lahko rišeš čez njo. Nagnjene slike in skice na ploskvi še vedno pokažejo samo obris.
- **Gradbene plošče v več vrstah**
  V vrstici plošč »Plates in a row« določa, koliko plošč stoji drug ob drugem: osem plošč lahko stoji kot dve vrsti po štiri. Telesa se premaknejo s svojimi ploščami.
- **Profili za nastavitve**
  Na dnu okna nastavitev lahko shraniš do dvanajst profilov, na primer enega za vsak tiskalnik. »Use profile« ga postavi na odprt načrt, s tiskalnikom in njegovo ploščo.
- **Dolga imena drsnikov se zlomijo**
  Ime drsnika, ki ni šlo ob polje z vrednostjo, se je končalo z »…«. Zdaj teče v drugo vrstico, v vsaki plošči s takimi drsniki.
- **Gridfinity: obroba na eno stran z enim klikom**
  Pod nastavitvami osnovne plošče z obrobo »All at the back«, »All at the front«, »All on the left« in »All on the right« premaknejo vso obrobo svoje smeri na svojo stran, tako da mreža sedi ob drugi steni predala. Plošča obdrži velikost in mesto.
- **Hollow na loftu**
  Loft iz knjižnice, pravokotnik spodaj in krog zgoraj, je zdaj mogoče narediti votlo tudi s stenami 1 ali 2 mm, odprt zgoraj, spodaj ali na obeh koncih ali zaprt okoli in okoli.
- **Make exact pokaže, da še dela**
  Sporočilo Make exact je po nekaj sekundah izginilo, medtem ko je delo teklo. Zdaj ostane, dokler ga ne zamenja rezultat ali napaka.

Ko Delete face ne more zapreti vrzeli, čeprav je izbrana vsaka ploskev, sporočilo zdaj pove obhod: zapolni jo z lastnim telesom ali odreži, kar moli ven, z luknjo, in obe združi.
- **Tvoj AI odpre shranjene datoteke .lyl po poti**
  S »layerling_open_project« AI odpre datoteko .lyl na tvojem računalniku samo po poti, kot nov načrt. Tvoje datoteke lahko ostanejo v tvojih mapah, »layerling_save_project« pa spremembe zapiše nazaj tja.
