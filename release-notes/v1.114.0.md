## What's new in 1.114.0

- **Your default stays on the server**
  When layerling runs with a server folder, for example in Docker, "Make default" now saves there too. Clear the browser storage or open layerling in another browser, and new designs still start with your own settings.
- **Corner radius for Gridfinity bins**
  A Gridfinity bin's outer corners no longer have to be rounded by 3.75 mm: set "Corner radius" in the Properties panel, down to 0 for square corners, so where four bins meet in a drawer only the 0.5 mm gaps between them are left. The feet and the inside stay as they are, so bins fit and stack as before.

---

## Kaj je novega v 1.114.0

- **Tvoje privzeto ostane na strežniku**
  Ko Layerling teče z mapo na strežniku, na primer v Dockerju, »Naredi za privzeto« zdaj shrani tudi tja. Počisti shrambo brskalnika ali odpri Layerling v drugem brskalniku, in novi projekti se še vedno začnejo z tvojimi nastavitvami.
- **Polmer oglišč posod Gridfinity**
  Zunanja oglišča posode Gridfinity ne smejo biti več nujno zaobljena s 3,75 mm: v podoknu Lastnosti nastavi »Polmer oglišč«, vse do 0 za ostra oglišča, tako da pri štirih posodah v predalu ostanejo le 0,5 mm vrzeli med njimi. Noge in notranjost ostanejo, kot so, zato posode pasejo in se zlagajo kot prej.
