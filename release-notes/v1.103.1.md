## What's new in 1.103.1

- **Several objects turn rigidly, even past the edge**
  When several objects were turned with the rotate handle, each centre was rounded to the grid and kept inside the workplane, so objects near the edge got stuck and the arrangement broke. The group now turns exactly around its pivot and keeps its spacing, even when parts move past the edge of the plane.
- **Sketch: keys 1 to 3 also change a selection**
  With the Select tool and lines or points selected, key 1 makes lines straight and points corners, 2 turns lines into curves and 3 makes points smooth, all selected ones at once. With nothing selected the keys still pick the drawing tool. The keys are named in the tooltips of the buttons.
- **Fit a Gridfinity baseplate to a drawer**
  A Gridfinity baseplate now has "Drawer width" and "Drawer depth" in the Properties panel: type the drawer's inside size, and the plate takes as many units as fit and splits the rest evenly into its padding, so it comes out 0.5 mm smaller than the drawer.

## Kaj je novega v 1.103.1

- **Več predmetov se vrti togo, tudi čez rob**
  Ko si z vrtilnim ročajem vrtel več predmetov, se je središče vsakega zaokrožilo na mrežo in ostalo znotraj delovne ravnine, zato so predmeti ob robu obtičali in razporeditev se je podrla. Skupina se zdaj vrti natančno okoli svojega vrtišča in obdrži razmike, tudi ko deli zaidejo čez rob ravnine.
- **Skica: tipke 1 do 3 spremenijo tudi izbor**
  Z orodjem Izberi in izbranimi črtami ali točkami tipka 1 naredi črte ravne in točke oglišča, 2 spremeni črte v krivulje, 3 pa točke zgladi – vse izbrane naenkrat. Brez izbora tipke še naprej izberejo risalno orodje. Tipke so zapisane v namigih gumbov.
- **Podstavek Gridfinity po meri predala**
  Podstavek Gridfinity ima zdaj v oknu Lastnosti »Širina predala« in »Globina predala«: vpiši notranjo mero predala, podstavek vzame toliko enot, kolikor jih gre noter, preostanek pa enakomerno razdeli v pas, tako da je 0,5 mm manjši od predala.
