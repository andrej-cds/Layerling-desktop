## What's new in 1.179.0

- **LayerLab: donations and support**
  The donate button and the support page now say LayerLab by CDS. The release files are called LayerLab-Setup-… and LayerLab-….dmg.

- **Two new latches for lids**
  The lid has two new kinds of latch, both printed in place on the box:

– Draw latches as on a tool case: push the lever to the wall, it snaps shut and pulls the lid tight.
– Turn buttons as on a cupboard door: a quarter turn over the lid's edge holds the lid.

You now pick the kind by picture tiles that show each latch closed, seen from the side.
- **The clip printed on the lid swings freely**
  With "Print the clips on the lid" the clip brushed the wall and the lid's edge while closing and had to be filed. The pins now stand a bit further out, and the clip swings clear all the way.
- **Straight into the slicer, without a download**
  When a slicer plugin shows layerling in a tab of its own, the export window offers "Send to …" next to the download, for example "Send to OrcaSlicer". It sends the file the window would write, and only when you click. In an ordinary browser tab nothing changes.

Today this works with OrcaSlicer 2.5 or newer through the plugin orca-layerling, a separate project, for Linux only so far. The link is in the guide.
- **Split groups keep their parts**
  So far splitting a group turned it into two meshes, and its parts were gone.

Now it becomes two groups. What the plane does not cut stays as it was, and "Ungroup" gives the parts of each half back. With connectors a group is still cut as one body.
- **Align faces: centred if you like**
  So far "Align faces" turned the part parallel and slid it only towards the other face; sideways it stayed where it was.

New for "Face to face" and "Flush" is the box "Centre the faces": the middles of the two faces then lie over each other as well, in one step. A gap you set stays.
- **Only the face you click**
  "Sketch from face" sometimes took more than the face you clicked, such as a cross-section through the whole part. Now only its outline and its holes go into the sketch.
- **Percentages and sums in the number fields**
  Under Length, Width and Height a line in small print now says what the number fields take: a percentage such as "50 %" (40 mm becomes 20 mm) and sums such as 40+2 or (140+2)/2. That worked before, but it was only in the guide.
- **Windows dragged low keep their buttons**
  If you drag a tool window such as "Fillet edges" far down to see the shape behind it, it used to shrink to its title bar, and the next tool opened there just as small.

Now the title, a few rows and the buttons always stay in view. That helps most on a tablet.
- **No more hollow walls from a part with gaps**
  When one part had gaps in its surface, grouping it with holes could leave the walls of the other parts hollow too. layerling now refuses to group such a mix and names the open part.

A chamfer or fillet that would tear such gaps into a closed mesh is not applied.
- **Fixes from a full check**
  A check of the whole program found a few bugs that are now fixed:

– Ctrl+F opens the browser's search again in the 3D view.
– Home in the history view no longer resets the camera.
– If the CAD kernel crashes it restarts, instead of showing a misleading message.
– "Lid" and "Mold" no longer hang after an error.
– On very low screens the buttons of moved tool windows stay within reach.
– Tracing a detailed picture no longer stalls the page for seconds.
– A long bent tube is now checked over its whole length for crossing itself.
– Arduino UNO and MEGA: the digital pin headers are now named correctly.
– A snap-fit stays within its size at large values too, and the panel says when a value was reduced.
– The vase drawing window lets go of the reference photo when it closes.
– A Gridfinity bin with an overhang no longer shows without the overhang or its chamfer.
– Gridfinity baseplates with the GridFlock puzzle no longer have faulty edges in their mesh, for example in the STL file.

## Kaj je novega v 1.179.0

- **LayerLab: donacije in podpora**
  Gumb za donacije in stran za podporo se zdaj imenujeta LayerLab by CDS. Datoteke izdaje se imenujejo LayerLab-Setup-… in LayerLab-….dmg.

- **Dva nova zapaha za pokrove**
  Pokrov ima dva nova tipa zapaha, oba natisnjena na mestu na škatli.

– Vlečni zapahi kot na kovčku z orodjem: potisni ročico k steni, zaskoči se in pokrov potegne tesno.
– Vrtljivi gumbi kot na vratih omare: četrt obrata čez rob pokrova drži pokrov.

Tip zdaj izbereš s slikovnimi ploščicami, ki kažejo vsak zapah zaprt, gledan s strani.
- **Sponka, natisnjena na pokrovu, se prosto zavrti**
  Pri »Print the clips on the lid« se je sponka med zapiranjem dotikala stene in roba pokrova, zato jo je bilo treba pilit. Čepi zdaj stojijo malo dlje navzven in sponka se zavrti povsem prosto.
- **Naravnost v rezalnik, brez prenosa**
  Kadar vtičnik rezalnika prikazuje layerling v svojem zavihku, okno za izvoz ob prenosu ponudi »Send to …«, na primer »Send to OrcaSlicer«. Pošlje datoteko, ki bi jo okno zapisalo, in samo ko klikneš. V navadnem zavihku brskalnika se nič ne spremeni.

Danes to deluje z OrcaSlicer 2.5 ali novejšim prek vtičnika orca-layerling, ločenega projekta, zaenkrat samo za Linux. Povezava je v navodilih.
- **Razdeljene skupine ohranijo dele**
  Doslej se je skupina z razdelitvijo spremenila v dve mreži in njeni deli so izginili.

Zdaj nastaneta dve skupini. Kar ravnina ne prereže, ostane, kot je bilo, »Ungroup« pa vrne dele vsake polovice. S konektorji se skupina še vedno razreže kot eno telo.
- **Align faces: po želji na sredino**
  Doslej je »Align faces« del obrnil vzporedno in ga premaknil samo proti drugi ploskvi; vstran je ostal, kjer je bil.

Novo je za »Face to face« in »Flush« polje »Centre the faces«: sredini obeh ploskev potem ležita tudi ena nad drugo, v enem koraku. Razmik, ki ga nastaviš, ostane.
- **Samo ploskev, ki jo klikneš**
  »Sketch from face« je včasih vzel več kot ploskev, na katero si kliknil, na primer prerez skozi cel del. Zdaj gresta v skico samo njen obris in njene luknje.
- **Odstotki in vsote v poljih za številke**
  Pod Length, Width in Height vrstica z drobnim tiskom zdaj pove, kaj polja za številke sprejmejo: odstotek, na primer »50 %« (40 mm postane 20 mm), in vsote, na primer 40+2 ali (140+2)/2. To je delovalo že prej, vendar je bilo zapisano samo v navodilih.
- **Okna, povlečena nizko, obdržijo gumbe**
  Če si orodno okno, na primer »Fillet edges«, povlekel daleč navzdol, da vidiš obliko za njim, se je skrčilo na naslovno vrstico in naslednje orodje, odprto tam, prav tako.

Zdaj naslov, nekaj vrstic in gumbi ostanejo vedno v pogledu. To najbolj pomaga na tabličnem računalniku.
- **Konec votlih sten zaradi dela z vrzelmi**
  Kadar je imel en del v površini vrzeli, so lahko pri združevanju z luknjami ostale votle tudi stene drugih delov. Layerling zdaj takšno mešanico zavrne in poimenuje odprt del.

Posnetje ali zaokrožitev, ki bi takšne vrzeli raztrgala v zaprto mrežo, se ne uveljavi.
- **Popravki po celotnem pregledu**
  Pregled celotnega programa je našel nekaj napak, ki so zdaj popravljene:

– Ctrl+F v 3D pogledu spet odpre iskanje brskalnika.
– Home v pogledu zgodovine ne ponastavi več kamere.
– Če se jedro CAD zruši, se znova zažene, namesto da pokaže zavajajoče sporočilo.
– »Lid« in »Mold« po napaki ne obvisita več.
– Na zelo nizkih zaslonih gumbi premaknjenih orodnih oken ostanejo dosegljivi.
– Sledenje podrobni sliki ne ustavi strani za več sekund.
– Dolga upognjena cev se zdaj preveri po vsej dolžini, ali se sama križa.
– Arduino UNO in MEGA: digitalne priključne letve so zdaj pravilno poimenovane.
– Zaskočni spoj ostane v svoji velikosti tudi pri velikih vrednostih, plošča pa pove, kdaj je bila vrednost zmanjšana.
– Okno za risanje vaze spusti referenčno fotografijo, ko se zapre.
– Gridfinity predal s previsom se ne prikaže več brez previsa ali njegovega posnetja.
– Gridfinity osnovne plošče s sestavljanko GridFlock nimajo več napačnih robov v mreži, na primer v datoteki STL.
