## What's new in 1.122.0

- **Undo says what it takes back**
  Point at Undo or Redo and the tooltip names the step with its measure, such as "Rounded the edges of Box (5 mm)", and the message after Ctrl+Z says what was undone.
- **Sketch from the cut**
  In the section view, "Sketch from cut" starts a new sketch right on the cut with its outlines already in it, holes as true circles. A lid, an insert or a gasket then starts from the outline of the part it has to fit.
- **Mouse buttons like your slicer, and a 3D mouse**
  Under Appearance in the settings, the mouse buttons can now work as in Bambu Studio and OrcaSlicer: a left drag on empty space turns the view, the right button shifts it. And a SpaceMouse steers the view in Chrome and Edge once it is connected there.
- **Hirth joint**
  A new shape next to the gears: a disc with V-shaped teeth running out from the centre. A copy turned over locks into it at every tooth and centres itself, for a hinge you can set at an angle or a knob that clicks into place.

## Kaj je novega v 1.122.0

- **Razveljavi pove, kaj vrača**
  Če pokažeš na Razveljavi ali Uveljavi, namig poimenuje korak z mero, na primer »Zaobljeni robovi škatle (5 mm)«, sporočilo po Ctrl+Z pa pove, kaj je bilo razveljavljeno.
- **Skica iz reza**
  V pogledu prereza »Skica iz reza« začne novo skico kar na rezu, z obrisi že v njej, luknje kot prave krožnice. Pokrov, vložek ali tesnilo se tako začne z obrisom dela, v katerega mora pasti.
- **Gumbi miške kot v tvojem rezalniku in 3D miška**
  V nastavitvah pod Videz lahko gumbi miške delujejo kot v Bambu Studiu in OrcaSlicerju: levi poteg po praznem prostoru vrti pogled, desni gumb ga premika. SpaceMouse pa krmili pogled v Chromu in Edgeu, ko je tam povezan.
- **Hirthov spoj**
  Nova oblika ob zobnikih: disk z zobmi v obliki črke V, ki tečejo od središča. Obrnjena kopija se zaskoči vanj pri vsakem zobu in se sama centrira, za tečaj, ki ga nastaviš pod kotom, ali gumb, ki se zaskoči na mestu.
