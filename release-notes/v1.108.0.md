## What's new in 1.108.0

- **Patterns group their pieces**
  The Pattern window has "Group the copies": the original and all copies then become one object, so a ring of 36 pieces is one part. Above 5 pieces the box ticks itself, and you can untick it at any time.
- **Select shapes that are hidden behind others**
  A right click now offers "Select other…" when several shapes lie under the pointer: the list shows them from front to back, the one you point at is outlined even through the others, and a click selects it. So you reach a hole inside a body without turning the view.
- **The framing square stays where you put it**
  A placed framing square now belongs to the design: after a reload or the next time you open it, it lies in the same place, turned the same way and measuring from the same points, also from a .lyl file. The × removes it.
- **Attachments: instructions and parts lists in the design**
  Manage now has "Attachments" (the paperclip): attach Markdown or text files to a design, such as assembly instructions or a parts list. They stay with the design, also in the .lyl file, the backup and the server folder. A design with attachments needs this layerling version or a newer one to open.
- **Align in the sketch**
  Select two or more outlines in a sketch and you can now line them up: left, centred, right, top or bottom. Two circles centred both ways become a clean ring.
- **Select several bodies on a tablet**
  Tap a body on a tablet and a round button appears in the lower left. Tap it, and every further body you tap joins the selection, as with the Shift key on a computer. A second tap on the button ends it.

## Kaj je novega v 1.108.0

- **Polja združijo svoje dele**
  Okno Polje ima »Združi kopije«: original in vse kopije postanejo en predmet, tako da je krog s 36 deli en del. Pri več kot 5 delih se kljukica vklopi sama, izklopiš jo lahko kadar koli.
- **Izberi oblike, skrite za drugimi**
  Desni klik zdaj ponudi »Izberi drugo …«, kadar leži pod kazalcem več oblik: seznam jih pokaže od spredaj nazaj, tista, na katero kažeš, je obrobljena tudi skozi druge, klik pa jo izbere. Tako prideš do luknje v telesu, ne da bi obračal pogled.
- **Kotnik ostane tam, kamor ga postaviš**
  Postavljen kotnik zdaj pripada projektu: po ponovnem nalaganju ali ob naslednjem odpiranju leži na istem mestu, obrnjen enako in meri od istih točk, tudi iz datoteke .lyl. Z × ga odstraniš.
- **Priloge: navodila in seznami delov v projektu**
  Upravljanje ima zdaj »Priloge« (sponka): projektu pripni datoteke Markdown ali besedilne datoteke, na primer navodila za sestavo ali seznam delov. Ostanejo pri projektu, tudi v datoteki .lyl, varnostni kopiji in mapi na strežniku. Projekt s prilogami za odpiranje potrebuje to ali novejšo različico layerlinga.
- **Poravnava v skici**
  Izberi dva ali več obrisov v skici in jih zdaj lahko poravnaš: levo, na sredino, desno, zgoraj ali spodaj. Dva kroga, poravnana na sredino v obeh smereh, postaneta čist obroč.
- **Izbira več teles na tablici**
  Tapni telo na tablici in spodaj levo se pojavi okrogel gumb. Tapni ga in vsako naslednje telo, ki ga tapneš, se pridruži izboru, kot s tipko Shift na računalniku. Drugi tap na gumb konča izbiranje.
