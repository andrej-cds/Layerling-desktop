## What's new in 1.103.0

- **Save STL as a plain download**
  In Chrome and Edge the STL export writes into the same file each time. In the settings under Appearance you can now switch on "Save STL as a download": you then get a plain download and the browser numbers the files itself, name (1).stl, name (2).stl.
- **Back to your own settings**
  A file you open brings its own colours, grid and plate. Next to "Make default" the settings now have "Use my default": it puts your saved colours, grid, shadows, camera and shape starts on the open design. The size of the plate, the printer and the units stay as they are.
- **Scale by percent from the keyboard**
  The percentage field has the focus when the window opens, and Enter applies the scale. Ctrl+K, the percentage and Enter do it without the mouse.
- **Material estimate on a click**
  The Material box in the export window now works out its numbers only when you press Calculate. For finely traced multicolour models the Export button stays usable at once.
- **Cone radius kept when ungrouping**
  A cone that was grouped with other parts and made larger now keeps its larger base radius when the group is ungrouped.
- **The revolve preview turns with the mouse**
  In the sketch with Revolve, the small 3D preview is no longer a still picture: drag with the left or the right button to turn it, use the wheel to zoom and the wheel button to move it, so you can see the underside too. The view you set stays while you keep drawing.
- **Face tool: the new part as a body of its own**
  The Face window has a new switch, "As a body of its own (not grouped)". With it on, the part pushed out of the face (or the hole of a pocket) stands beside the body instead of in a group with it: resize it, move it or use it to cut right away.
- **Ctrl+arrows slide the sketch**
  With nothing selected in the sketch editor, Ctrl and the arrow keys slide the view, as in the 3D view: a tenth of the view a press, half with Shift as well. The sketch moves the way the arrow points.
- **Padding for Gridfinity baseplates**
  A Gridfinity baseplate can now have a solid border on any side, so it fills a drawer that is not a whole number of units: set "Padding on the right" or another side in the Properties panel. The grid stays whole units, and the plate's size grows with the padding.
- **Loft: segments per circle**
  The circles and rounded corners of a Loft were always built from 96 pieces, so on a large round end the facets showed. The new field "Segments per circle" (16 to 360) sets it, in the view and in STL, OBJ and 3MF.
- **A scaled star keeps its shape**
  When a star was resized only its outer size changed, so a smaller star came out less pointy and a larger one more pointy. The inner size and the roundings now stretch with it.

## Kaj je novega v 1.103.0

- **STL kot navaden prenos**
  V Chromu in Edgeu izvoz STL vsakič piše v isto datoteko. V nastavitvah pod Videz zdaj vklopiš »Shrani STL kot prenos«: dobiš navaden prenos, brskalnik pa datoteke oštevilči sam, ime (1).stl, ime (2).stl.
- **Nazaj na svoje nastavitve**
  Odprta datoteka prinese svoje barve, mrežo in ploščo. V nastavitvah je zdaj ob »Nastavi kot privzeto« tudi »Uporabi mojo privzeto«: na odprt osnutek uporabi tvoje shranjene barve, mrežo, sence, kamero in začetne vrednosti oblik. Velikost plošče, tiskalnik in enote ostanejo, kot so.
- **Pomanjšanje in povečanje v odstotkih s tipkovnico**
  Polje za odstotke ima ob odprtju fokus, Enter pa uporabi spremembo velikosti. Ctrl+K, odstotek, Enter: povsem brez miške.
- **Ocena materiala na klik**
  Polje Material v oknu za izvoz izračuna številke šele, ko klikneš Izračunaj. Pri fino posnetih večbarvnih modelih je gumb Izvozi tako takoj uporaben.
- **Polmer stožca ostane pri razdružitvi skupine**
  Stožec, ki je bil združen z drugimi deli in povečan, ob razdružitvi skupine obdrži tudi svoj večji polmer osnovne ploskve.
- **Predogled vrtenine se vrti z miško**
  V skici z vrtenjem majhen 3D-predogled ni več mirujoča slika: z levim ali desnim gumbom ga zavrtiš, s kolescem povečuješ, s sredinskim gumbom ga premakneš, tako vidiš tudi spodnjo stran. Nastavljen pogled ostane, medtem ko rišeš naprej.
- **Ploskev: nov del kot lastno telo**
  Okno Ploskev ima novo stikalo »Kot lastno telo (ne združeno)«. Ko je vklopljeno, izvlečeni del (ali luknja žepa) stoji ob telesu, ne v skupini z njim: takoj mu lahko spremeniš velikost, ga premakneš ali z njim rezeš.
- **Ctrl+puščice premikajo skico**
  Če v urejevalniku skic ni nič izbrano, Ctrl in puščice premikajo pogled kot v 3D-pogledu: desetino slike na pritisk, s Shiftom polovico. Skica se premakne v smeri puščice.
- **Pas za osnovne plošče Gridfinity**
  Osnovna plošča Gridfinity lahko zdaj dobi poln pas na kateri koli strani, da zapolni predal, ki ni celo število enot: v oknu Lastnosti nastavi »Pas desno« ali drugo stran. Mreža ostane v celih enotah, plošča pa se poveča za pas.
- **Loft: odsekov na krog**
  Krogi in zaobljeni vogali pri Loftu so bili vedno sestavljeni iz 96 kosov, zato so se pri velikih okroglih koncih videle fasete. Novo polje »Odsekov na krog« (16 do 360) to nastavi, v pogledu in v STL, OBJ in 3MF.
- **Povečana zvezda ohrani obliko**
  Pri spremembi velikosti zvezde je zrasel samo zunanji polmer, zato je bila manjša zvezda manj koničasta, večja pa bolj. Notranji polmer in zaobljenja zdaj rastejo z njim.
