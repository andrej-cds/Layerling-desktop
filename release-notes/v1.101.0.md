## What's new in 1.101.0

- **Take your Tinkercad designs with you**
  A member of the community has made a free bookmark, Export to layerling, that converts a Tinkercad design in your browser into a layerling file with its real shapes. It is a project of its own, not part of layerling, and we have not tested it ourselves yet - we are curious how it works for you. You find it in the guide under Files and saving and on the start page.
- **A second view in its own window**
  The new button in the camera bar opens a second window that shows the same design through a camera of its own. It follows every change, has its own view cube and camera bar, and opens where you left it.
- **SVG with the edges seen from above**
  The SVG export has a new view next to the silhouette, "Visible edges from above": the edges you see from above as lines in the colour of their body, with pockets, engraved and raised text and parts standing on others. Every area has a closed outline, also where two bodies meet, and engraves in a laser program without taking in the places of other bodies. A design with too many edges gets the silhouette, and the message says so. The silhouette stays the default.
- **The mouse wheel zooms properly again**
  If Windows is set to scroll one screen at a time per wheel notch (or in Firefox), the wheel barely zoomed in the 3D view, whatever the zoom speed. A notch now zooms like on any other wheel. In the workspace settings, long labels no longer run over the next row.

## Kaj je novega v 1.101.0

- **Svoje načrte iz Tinkercada vzemi s sabo**
  Član skupnosti je naredil brezplačno zaznamek, Export to layerling, ki načrt iz Tinkercada v tvojem brskalniku pretvori v datoteko layerling z njegovimi pravimi oblikami. To je samostojen projekt in ni del layerlinga, mi pa ga še nismo preizkusili – zanima nas, kako deluje pri tebi. Najdeš ga v vodniku pod Datoteke in shranjevanje ter na začetni strani.
- **Drugi pogled v svojem oknu**
  Nov gumb v vrstici kamere odpre drugo okno, ki kaže isti načrt skozi svojo kamero. Sledi vsaki spremembi, ima svojo kocko za usmerjanje in vrstico kamere ter se odpre tam, kjer si ga pustil.
- **SVG z robovi, gledanimi od zgoraj**
  Izvoz SVG ima poleg silhuete nov pogled »Vidni robovi od zgoraj«: robovi, ki jih vidiš od zgoraj, kot črte v barvi svojega telesa, z žepi, vgraviranim in dvignjenim besedilom ter deli, ki stojijo na drugih. Vsaka površina ima zaprt obris, tudi tam, kjer se dve telesi srečata, in se v laserskem programu gravira brez mest drugih teles. Načrt s preveč robovi dobi silhueto, sporočilo pa to pove. Silhueta ostaja privzeta.
- **Kolesce miške spet pravilno povečuje**
  Če je Windows nastavljen na drsenje za cel zaslon na kliku kolesca (ali v Firefoxu), kolesce v 3D pogledu skoraj ni povečevalo, ne glede na hitrost povečave. Klik zdaj povečuje kot na vsakem drugem kolescu. V nastavitvah delovnega prostora dolge oznake ne prekrivajo več naslednje vrstice.
