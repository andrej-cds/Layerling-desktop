## What's new in 1.121.1

- **Matching icons, Polish complete again**
  Flexi joints, Cookie cutter and Lid now have icons in the ribbon that match the other tools. The Polish interface and guide are up to date again, and the parts of a cookie cutter (wall, rim, inner walls, bridges, plate) are named in your language.

---

## Kaj je novega v 1.121.1

- **Ujemajoče ikone, poljščina spet popolna**
  Gibljivi spoji, Rezalnik za piškote in Pokrov imajo zdaj v traku ikone, ki se ujemajo z drugimi orodji. Poljski vmesnik in navodila so spet posodobljeni, deli rezalnika za piškote (stena, rob, notranje stene, mostički, plošča) pa so poimenovani v tvojem jeziku.
