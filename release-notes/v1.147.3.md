## What's new in 1.147.3

- **Add a picture to a folder**
  The "Add picture" command in a server folder's menu (three dots) was missing in 1.147.2. It is there now, together with "Change picture" and "Remove picture".

---

## Kaj je novega v 1.147.3

- **Slika za mapo**
  Ukaza »Add picture« v meniju mape na strežniku (tri pike) v 1.147.2 ni bilo. Zdaj je tam, skupaj z ukazoma »Change picture« in »Remove picture«.
