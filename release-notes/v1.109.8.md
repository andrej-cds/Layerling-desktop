## What's new in 1.109.8

Only the desktop part changes in this release (layerling stays at 1.109.0).

- **Open in Fusion: fewer crashes right after Fusion starts**
  When Fusion was not running, the import came while its Home page was still loading, and Fusion sometimes crashed a few seconds later. The import now waits 15 more seconds after Fusion has finished loading. A cold start therefore takes a little longer.

---

## Kaj je novega v 1.109.8

V tej izdaji se spremeni samo namizni del (layerling ostaja pri 1.109.0).

- **Odpiranje v Fusionu: manj sesutij takoj po zagonu Fusiona**
  Če Fusion ni tekel, je uvoz prišel, ko se je domača stran še nalagala, in Fusion se je nekaj sekund pozneje včasih sesul. Uvoz zdaj po koncu nalaganja počaka še 15 sekund. Hladen zagon je zato nekoliko daljši.
