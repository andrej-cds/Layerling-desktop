## What's new in 1.86.0

- **The Face tool starts from its defaults**
  The Face tool opened with the values from last time, even when nothing was created, so an inset that fitted one face could be too wide for the next. Now it starts each time with an inset of 0, no corner radius, a depth of 2 mm and “Out: add material”.
- **A chapter for tablets**
  The guide has a new chapter “Tablet and touch”: gestures, selecting several bodies, the long press menu, the stylus and what to know on an iPad. On a touch device a hint on the start page points to it once.
- **Delete face**
  The new tool "Delete face" takes a fillet, a hole or a boss off a body again: click its faces, then "Delete", and the faces around them close the gap. The body stays exact, so Fillet, Chamfer and the STEP export still work on it.
- **Custom shapes hang on the pointer**
  A click on a custom shape put it into the middle of the workplane at once. Now it hangs on the pointer like the shapes of the library, follows it also over the faces of other bodies, and a click sets it down. Esc puts it away.
- **A design that is too big is saved anyway**
  A design with very large meshes and a long history failed to save after almost every change, with only the note that the shapes could not be saved. Now the message names the reason (too big for the project file, or no room left in the browser). If only the whole history is too much, the stored copy keeps the newest steps that fit, and says so once. Undo in the open session still goes through every step.
- **A line round a traced sketch no longer times out**
  A sketch from an SVG with many small curves (a traced drawing) stopped with “OpenCascade timed out while building the sketch” as soon as a line was set round it. The curves are now cut into pieces only as finely as 0.01 mm needs, and the validity check, which took minutes with very many edges, is left out for sketches that large. Such a body now builds in a few seconds instead of a minute.
- **Interactive Help hits more buttons**
  For some buttons Interactive Help found nothing or led to the wrong section: Paste, Delete, Ungroup, Workspace settings, Theme, the footer and others. The first chapter of the guide now has a section, The buttons around the work area, and the help leads there.
- **F zooms to the selection, as in Tinkercad**
  The F key now zooms to what is selected, and brings the whole scene into view when nothing is selected. Until now F always showed the whole scene, and the selection needed Shift+F, which still works. The whole scene is always shown by the Home key and the house button. The sketch works the same way.
- **A twisted star stays straight**
  A star that you twist over its height turned about the middle of its box instead of its own middle. As a five-pointed star is not centred in its box, its middle circled round the axis and the tips stuck out by different amounts. Now the star turns about its own middle and the axis stays straight.
- **The view follows bodies set high above the plate**
  Since a height no longer ends at 220 mm, the view could not take a body high above the plate into its middle: Zoom to the selection let it slip off the top edge, and the pivot stayed stuck at 120 mm. The pivot now reaches up to the top of the highest body (and down to the foot of the lowest), so the view zooms, shifts and turns up there as well.
- **Install layerling as an app**
  The start page and the editor (there after half a minute of work) now have a card that recommends installing layerling as an app: it starts faster, works without internet and does not have to be loaded each time. You lose nothing, as layerling still tells you about new versions. "Not now" hides the card for two weeks.
- **Open sketch line: the message names the way**
  An open line in the sketch cannot be extruded, as the outline has to be closed. The message now adds that you can build the line as a stroke: tick "Build as a stroke" in the Finish section and it gets a width.
- **French: better names for five shapes**
  The counterbores, the bent tube and the half cylinder now carry the names they have in a French workshop: Trou lamé, Trou fraisé, Trou doublement fraisé, Tube cintré and Demi-cylindre. The "stroke" was also called a stroke in the medical sense by mistake; it is "Trait" now.

## Kaj je novega v 1.86.0

- **Orodje »Ploskev« se začne s privzetimi vrednostmi**
  Orodje »Ploskev« se je odprlo z vrednostmi iz prejšnjič, tudi če ni nastalo nič, zato je bil odmik, ki je ustrezal eni ploskvi, lahko za naslednjo preširok. Zdaj se vsakič začne z odmikom 0, brez zaokrožitve vogalov, globino 2 mm in »Navzven: dodaj material«.
- **Poglavje za tablice**
  Hitri vodnik ima novo poglavje »Tablica in dotik«: kretnje, izbira več teles, meni dolgega pritiska, pisalo in kar je dobro vedeti na iPadu. Na napravi na dotik na to enkrat opozori namig na začetni strani.
- **Izbriši ploskev**
  Novo orodje »Izbriši ploskev« spet odstrani zaobljenje, luknjo ali izboklino s telesa: klikni njihove ploskve, nato »Izbriši« in sosednje ploskve zapolnijo vrzel. Telo ostane natančno, zato Zaobljenje, Posnetek in izvoz STEP še vedno delujejo na njem.
- **Lastne oblike visijo na kazalcu**
  Klik na lastno obliko jo je takoj postavil na sredino delovne plošče. Zdaj visi na kazalcu kot oblike iz knjižnice, ga spremlja tudi čez ploskve drugih teles, klik pa jo odloži. Esc jo odstrani.
- **Prevelik načrt se vseeno shrani**
  Načrt z zelo velikimi mrežami in dolgo zgodovino se je po skoraj vsaki spremembi nehal shranjevati, s samo obvestilom, da oblik ni mogoče shraniti. Zdaj sporočilo navede razlog (preveliko za datoteko projekta ali v brskalniku ni več prostora). Če je preveč le cela zgodovina, shranjena kopija obdrži najnovejše korake, ki gredo noter, in to enkrat pove. Razveljavi v odprti seji še vedno gre skozi vse korake.
- **Črta okrog vektorizirane skice ne povzroči več časovne omejitve**
  Skica iz SVG z veliko majhnimi krivuljami (vektorizirana risba) se je ustavila z napako »OpenCascade timed out while building the sketch«, takoj ko si okrog nje nastavil črto. Krivulje se zdaj režejo le tako drobno, kot zahteva 0,01 mm, preverjanje veljavnosti, ki je pri zelo veliko robovih trajalo minute, pa pri tako velikih skicah izpustimo. Tako telo se zdaj zgradi v nekaj sekundah namesto v minuti.
- **Interaktivna pomoč zadene več gumbov**
  Pri nekaterih gumbih Interaktivna pomoč ni našla ničesar ali je vodila v napačen razdelek: Prilepi, Izbriši, Razdruži, Nastavitve delovnega prostora, Tema, noga in drugi. Prvo poglavje vodnika ima zdaj razdelek »Gumbi okrog delovnega območja« in pomoč vodi tja.
- **F povečuje na izbor, kot v Tinkercadu**
  Tipka F zdaj poveča pogled na izbrano, ko pa ni nič izbrano, pokaže celoten prizor. Doslej je F vedno pokazal celoten prizor, za izbor pa je bil potreben Shift+F, ki še vedno deluje. Celoten prizor vedno pokažeta tipka Home in gumb s hišico. Skica deluje enako.
- **Zavrtena zvezda ostane ravna**
  Zvezda, ki jo zavrtiš po višini, se je vrtela okrog sredine svojega okvirja namesto okrog svoje sredine. Ker petkraka zvezda v okvirju ni na sredini, je njena sredina krožila okrog osi in konice so molele različno daleč. Zdaj se zvezda vrti okrog svoje sredine in os ostane ravna.
- **Pogled sledi telesom visoko nad ploščo**
  Odkar višina ni več omejena na 220 mm, pogled ni mogel vzeti telesa visoko nad ploščo v sredino: Povečaj izbor ga je spustil čez zgornji rob, vrtišče pa je obtičalo pri 120 mm. Vrtišče zdaj seže do vrha najvišjega telesa (in navzdol do vznožja najnižjega), tako da se pogled tudi tam povečuje, premika in vrti.
- **Namesti layerling kot aplikacijo**
  Začetna stran in urejevalnik (tam po pol minute dela) imata zdaj kartico, ki priporoča namestitev layerlinga kot aplikacije: hitreje se zažene, deluje brez interneta in ga ni treba vsakič nalagati. Nič ne izgubiš, saj te layerling o novih različicah še vedno obvesti. »Ne zdaj« skrije kartico za dva tedna.
- **Odprta črta v skici: sporočilo pove pot**
  Odprte črte v skici ni mogoče iztegniti, ker mora biti obris sklenjen. Sporočilo zdaj doda, da lahko črto zgradiš kot potezo: obkljukaj »Zgradi kot potezo« v razdelku Zaključi in dobi širino.
- **Francoščina: boljša imena za pet oblik**
  Grezila, upognjena cev in pol valj zdaj nosijo imena, kakršna imajo v francoski delavnici: Trou lamé, Trou fraisé, Trou doublement fraisé, Tube cintré in Demi-cylindre. »Poteza« je bila po pomoti imenovana tudi poteza v medicinskem smislu; zdaj je »Trait«.
