## What's new in 1.119.0

- **Loft sketches**
  Two sketches or imported SVGs become one body: one outline at the bottom, the other at the top, straight walls between them. Two rings give a hollow frame, and Edit group opens the sketches again.

---

## Kaj je novega v 1.119.0

- **Povezovanje skic (loft)**
  Dve skici ali uvožena SVG postaneta eno telo: en obris spodaj, drugi zgoraj, vmes ravne stene. Dva obroča dasta votel okvir, »Uredi skupino« pa skici znova odpre.
