## What's new in 1.133.0

- **LayerLing by CDS in one line**
  The name next to the logo is now on one line and exactly as wide as the line under it.
- **Press an image on just once**
  The image texture has a fourth choice next to the tiles: Once. The image is pressed on a single time where you put it: Around and Height place its middle, Turned turns it, Image width and Image height set its size. The surface stays smooth around it, and the edges stay clean.
- **Plugs for batteries, motors and the serial port**
  The port opening now knows XT30, XT60, MT60, MPX and D-Sub 9, sized from the makers' drawings with about 0.25 mm of room all round. D-Sub 9 brings its two screw holes along: the opening and the holes move and turn as one group.
- **SBC boards: microSD, ribbon cables, GPIO and ESP32**
  Every Raspberry Pi and the BIGTREETECH Pi have their microSD card slot now. Ribbon cables can leave through the lid or sideways through the wall, and the GPIO header through the lid. Three small ESP32 boards join: XIAO ESP32-C6, ESP32-C3 Super Mini and ESP32-C6 Super Mini.

## Kaj je novega v 1.133.0

- **LayerLing by CDS v eni vrstici**
  Ime ob logotipu je zdaj v eni vrstici in točno tako široko kot vrstica pod njim.
- **Sliko pritisni samo enkrat**
  Slikovna tekstura ima ob ploščicah četrto izbiro: Once. Slika se pritisne samo enkrat, tam kjer jo postaviš: Around in Height postavita njeno sredino, Turned jo obrne, Image width in Image height določita velikost. Površina ostane okoli nje gladka, robovi pa čisti.
- **Vtičnice za baterije, motorje in serijska vrata**
  Odprtina za vrata zdaj pozna XT30, XT60, MT60, MPX in D-Sub 9, velikosti po risbah proizvajalcev z okoli 0,25 mm zračnosti naokoli. D-Sub 9 prinese zraven dve vijačni luknji: odprtina in luknji se premikajo in vrtijo kot ena skupina.
- **Plošče SBC: microSD, trakasti kabli, GPIO in ESP32**
  Vsak Raspberry Pi in BIGTREETECH Pi ima zdaj režo za kartico microSD. Trakasti kabli lahko izstopijo skozi pokrov ali vstran skozi steno, glava GPIO pa skozi pokrov. Pridružijo se tri majhne plošče ESP32: XIAO ESP32-C6, ESP32-C3 Super Mini in ESP32-C6 Super Mini.
