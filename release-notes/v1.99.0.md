## What's new in 1.99.0

- **Rounded box corner radius to 0.01 mm**
  The corner radius field of the rounded box rounded to tenths, so 0.25 mm came back as 0.3 mm. Now two decimals are kept, for the radius at the top and bottom too.
- **Close a gap in a sketch**
  If you erased a side of a shape in the sketch, just drag one open end onto the other: it snaps there, the two become one point, and the outline is closed again. No need to draw a new line in between.
- **Tags on side faces**
  A name tag or icon tag on a standing or sloped face now builds its layers out of the face. Before, the base plate came out in front with the letters shifted behind it.
- **layerling speaks Spanish**
  The interface and the guide are now in Spanish (Spain). Choose it in the language menu; a Spanish browser gets it by itself. Whatever is not translated yet shows in English.
- **Thin imported parts have the right size**
  An imported part thinner than 1 mm showed 1 mm and came out too small when you typed a height. The sizes are right now, saved shapes included.
- **Fold the hinge open or shut**
  The hinge has a new slider, the opening angle. It folds the second leaf up about the pin, from flat (0°) to closed (180°), so you can set the hinge as it stands in your design.
- **A simple mode for children**
  Switch on a simple mode in the settings: fewer tools, a short list of shapes, larger buttons and a start page without links out of layerling. Leaving it takes a two-second press. A link with ?mode=simple switches it on for that device, and a server can set it.
- **SVG files from CorelDRAW import**
  Some programs, CorelDRAW for one, save SVG files as UTF-16. Such files used to fail with "SVG is not valid XML". Now they are read.

## Kaj je novega v 1.99.0

- **Polmer zaobljenega kvadra na 0,01 mm**
  Polje za polmer vogala zaobljenega kvadra je zaokrožalo na desetinke: iz 0,25 mm je postalo 0,3 mm. Zdaj se ohranita dve decimalki, tudi pri polmeru zgoraj in spodaj.
- **Zapri vrzel v skici**
  Če si v skici izbrisal stranico oblike, zdaj preprosto povlečeš en odprt konec na drugega: zaskoči, oba postaneta ena točka in obris je spet zaprt. Nove črte vmes ni več treba risati.
- **Oznake na stranskih ploskvah**
  Napis z imenom ali simbolna oznaka na stoječi ali poševni ploskvi zdaj plasti gradi iz ploskve navzven. Prej je osnovna plošča ležala spredaj, črke pa premaknjene za njo.
- **layerling govori špansko**
  Vmesnik in navodila so zdaj tudi v španščini (Španija). Izbereš jo v meniju jezikov; španski brskalnik jo dobi sam. Kar še ni prevedeno, se prikaže v angleščini.
- **Tanki uvoženi deli imajo pravo velikost**
  Uvožen del, tanjši od 1 mm, je kazal 1 mm in je ob vpisani višini postal premajhen. Zdaj mere držijo, tudi pri že shranjenih oblikah.
- **Odpri ali zapri tečaj**
  Tečaj ima nov drsnik, kot odpiranja. Drugo krilo zavrti okrog čepa, od ravnega (0°) do zaprtega (180°), da tečaj postaviš tako, kot stoji v tvojem načrtu.
- **Preprosti način za otroke**
  V nastavitvah vklopiš preprosti način: manj orodij, kratek seznam oblik, večji gumbi in začetna stran brez povezav ven. Izklopiš ga samo z dvosekundnim držanjem. Povezava z ?mode=simple ga vklopi na napravi, nastavi pa ga lahko tudi strežnik.
- **SVG iz CorelDRAW se uvozi**
  Nekateri programi, na primer CorelDRAW, shranijo datoteke SVG v UTF-16. Take datoteke so prej javile »SVG is not valid XML«. Zdaj se preberejo.
