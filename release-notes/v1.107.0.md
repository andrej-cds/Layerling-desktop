## What's new in 1.107.0

- **Smooth straightens a corner with two handles at once**
  When a sketch point has two separate handles, Smooth (in the point's menu or with the 3 key) now makes it a smooth point at once: both handles end up on one line, each keeping its length. No more detour through Corner.
- **Soft light**
  Appearance in the settings now has "Soft light": most of the light then comes from where you look and turns with the view. No face is left dark, and the top of a dark body is no longer burnt out to white.
- **Inserted designs bring their notes**
  "Insert into this design" and dropping .lyl files on the import window now bring the saved design's notes and reference points too, in the same place. Until now only the bodies came.
- **Wall thickness for Gridfinity bins**
  A Gridfinity bin's wall is no longer fixed at 0.95 mm: set "Wall thickness" in the Properties panel, from 0.8 mm up to where the walls meet. The outside stays the same, so the bin still fits and stacks; once the walls leave less than 1 mm inside, it is built solid.
- **The browser is asked to keep your designs**
  layerling now asks the browser once not to clear the storage with your designs first when it runs short of room. Safari clears the data of a website that has not been visited for seven days, but not that of an installed app, and the guide (Installing) now says so. Important designs should still be saved as a file.
- **Multi-colour SVG: one body per colour**
  An SVG drawn in several colours now comes in as one body per colour, each in its colour and in its place in the drawing, ready for a multicolour print. An SVG of one colour stays one body, and so does one of more than 12 colours, such as a traced picture.
- **Small holes in meshes are closed**
  An imported mesh with openings, such as a figure's open eyes, could not be split, grouped or cut. layerling now closes such openings with a flat cap first and says how many there were. If an opening is too large and uneven for that, the message explains what to do.
- **Tape measure: typing a length moves the body**
  When one end of a measurement sits on a body, its number can be clicked. Type a new length, and the body at the second point moves along the measured line to exactly that distance, as with the ruler in Tinkercad.
- **Shapes snap to the middle of a face**
  When you place a new shape on a face, it snaps to the middle of that face once the pointer comes near it. Dragged from the shape library onto a face, it always lands in the middle. Hold Alt to set it down anywhere.

## Kaj je novega v 1.107.0

- **Zgladi poravna oglišče z dvema ročicama naenkrat**
  Kadar ima točka skice dve ločeni ročici, jo Zgladi (v meniju točke ali s tipko 3) naredi gladko naenkrat: obe ročici ležita na isti premici, vsaka ohrani svojo dolžino. Ni več ovinka prek Oglišča.
- **Mehka svetloba**
  Videz v nastavitvah ima zdaj »Mehko svetlobo«: večina svetlobe pride od tam, kamor gledaš, in se vrti s pogledom. Nobena ploskev ne ostane temna, zgornja stran temnega telesa pa ni več izžgana v belo.
- **Vstavljeni projekti prinesejo svoje opombe**
  »Vstavi v ta projekt« in spuščanje datotek .lyl v okno za uvoz zdaj prineseta tudi opombe in referenčne točke shranjenega projekta, na istem mestu. Doslej so prišla samo telesa.
- **Debelina stene posod Gridfinity**
  Stena posode Gridfinity ni več določena na 0,95 mm: v podoknu Lastnosti nastavi »Debelina stene«, od 0,8 mm do mesta, kjer se stene srečajo. Zunanjost ostane enaka, zato posoda še vedno pasi in se zlaga; če stene znotraj pustijo manj kot 1 mm, je posoda zgrajena polna.
- **Brskalnik je naprošen, naj ohrani tvoje projekte**
  layerling zdaj enkrat prosi brskalnik, naj pri pomanjkanju prostora najprej ne pobriše shrambe s tvojimi projekti. Safari izbriše podatke spletnega mesta, ki ga sedem dni nisi obiskal, podatkov nameščene aplikacije pa ne, in vodnik (Namestitev) to zdaj pove. Pomembne projekte vseeno shrani kot datoteko.
- **Večbarvni SVG: eno telo na barvo**
  SVG, narisan v več barvah, zdaj pride kot eno telo na barvo, vsako v svoji barvi in na svojem mestu v risbi, pripravljeno za večbarvni tisk. SVG ene same barve ostane eno telo, enako SVG z več kot 12 barvami, na primer posnetek slike.
- **Majhne luknje v mrežah se zaprejo**
  Uvožene mreže z odprtinami, na primer z odprtimi očmi figure, ni bilo mogoče razdeliti, združiti v skupino ali rezati. layerling take odprtine zdaj najprej zapre z ravnim pokrovom in pove, koliko jih je bilo. Če je odprtina prevelika in neravna, sporočilo pojasni, kaj storiti.
- **Merilni trak: vnos dolžine premakne telo**
  Kadar leži eno krajišče meritve na telesu, lahko klikneš njeno številko. Vnesi novo dolžino in telo pri drugi točki se premakne vzdolž merjene črte natanko na to razdaljo, kot z ravnilom v Tinkercadu.
- **Oblike se pripnejo na sredino ploskve**
  Ko novo obliko postaviš na ploskev, se pripne na sredino te ploskve, ko ji prideš blizu. Če jo povlečeš iz knjižnice oblik na ploskev, vedno pristane na sredini. Z Alt jo odložiš kamor koli.
