## What's new in 1.124.2

- **Delete face for the wedge, pyramid and paraboloid**
  Where a wedge, a pyramid or a paraboloid sticks out of another body, group the two and take off what sticks out with Delete face. The STEP export now takes them too, as bodies and as holes.
- **Pattern only outside, also after a fillet**
  On a cup with rounded rims, Pick faces for the surface texture used to take the whole cup, the inside and the bottom too. Now a face also ends at a tight round: a click on the outside takes only the outside, and the rounds can be picked on their own.
- **Crisper headings**
  Headings, tabs and ribbon labels are a little less heavy. On the Mac the letters ran together at small sizes and looked blurred; now the interface uses Avenir Next Demi Bold there instead of Heavy, and Arial Bold instead of Arial Black on Windows.
- **Folded groups show that they open**
  When the window is narrow, groups in the ribbon fold into one button. It looked like a single tool, History like Undo, say. Now a small arrow in the group's colour hangs beneath it, and its hint says that a click shows the tools.

## Kaj je novega v 1.124.2

- **Brisanje ploskve za klin, piramido in paraboloid**
  Kjer klin, piramida ali paraboloid moli iz drugega telesa, združi obe in odstrani, kar moli, z »Izbriši ploskev«. Izvoz STEP ju zdaj prav tako zajame, kot telesi in kot luknji.
- **Vzorec samo zunaj, tudi po zaobljenju**
  Pri skodelici z zaobljenimi robovi je »Izberi ploskve« za teksturo površine doslej zajel vso skodelico, tudi notranjost in dno. Zdaj se ploskev konča tudi pri tesnem zaobljenju: klik na zunanjost zajame samo zunanjost, zaobljenja pa lahko izbereš posebej.
- **Ostrejši naslovi**
  Naslovi, zavihki in oznake traku so malo manj debeli. Na Macu so se črke pri majhnih velikostih zlivale in so bile videti zamegljene; zdaj vmesnik tam uporablja Avenir Next Demi Bold namesto Heavy, v Windowsih pa Arial Bold namesto Arial Black.
- **Zložene skupine pokažejo, da se odprejo**
  Ko je okno ozko, se skupine na traku zložijo v en gumb. Videti je bilo kot eno samo orodje, npr. Zgodovina kot Razveljavi. Zdaj pod njim visi majhna puščica v barvi skupine, namig pa pove, da klik pokaže orodja.
