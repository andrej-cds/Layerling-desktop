## What's new in 1.91.0

- **Ctrl and the arrow keys pan the view**
  With nothing selected, hold Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac) and press the arrow keys to slide the view across the screen: a tenth of the picture a press, half of it with Shift as well. The plain arrow keys still turn the view.
- **Raised floor for Gridfinity bins**
  A Gridfinity bin can now have a raised floor: set "Floor height" in the Properties panel, and the bin is solid up to it, a blank to cut the shapes of your tools into.
- **The cut face follows the plane without lag**
  The filled cut face of the section view lagged behind the plane while you dragged it and only came back when your hand rested. Now it is cut for the newest position of the plane at every step.
- **Folders for custom shapes**
  Custom shapes can be sorted into folders, up to three levels deep, in the browser and on the server. A folder has a counter and can be renamed and deleted (after asking), and a shape is put into a folder when you save it or moved to another one afterwards.
- **A loose hole is no longer lost in the export**
  A hole you had not grouped was missing from the STL, 3MF or OBJ file when the body it cuts from touched or overlapped another body of the same colour. The material estimate then counted it as solid too. The hole now stays.

## Kaj je novega v 1.91.0

- **Ctrl in smerne tipke premikajo pogled**
  Ko ni nič izbrano, drži Ctrl (Cmd na Macu) in pritiskaj smerne tipke, da pogled zdrsne po zaslonu: z vsakim pritiskom za desetino slike, s Shiftom še za polovico. Same smerne tipke pogled še vedno vrtijo.
- **Dvignjeno dno za posode Gridfinity**
  Posoda Gridfinity lahko zdaj dobi dvignjeno dno: v oknu lastnosti nastaviš »Višina dna« in posoda je do nje polna, kot surovec, v katerega izrežeš oblike svojih orodij.
- **Prerezana ploskev sledi ravnini brez zaostajanja**
  Zapolnjena prerezana ploskev pogleda prereza je med vlečenjem ravnine zaostajala in se je vrnila šele, ko je roka mirovala. Zdaj je ob vsakem koraku izrezana za najnovejši položaj ravnine.
- **Mape za lastne oblike**
  Lastne oblike je mogoče razvrstiti v mape, do tri ravni globoko, v brskalniku in na strežniku. Mapa ima števec, jo je mogoče preimenovati in izbrisati (po vprašanju), oblika pa gre v mapo ob shranjevanju ali pa jo pozneje premakneš v drugo.
- **Prosta luknja se ne izgubi več pri izvozu**
  Luknja, ki je nisi združil v skupino, je manjkala v datoteki STL, 3MF ali OBJ, kadar se je telo, iz katerega reže, dotikalo drugega telesa iste barve ali se z njim prekrivalo. Ocena materiala jo je zato štela kot polno. Luknja zdaj ostane.
