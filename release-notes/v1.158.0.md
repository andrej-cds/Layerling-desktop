## What's new in 1.158.0

- **Resize right up to the wall**
  When you drag a side handle to make a shape bigger, the side now stops at the edges of other shapes and at the wall it runs into, the inside wall of a box or case as well. So a part fills a box exactly from wall to wall, without measuring.
- **A third arm on the framing square, pointing up**
  The framing square has a third arm that stands straight up from its corner, with its own ticks, for cylinders and anything you build upwards. The new button with three axes under its handle switches each arm on or off.
- **Edits are no longer lost**
  If the stored time of a design was ahead of your computer's clock, for example after the clock was set back, no edit was saved after a reload, and changing the snap step brought the old state back. Now every edit is saved, and the first one puts such a design right again.
- **The protractor measures what a straight view shows**
  Seen straight from the top, the front or a side, the protractor now measures the angle you see: the diagonal of a cube seen from the top gives 45° instead of 54.7°. It stays so when you turn the view afterwards.
- **Support rib: bridges with a gap to the part**
  Under More settings, "Gap at the bridge ends" lets each bridge end just before the part. The slicer then prints the bridge as a path of its own, and the support breaks away more easily. Half a line, about 0.2 mm for a 0.4 mm nozzle, is a good start.
- **New boards: Cheap Yellow Display**
  The SBC boards now include the ESP32-2432S028R ("CYD") with micro-USB and with micro-USB plus USB-C, screen up, with every connector on the back. Buttons under a board now reach down through the floor.
- **An SBC board stays on its mounting holes**
  When you change an SBC board's clearance, its reach through the wall or its openings, the board now keeps its place, also turned or tilted. Before, it could move along with its holes.
- **A sketch body keeps its taper, twist and lean**
  When you edit the sketch of a sketch body or change its fill, its taper, twist and lean now stay. The taper grows or shrinks with the outline.
- **Parts lying on their side scale along**
  When you stretch several selected parts, a cylinder lying on its side now grows along: longer with the handles at its ends, thicker and still round with those at its side.
- **Bundles: where their holes cut**
  The note in a bundle's settings now says that its holes never cut the bundle's own parts, not even later in a group, only the other parts.

## Kaj je novega v 1.158.0

- **Spreminjanje velikosti do stene**
  Ko povlečeš stranski ročaj, da oblika postane večja, se stranica zdaj ustavi na robovih drugih oblik in na steni, v katero naleti, tudi na notranji steni škatle ali ohišja. Tako del napolni škatlo natančno od stene do stene, brez merjenja.
- **Tretji krak kotnika, ki kaže navzgor**
  Kotnik ima tretji krak, ki stoji naravnost navzgor iz njegovega vogala, z lastnimi črticami, za valje in vse, kar gradiš navzgor. Nov gumb s tremi osmi pod njegovim ročajem vsak krak vklopi ali izklopi.
- **Urejanja se ne izgubijo več**
  Če je bil shranjeni čas načrta pred uro računalnika, na primer po vrnitvi ure nazaj, po ponovnem nalaganju ni bilo shranjeno nobeno urejanje, sprememba koraka pripenjanja pa je vrnila staro stanje. Zdaj je vsako urejanje shranjeno, prvo tak načrt spet postavi v red.
- **Kotomer meri, kar pokaže raven pogled**
  Pri pogledu naravnost od zgoraj, spredaj ali s strani kotomer zdaj meri kot, ki ga vidiš: diagonala kocke, gledana od zgoraj, da 45° namesto 54,7°. Tako ostane, tudi ko pogled pozneje zasukaš.
- **Podporno rebro: mostovi z režo do dela**
  V »More settings« »Gap at the bridge ends« omogoči, da se vsak most konča tik pred delom. Razrezovalnik most natisne kot lastno pot, podpora pa se lažje odlomi. Dobro izhodišče je pol črte, približno 0,2 mm pri šobi 0,4 mm.
- **Nove plošče: Cheap Yellow Display**
  Plošče SBC zdaj vključujejo ESP32-2432S028R (»CYD«) z micro-USB in z micro-USB plus USB-C, zaslon navzgor, vsi priključki na hrbtni strani. Gumbi pod ploščo zdaj segajo skozi dno.
- **Plošča SBC ostane na svojih pritrdilnih luknjah**
  Ko spremeniš zračnost plošče SBC, njen doseg skozi steno ali odprtine, plošča zdaj ohrani svoje mesto, tudi zasukana ali nagnjena. Prej se je lahko premaknila skupaj z luknjami.
- **Skicirano telo ohrani zožitev, zasuk in nagib**
  Ko urejaš skico skiciranega telesa ali spremeniš njegovo zapolnitev, zožitev, zasuk in nagib zdaj ostanejo. Zožitev raste ali se krči z obrisom.
- **Deli, ki ležijo na boku, se razširijo vzdolž**
  Ko raztegneš več izbranih delov, valj, ki leži na boku, zdaj zraste vzdolž: daljši z ročaji na koncih, debelejši in še vedno okrogel s tistimi ob strani.
- **Snopi: kje režejo njihove luknje**
  Opomba v nastavitvah snopa zdaj pove, da njegove luknje nikoli ne režejo lastnih delov snopa, tudi pozneje v skupini ne, samo druge dele.
