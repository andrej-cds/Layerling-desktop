## What's new in 1.129.1

- **Trace with curves, a lasso and more**
  Trace in a picture's settings now lays the outlines as curves and corners, with as many points as you choose, and shows them in red over the picture first. On a photo, Object takes everything that stands out from the background as one outline; clicked areas become holes, Cut away removes a shadow, say, and the magnetic lasso follows an edge by itself where no colour tells the shape apart. The vase drawing can trace a photo the same way.
- **Hinge: lift-off and nut pockets**
  The hinge has a Lift-off style: two knuckles, the lower one with the pin, the upper one a sleeve that lifts off, so a lid or a door can be taken off. On every style the screw holes can now get hexagonal pockets for nuts, countersunk on one leaf and with nut pockets on the other if you like.

## Kaj je novega v 1.129.1

- **Sledenje s krivuljami, laso in več**
  Sledenje v nastavitvah slike zdaj položi obrise kot krivulje in vogale s toliko točkami, kot jih izbereš, in jih najprej pokaže rdeče nad sliko. Pri fotografiji Object vzame vse, kar izstopa iz ozadja, kot en obris; kliknjena območja postanejo luknje, Cut away odstrani npr. senco, magnetni laso pa sam sledi robu, kjer barva ne loči oblike. Risba vaze lahko na enak način sledi fotografiji.
- **Tečaj: snemljiv in žepi za matice**
  Tečaj ima slog Lift-off: dva členka, spodnji s sornikom, zgornji kot tulec, ki se dvigne, tako da lahko pokrov ali vrata snameš. Pri vseh slogih lahko luknje za vijake zdaj dobijo šesterokotne žepe za matice, na enem krilu poglobljene, na drugem po želji z žepi za matice.
