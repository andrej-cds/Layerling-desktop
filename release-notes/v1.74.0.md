## What's new in 1.74.0

- **Russian as a language**
  layerling now speaks Russian: the whole interface and the 16 chapters of the guide. The language is chosen automatically for a browser set to Russian and can be changed in the language list. It was translated with an AI assistant; wrong words are fixed with a small pull request on GitHub.
- **Revolve from a line with a width**
  A revolve sketch has the Stroke button again. Draw the wall of a vase or a cup as one open line to the left of the axis, tick the stroke and set the width: the revolve turns that band into a thin wall, as in Tinkercad. What reaches across the axis is cut off. The body keeps the drawn line, so Edit sketch goes on from there.
- **The object list stays open**
  The object list (Ctrl+Shift+O) started closed with every design and after every reload. It now stays open or closed as you left it, for all designs, in this browser.
- **Align and Snap to Grid under Arrange**
  Align and Snap to Grid sat in the ribbon under Modify, beside Chamfer, Fillet and Hollow. They place a shape and do not change it, so they now head the Arrange group, beside Drop to workplane, Lay flat and Center on workplane.
- **An open cut is reported**
  When the surface of an imported part has gaps, a STEP part with a few cracks in its mesh for example, the cut face stays open when you group it with a hole, and the part is hollow inside. The message afterwards now says so, with the number of open edges, and suggests importing the part as STL or 3MF.
- **Framing square: every number on its own**
  Beside each green number of the framing square sits a small switch: the number counts to the edge or to the middle of the body, each on its own. A standing cylinder is placed by its middle in the two arms and its bottom in height, a lying one by its middle across and an end along. The button at the handle still switches all three at once. The height dimension now stands outside the body, on the edge furthest right on the screen.
- **A new name in the STL export asks for a new file**
  In Chrome and Edge every STL export wrote into the file you chose the first time, and the file name field did nothing any more. If you now type another name there, layerling asks where the new file should go. The line under the window says whether the old file is written or you are asked.
- **A double click on a slider resets it**
  A double click on a slider in a shape's settings sets the value back to its default, like the small arrow next to its name. It works for seven sliders of the workspace settings as well, such as zoom speed and shadow strength.
- **Snap grid: the dragged face lands on the grid**
  Dragging the height handle snapped the change of the height by whole grid steps: a body 0.5 mm high on a 1 mm grid went to 1.5 mm. Now the face you drag lands on the grid of the workplane (the top, or the bottom for the lower handle), so it goes to 1.0 mm. The lift arrow puts the bottom of the body on the grid the same way. A tilted selection keeps the old step.

## Kaj je novega v 1.74.0

- **Ruščina kot jezik**
  layerling zdaj govori rusko: ves vmesnik in 16 poglavij navodil. Jezik se izbere samodejno za brskalnik, nastavljen na ruščino, spremeniš pa ga lahko na seznamu jezikov. Prevedel ga je pomočnik z umetno inteligenco; napačne besede se popravijo z majhnim pull requestom na GitHubu.
- **Vrtenje iz črte z debelino**
  Skica za vrtenje ima spet gumb Poteza. Steno vaze ali skodelice nariši kot eno odprto črto levo od osi, obkljukaj potezo in nastavi debelino: vrtenje spremeni ta trak v tanko steno, kot v Tinkercadu. Kar sega čez os, se odreže. Telo obdrži narisano črto, zato Uredi skico nadaljuje od tam.
- **Seznam predmetov ostane odprt**
  Seznam predmetov (Ctrl+Shift+O) se je ob vsakem načrtu in po vsakem ponovnem nalaganju začel zaprt. Zdaj ostane odprt ali zaprt, kakor si ga pustil, za vse načrte v tem brskalniku.
- **Poravnaj in Pripni na mrežo pod Razporedi**
  Poravnaj in Pripni na mrežo sta bila v traku pod Spremeni, ob Posnetju, Zaokrožitvi in Votli obliki. Oblik ne spreminjata, ampak jih postavita, zato zdaj vodita skupino Razporedi, ob Spusti na delovno ravnino, Položi in Središči na delovno ravnino.
- **Odprt rez se sporoči**
  Kadar ima površina uvoženega dela vrzeli, na primer del STEP z nekaj razpokami v mreži, ostane prerezna ploskev odprta, ko ga združiš z luknjo, del pa je znotraj votel. Sporočilo zdaj to pove, skupaj s številom odprtih robov, in predlaga uvoz dela kot STL ali 3MF.
- **Okvirni kotnik: vsaka številka zase**
  Ob vsaki zeleni številki okvirnega kotnika je majhno stikalo: številka šteje do roba ali do sredine telesa, vsaka zase. Stoječ valj se postavi s sredino v obeh krakih in dnom po višini, ležeč s sredino počez in koncem po dolžini. Gumb pri ročaju še vedno preklopi vse tri naenkrat. Mera višine zdaj stoji zunaj telesa, na robu, ki je na zaslonu najbolj desno.
- **Novo ime pri izvozu STL vpraša po novi datoteki**
  V Chromu in Edgeu je vsak izvoz STL pisal v datoteko, izbrano prvič, polje z imenom datoteke pa ni več delovalo. Če tam zdaj vtipkaš drugo ime, te layerling vpraša, kam naj gre nova datoteka. Vrstica pod oknom pove, ali se piše v staro datoteko ali te bo vprašal.
- **Dvoklik na drsnik ga ponastavi**
  Dvoklik na drsnik v nastavitvah oblike vrne vrednost na privzeto, kakor majhna puščica ob njegovem imenu. Deluje tudi pri sedmih drsnikih delovnih nastavitev, na primer hitrost približevanja in moč sence.
- **Mreža pripenjanja: vlečena ploskev pristane na mreži**
  Vlečenje ročaja višine je pripenjalo spremembo višine po celih korakih mreže: telo visoko 0,5 mm je na mreži 1 mm skočilo na 1,5 mm. Zdaj vlečena ploskev pristane na mreži delovne ravnine (zgornja, pri spodnjem ročaju pa spodnja), zato gre na 1,0 mm. Puščica za dvig na enak način postavi dno telesa na mrežo. Nagnjen izbor obdrži star korak.
