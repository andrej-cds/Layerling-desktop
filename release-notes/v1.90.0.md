## What's new in 1.90.0

- **QR codes in the shape library**
  The new QR code entry turns a text or a web address into a code to print: alone, raised on a plate in two colours, or engraved into the plate. Error correction, width and quiet zone can be chosen, the preview shows the code first, and a warning appears when the modules get too small for a readable print. You can change the text later in the properties and make the code again.
- **The mouse wheel changes numbers in the tool panels too**
  What already worked in the properties of a shape works now in Chamfer, Fillet, Hollow, Pattern, Hole pattern, Face, Mate faces, Split and the Section view menu: click into the number field, then the mouse wheel changes the value. Ten times as much with Shift, a tenth with Ctrl, a hundredth with Alt.
- **The cut face follows the plane**
  The filled cut face of the section view no longer waits until the plane stands still: it follows the plane while you move it, so the body no longer looks hollow for a moment.
- **Notes stay with their bodies in groups**
  Group takes a pinned note into the group, Ungroup hands it to the visible part under it, and while a group is open for editing its notes move with the part they sit on. A note whose body is deleted stays where it stood.
- **Finger scoops for Gridfinity bins**
  A Gridfinity bin of 2 height units or more can now have a finger scoop along any of its inner walls, several at once: the floor rises along a quarter circle into the wall, so small parts can be pushed out. You set the radius in the Properties panel.
- **Half-size grid for Gridfinity bins**
  A Gridfinity bin can now be built in cells of 21 mm, each with a foot of its own; four of them stand in one socket of an ordinary baseplate. So bins of 1½ or 2½ units fit beside whole ones. You switch on "Half-size grid (21 mm)" in the Properties panel.
- **The revolve sketch shows the front view when edited again**
  When you open the sketch of a revolved body again to edit it, it shows the other bodies from the front, in the same view as the profile, as while you draw it. Before, the view from above came back as soon as the body stood above the plate.
- **Angles with two decimals**
  A rotation is now kept with two decimals and shown in the number field: 11.25° stays 11.25°, also when you turn with Shift on the rotation circle. Before, it became 11.3°, and the field showed whole degrees.
- **The chamfer slider moves in tenths of a millimetre**
  The slider for the distance or radius of a chamfer or fillet now moves in tenths of a millimetre up to 10 mm instead of giving odd values like 2.347. Larger or more exact values you type into the field.
- **Pockets of a whole depth can be chamfered**
  When a hole ends exactly on a face of the body, for example with a height of 5.000 pulled through a workplane on a slope, a chamfer on the rim of the pocket failed. The pocket is now cut cleanly when grouped, and the chamfer works.
- **Custom shapes take their notes along**
  If you save bodies with pinned notes as a custom shape, the notes come along when you put the shape into a design. They appear closed, pinned to the new bodies, with new numbers.

## Kaj je novega v 1.90.0

- **Kode QR v knjižnici oblik**
  Nov vnos Koda QR iz besedila ali spletnega naslova naredi kodo za tiskanje: samostojno, privzdignjeno na plošči v dveh barvah ali vgravirano v ploščo. Popravljanje napak, širina in mirno območje so izbirni, predogled kodo pokaže vnaprej, opozorilo pa se pojavi, ko so moduli premajhni za berljiv tisk. Besedilo lahko pozneje spremeniš v lastnostih in kodo ustvariš znova.
- **Kolesce miške spreminja številke tudi v oknih orodij**
  Kar je že delovalo v lastnostih oblike, zdaj deluje tudi pri Posnetku, Zaobljenju, Izdolbi, Vzorcu, Vzorcu lukenj, Ploskvi, Prilagajanju ploskev, Razdelitvi in v meniju prereza: klikni v številčno polje, nato kolesce miške spreminja vrednost. S Shiftom desetkrat toliko, s Ctrl desetinko, z Alt stotinko.
- **Prerezana ploskev sledi ravnini**
  Zapolnjena prerezana ploskev pogleda prereza ne čaka več, da se ravnina ustavi: med premikanjem ravnine teče z njo, tako da telo ni več za trenutek videti votlo.
- **Opombe v skupinah ostanejo na svojih telesih**
  Združi v skupino prikovano opombo vzame v skupino, Razdruži jo preda vidnemu delu pod njo, in dokler je skupina odprta za urejanje, se njene opombe premikajo z delom, na katerem so. Opomba, katere telo izbrišeš, ostane, kjer je stala.
- **Žlebovi za prste pri posodah Gridfinity**
  Posoda Gridfinity z višino vsaj 2 enoti lahko zdaj dobi žleb za prste ob vsaki notranji steni, tudi ob več hkrati: dno se ob steni dvigne po četrtini kroga, da se majhni deli lažje potisnejo ven. Polmer nastaviš v oknu lastnosti.
- **Polovična mreža za posode Gridfinity**
  Posodo Gridfinity je zdaj mogoče zgraditi v celicah po 21 mm, vsaka s svojo nogo; štiri stojijo v enem ležišču običajne osnovne plošče. Tako se poleg celih posod prilegajo tudi posode velikosti 1½ ali 2½ enote. »Polovična mreža (21 mm)« vklopiš v oknu lastnosti.
- **Skica vrtenja ob ponovnem urejanju spet pokaže pogled od spredaj**
  Ko skico zavrtenega telesa znova odpreš za urejanje, druga telesa spet pokaže od spredaj, v istem pogledu kot profil, kot med risanjem. Prej se je pogled od zgoraj vrnil, takoj ko je telo stalo nad ploščo.
- **Koti z dvema decimalkama**
  Zasuk se zdaj shrani z dvema decimalkama in je tako prikazan v številčnem polju: 11,25° ostane 11,25°, tudi ko vrtiš s Shiftom na krogu vrtenja. Prej je postalo 11,3°, polje pa je kazalo cele stopinje.
- **Drsnik pri posnetku se premika po desetinkah milimetra**
  Drsnik za razdaljo ali polmer pri posnetku in zaobljenju zdaj skače po desetinkah milimetra do 10 mm, namesto da bi dajal nenavadne vrednosti, kot je 2,347. Večje ali natančnejše vrednosti vtipkaš v polje.
- **Žepe s polno globino je mogoče posneti**
  Kadar se vdolbina konča natanko na ploskvi telesa, na primer z višino 5,000, povlečeno skozi delovno ravnino na nagibu, posnetek na robu žepa ni uspel. Žep se zdaj ob združevanju v skupino čisto izreže, posnetek pa deluje.
- **Lastne oblike vzamejo opombe s seboj**
  Če telesa s prikovanimi opombami shraniš kot lastno obliko, opombe pridejo zraven, ko obliko vstaviš v osnutek. Pojavijo se zaprte, prikovane na nova telesa, z novimi številkami.
