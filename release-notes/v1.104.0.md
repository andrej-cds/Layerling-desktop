## What's new in 1.104.0

- **Tinkercad imports with deeply nested groups open again**
  A design with many groups nested inside each other, as the Tinkercad importer can write them, made the browser tab crash on opening ("out of memory"), even with a file of 12 MB. The editor made a key for each group that doubled in length with every level of nesting (346 MB at 19 levels). It now stays small, and the files open.
- **DXF for laser cutting shops**
  The export window now has DXF: the top view or the footprint as one outline of lines, true arcs and circles on layer 0 in white, in millimetres - the way laser cutting shops ask for the file. Holes and roundings become real circles and arcs instead of many short straight pieces that make the cutting head shake. Not yet tried on a laser cutter: feedback is welcome.
- **Framing square: the distance fields no longer cover each other**
  When a body sat right at the corner of the framing square, the three green distance fields (X, Y, Z) fell on the same spot and covered each other and the square's buttons. They now step aside where they would overlap, and stay at the middle of their arrows where there is room. Each number now starts with a small x, y or z: the axis it belongs to.
- **Save export files on the server**
  When the server folder is on, the export window now has "Save to the server" for STL, 3MF, OBJ, STEP, SVG, PNG and the drawing too. The file lands in the folder of your design, so a program that shares the folder, such as a slicer in Docker, can open it there. It does not show in the list of designs, and a file of the same name is replaced only by a second press on the button.
- **Search the keyboard shortcuts**
  The keyboard shortcuts window has a search box at the top. It shows only the shortcuts whose description, group or keys contain every word you type, such as "ctrl+z" or "mirror". The X at the end of the box empties it, and with the box empty the whole list is back.
- **Notes on sketches**
  In the sketch editor, the Note button under Manage or the N key now puts a note on the sketch, as in the 3D editor. The notes belong to the sketch's body: in the 3D view they sit on it and move with it, and when you open the sketch again they are back on it, including the ones placed in 3D.
- **Alt with the arrow keys moves in fine steps**
  Shift with an arrow key moves a body by five grid steps; Alt now does the opposite and moves it by a fifth of a grid step, so with a 1 mm grid it is 0.2 mm. It works on the workplane and in the sketch (points, lines, image and curve handles), and the shortcut list names it.
- **A group shows its real outline under the sketch**
  A group of bodies, say a round plate and a gear, was drawn under the sketch as a plain box. It now shows the outline of each of its parts, the round plate and the gear, and the sketch snaps to them.

## Kaj je novega v 1.104.0

- **Uvoz iz Tinkercada z globoko gnezdenimi skupinami se spet odpre**
  Načrt z veliko skupinami, ugnezdenimi druga v drugo, kot jih zna zapisati uvoznik iz Tinkercada, je ob odpiranju sesul zavihek brskalnika (»zmanjkalo pomnilnika«), tudi pri datoteki z 12 MB. Urejevalnik je za vsako skupino naredil ključ, ki se je z vsako stopnjo gnezdenja podvojil (346 MB pri 19 stopnjah). Zdaj ostane majhen in datoteke se odprejo.
- **DXF za delavnice za lasersko rezanje**
  Okno za izvoz ima zdaj DXF: pogled od zgoraj ali odtis kot en obris iz črt, pravih lokov in krogov na plasti 0 v beli barvi, v milimetrih – tako, kot datoteko zahtevajo delavnice za lasersko rezanje. Luknje in zaokrožitve postanejo pravi krogi in loki namesto številnih kratkih ravnih kosov, od katerih se reznik trese. Na laserskem rezalniku še ni preizkušeno: povratne informacije so dobrodošle.
- **Kotnik: polja z razdaljami se ne prekrivajo več**
  Ko je telo stalo tik ob kotu kotnika, so se tri zelena polja z razdaljami (X, Y, Z) znašla na istem mestu in prekrila drugo drugo ter gumbe kotnika. Zdaj se umaknejo tam, kjer bi se prekrivala, in ostanejo na sredini svojih puščic, kjer je prostor. Vsaka številka se zdaj začne z majhnim x, y ali z: osjo, ki ji pripada.
- **Shrani izvožene datoteke na strežnik**
  Ko je mapa na strežniku vklopljena, ima okno za izvoz zdaj »Shrani na strežnik« za STL, 3MF, OBJ, STEP, SVG, PNG in tudi za risbo. Datoteka pristane v mapi tvojega načrta, tako da jo lahko odpre program, ki mapo deli, na primer razrezovalnik v Dockerju. V seznamu načrtov se ne pokaže, datoteko z istim imenom pa zamenja šele drugi pritisk na gumb.
- **Iskanje po bližnjicah na tipkovnici**
  Okno z bližnjicami na tipkovnici ima na vrhu iskalno polje. Pokaže samo bližnjice, katerih opis, skupina ali tipke vsebujejo vse besede, ki jih vpišeš, na primer »ctrl+z« ali »zrcali«. X na koncu polja ga izprazni, s praznim poljem se vrne celoten seznam.
- **Opombe na skicah**
  V urejevalniku skic gumb Opomba pod Upravljanje ali tipka N zdaj postavi opombo na skico, kot v 3D urejevalniku. Opombe pripadajo telesu skice: v 3D pogledu ležijo na njem in se premikajo z njim, ob ponovnem odpiranju skice pa so spet na njej, tudi tiste, postavljene v 3D.
- **Alt s puščicami premika v drobnih korakih**
  Shift s puščico premakne telo za pet korakov mreže; Alt zdaj naredi nasprotno in ga premakne za petino koraka mreže, pri mreži 1 mm torej za 0,2 mm. Deluje na delovni ravnini in v skici (točke, črte, ročaji slik in krivulj), seznam bližnjic pa ga navaja.
- **Skupina pod skico pokaže pravi obris**
  Skupina teles, recimo okrogla plošča in zobnik, je bila pod skico narisana kot navaden kvader. Zdaj pokaže obris vsakega svojega dela, okrogle plošče in zobnika, skica pa se nanju pripenja.
