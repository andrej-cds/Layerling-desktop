## What's new in 1.92.0

- **A revolve can wind up as one continuous strand**
  A revolved body now has a Pitch in the Properties panel. Above 0 the outline winds up as a helix instead of rings, like filament on a spool, and you set the number of turns.
- **Alt turns the selection frame around**
  Hold Alt while you drag a selection frame: it works the other way round for that one drag. With the setting "The direction of the frame decides" on, a frame dragged to the right then takes everything it touches and one dragged to the left only what lies completely inside. With the setting off, Alt takes only what lies completely inside.
- **An engraving around a cylinder cuts all the way through**
  A logo wrapped inward and cut out of a cylinder as a hole left a hair-thin skin standing in places. The wrapped body now reaches 0.05 mm beyond the wall, so the hole cuts all the way through.
- **The wheel in a size field stops at the lower limit**
  The mouse wheel in a size field of the properties no longer runs below the smallest value (0.01 mm) into negative numbers: the field stops at the limit.
- **Tiny bodies can be moved on a tablet again**
  When a selected body is very small on the screen, a finger no longer grabs a handle instead of the body: the handles' wide catch zones are dropped then, and the middle stays free for dragging.
- **A see-through body has a see-through cut face**
  When you cut a see-through body in the section view, the filled cut face is now as see-through as the body, no longer solid.
- **The sketch's shape panel: its own place and size**
  The sketch's shape panel now remembers its place, its pin and its size on its own and no longer shares them with the 3D editor. Its edges and corners can be pulled to the size you want, like the 3D editor's panel.
- **No offline promise where it cannot work**
  The card "Install layerling as an app" and the "Works offline" tile on the start page now appear only where layerling can really keep itself for offline use. In the Docker image, which runs as a Node server, and at a plain-http address they are gone.
- **The view cube can sit in any corner**
  Settings > Appearance > View cube position puts the view cube in the top left, top right, bottom left or bottom right of the workplane. In any corner but the top left, the camera controls move up into its place.
- **Traced SVGs stay small enough to join**
  An imported SVG with very many curves, such as a traced drawing, was cut into over 350,000 triangles, so several copies could no longer be joined. It is now about ten times fewer, and the drawing looks the same at that size. Sketches drawn by hand stay as fine as before.
- **Thin, small letters are no longer lost in the export**
  A small, flat text lost single letters in the export as STL, 3MF or OBJ, with a thin text most of them: the dot of the i, the apostrophe, small letters. The export took them for splinters that a cut had left behind. That now applies only to bodies that a cut, a merge of several bodies or the colour separation has changed.
- **An SVG with touching shapes comes in whole**
  In a drawn or traced picture two shapes often meet in just one point. The import then left some shapes out, and the picture came in smaller and with gaps. Such shapes are now built together, and the picture stays whole.
- **The chamfillet on a cylinder sits at the foot again**
  A chamfillet on the bottom edge of a cylinder came out the wrong way round: the fillet at the foot, the chamfer above it, and the top rim rounded as well. Now the chamfer sits on the plate and blends into the wall, and the top rim stays untouched.
- **Typing Japanese and Chinese into the Text field**
  With an input method (IME) nothing sensible could be typed into the Text field of a text or a name tag: "ishi" came out as "いｓｈい" and could not be converted to kanji. The field now keeps what is typed and takes a word over once it is done. This holds for Japanese, Chinese and Korean.
- **Interactive Help explains each setting on its own**
  In the Workspace settings, a click with Interactive Help on often led only to a long section, or to nothing. Now the guide has a section of its own for each group of settings (English and German), plus "Choosing colours" for the colour card, and the fields, sliders, colour buttons and lists find their section, the Fast mode switch too.
- **The History view says what the arrows undo and redo**
  Under the slider of the History view, a row now names the step the left arrow would undo ("Last step: Moved Box") and the one the right arrow would redo ("Next step: Grouped 2 objects into Group"), with "none" at the oldest and the newest state. It also works for designs saved before.

## Kaj je novega v 1.92.0

- **Vrtenje se lahko navije v eno neprekinjeno nit**
  Zavrteno telo ima zdaj v oknu lastnosti polje Korak. Nad 0 se obris ne navije v kolobarje, temveč v vijačnico, kot nit na kolutu, število ovojev pa nastaviš sam.
- **Alt obrne okvir izbire**
  Drži Alt med vlečenjem okvira izbire: za tisto vleko deluje obratno. Če je vklopljena nastavitev »Smer okvira odloča«, okvir, povlečen v desno, vzame vse, česar se dotakne, okvir, povlečen v levo, pa le tisto, kar leži povsem znotraj. Če je nastavitev izklopljena, Alt vzame samo tisto, kar leži povsem znotraj.
- **Gravura okoli valja prereže skozi in skozi**
  Logotip, ovit navznoter in izrezan iz valja kot luknja, je na mestih pustil lasasto tanko kožico. Ovito telo zdaj sega 0,05 mm čez steno, zato luknja prereže vse do konca.
- **Kolesce v polju velikosti se ustavi pri spodnji meji**
  Kolesce miške v polju velikosti v lastnostih ne zdrsne več pod najmanjšo vrednost (0,01 mm) v negativna števila: polje se ustavi pri meji.
- **Drobna telesa je na tablici spet mogoče premikati**
  Kadar je izbrano telo na zaslonu zelo majhno, prst ne zagrabi več ročice namesto telesa: široka področja ročic se takrat opustijo, sredina pa ostane prosta za vlečenje.
- **Prosojno telo ima prosojno prerezano ploskev**
  Ko v pogledu prereza prerežeš prosojno telo, je zapolnjena prerezana ploskev zdaj enako prosojna kot telo in ni več polna.
- **Okno oblik v skici: svoje mesto in velikost**
  Okno oblik v skici si zdaj samo zapomni svoje mesto, pripetost in velikost in jih ne deli več s 3D urejevalnikom. Robove in vogale lahko povlečeš na želeno velikost, kot pri oknu 3D urejevalnika.
- **Brez obljube delovanja brez povezave, kjer ne more delovati**
  Kartica »Namesti Layerling kot aplikacijo« in ploščica »Deluje brez povezave« na začetni strani se zdaj pokažeta samo tam, kjer se Layerling res lahko shrani za uporabo brez povezave. V sliki Docker, ki teče kot strežnik Node, in na navadnem naslovu http ju ni.
- **Kocka pogleda je lahko v katerem koli kotu**
  Nastavitve > Videz > Položaj kocke pogleda postavi kocko pogleda zgoraj levo, zgoraj desno, spodaj levo ali spodaj desno na delovni ravnini. V vseh kotih razen zgoraj levo se upravljanje kamere premakne navzgor na njeno mesto.
- **Posnete datoteke SVG ostanejo dovolj majhne za združevanje**
  Uvožen SVG z zelo veliko krivuljami, na primer posneta risba, je bil razrezan na več kot 350.000 trikotnikov, zato več kopij ni bilo mogoče več združiti. Zdaj jih je približno desetkrat manj, risba pa je v tej velikosti videti enako. Ročno narisane skice ostanejo enako fine kot prej.
- **Tanke, majhne črke se ne izgubijo več pri izvozu**
  Majhno, ploščato besedilo je pri izvozu STL, 3MF ali OBJ izgubilo posamezne črke, tanko besedilo večino: pičko na i-ju, opuščaj, male črke. Izvoz jih je imel za ostružke, ki jih je pustil rez. To zdaj velja samo za telesa, ki jih je spremenil rez, združitev več teles ali ločitev barv.
- **SVG z dotikajočimi se oblikami pride celoten**
  V narisani ali posneti sliki se dve obliki pogosto stikata v samo eni točki. Uvoz je takrat nekatere oblike izpustil, slika pa je prišla manjša in z luknjami. Take oblike se zdaj sestavijo skupaj in slika ostane cela.
- **Posnetek + zaobljenje na valju je spet pri nogi**
  Posnetek + zaobljenje na spodnjem robu valja je prišel napačno obrnjen: zaobljenje pri nogi, posnetek nad njim, zaobljen pa je bil tudi zgornji rob. Zdaj posnetek leži na plošči in se prelije v steno, zgornji rob ostane nedotaknjen.
- **Vnos japonščine in kitajščine v polje Besedilo**
  Z vnosno metodo (IME) v polje Besedilo pri besedilu ali imenski tablici ni bilo mogoče vtipkati ničesar smiselnega: »ishi« je postalo »いｓｈい« in ga ni bilo mogoče pretvoriti v kanji. Polje zdaj ohrani vtipkano in besedo prevzame, ko je končana. To velja za japonščino, kitajščino in korejščino.
- **Interaktivna pomoč razloži vsako nastavitev posebej**
  V nastavitvah delovnega prostora je klik z interaktivno pomočjo pogosto vodil le do dolgega razdelka ali nikamor. Zdaj ima navodilo za vsako skupino nastavitev svoj razdelek (angleško in nemško), poleg tega še »Izbira barv« za barvno kartico, polja, drsniki, barvni gumbi in seznami pa najdejo svoj razdelek, tudi stikalo Hitri način.
- **Pogled zgodovine pove, kaj puščici razveljavita in uveljavita**
  Pod drsnikom pogleda zgodovine vrstica zdaj poimenuje korak, ki bi ga razveljavila leva puščica (»Zadnji korak: Premaknjeno Škatla«), in tistega, ki bi ga uveljavila desna (»Naslednji korak: 2 predmeta združena v skupino«), pri najstarejšem in najnovejšem stanju pa »ni«. Deluje tudi za osnutke, shranjene prej.
